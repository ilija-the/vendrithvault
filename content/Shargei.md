Shargei, also known as Red Landing, is a large settlement on the northern coast of the [[Celene]] of [[Canath]], which dominates the western edge of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]]. 

Shargei enjoys a bit of a rivalry with [[Twinvale]], which rules the eastern edge of the Painted Province.
