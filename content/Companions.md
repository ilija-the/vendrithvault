Companions is a term used to refer to creatures that follow and assist people, usually [[Adventurers|adventurers]]. These companions often have magical abilities or are especially trained or in tune with their instincts. [[Familiars|Familiars]] and [[Eidolon|Eidolons]] are aparticular types of companions, whereas the most common type is known as Animal Companions.
