The Old Crone was a [[Kholo]] [[Hags|Hag]] that likely was related to [[Ragnil Veldaks]] and her secret daughter, [[Mistress Crimtone]]. She drew her powers from the blood and energy of these two from afar, and fought to ensure they would remain alive and on the path to power. She cared little for their comforts, however, and eventually imprisoned Crimtone for her own 'safekeeping.' Her imprisonment was done by the [[The Fat Tiger|Fat Tiger]] for money.
