Scalendia is the island directly north of [[Isle of Flod|Flod]] and is regarded as the home of Vilhi [[Iruxi|Lizardfolk]]. Being on the precipice of the [[World Fleece]], right behind the [[Wan Islets]], the isle has outsized control over who gets to see the World Fleece and travel past it.
