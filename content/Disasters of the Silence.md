#events 

A name given to a variety of [[Natural Disasters]] that have happened since the start of the [[The Great Silence]]. The [[Magus in the High Tower]] has famously attributed the frequency and scale of these directly to the exiling of the [[Titans]] - therefore blaming them on the [[Sanitorium]]. [[Crusaders]] often see them as divine retribution.

One such disaster is the [[Sinking of Sargathia]].
