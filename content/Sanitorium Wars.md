After the [[Sanitorium]] was proclaimed and the [[Western Kobold Dynasty|Kobold Emperor]] abdicated, a number of provinces, tribes and governors refused to serve or recognise the Sanitorium's new rule over [[Mogxa]]. As a result, the Sanitorium led a number of wars and conflicts in order to reunify what the empire previously held.
