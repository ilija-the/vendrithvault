The Lord is a mysterious [[Underworld]] sentient and talking [[Slime|Slime]], likely a [[Slimes#Gelatinous Cube|Gelatinous Cube]]. He is visibly full of skeletons on his inside, arranged to look like a smiling face, and has prehensile arms and a tail made of bones and goop. He is a mob boss of an unknown amount of power, working from the shadows towards unknown goals.
