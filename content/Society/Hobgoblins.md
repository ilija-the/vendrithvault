Hobgoblins are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] that were once part of [[Hightower Civilisation]], whom they acted out a (slave) rebellion against. Once the same race as the [[Vanara]], the two have separated as a result of the Vanara staying with their masters, with Hobgoblins leaving to forge their own path.
