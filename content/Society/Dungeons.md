Dungeon is a term that widely refers to a variety of [[Creatures|creature]]-made or naturally occurring complex of rooms and corridors - often underground, or sprawling across or under large buildings like castles, temples or similar. The term is usually used for this latter kind only if they've been abandoned for their original purpose, such as in ruins, as "a dungeon" is usually somewhere occupied by dangerous [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]], [[Banditry|bandit]] or [[Barbarian|barbarian]]-like [[Ancestries|people]], as well as various [[Haunts]] and [[Traps]] set up or left behind there. It's a prime destination for [[Adventurers]] to try to clear out or find loot within.
