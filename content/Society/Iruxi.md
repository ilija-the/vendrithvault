The Iruxi, also known as Lizardfolk, are a scaled [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] mostly known to inhabit the marshy areas of [[Veducia]] not yet 'civilised.' While they are looked upon as primitive and isolated to such wilderness regions, they can in reality be found in many parts of the world.
