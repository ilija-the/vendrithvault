The Aiuvarin, also known as Half-Elves, are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] descendent [[Elves|elves]] but lacking their constructed nature. Aiuvarin result when elves procreate with [[Humans|humans]] or other similar ancestries, and unlike the elves, the aiuvarin consist of regular flesh and blood. Aiuvarin can also pass on their form to their own children for a generation or two, or longer if they only pair with other aiuvarin, though this is rare due to their relatively sparse numbers and lacking much of their own society. They still share much of the bodily size and form of elves - they are known to be skinnier and vary more in height than humans, have pointy ears, and usually have black sclera (those with [[Darkvision]]).
