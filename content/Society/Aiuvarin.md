The Aiuvarin, also known as Half-Elves, are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] descendent [[Elves|elves]] but lacking their constructed nature. Aiuvarin result when elves procreate with humans or other similar ancestries, and unlike the elves, the aiuvarin consist of regular flesh and blood. They still share much of the bodily size and form of elves - they are known to be skinnier and vary more in height than humans, have pointy ears, and usually have black sclera (especially those with darkvision).
