In the organisation of the [[Titans]] of [[Canath]] and [[Mogxa]], it was long standard to treat the twenty-one most important Titans as especially important - it is these who are thought to have wielded official power over [[Haeven]]. The twenty-one for example always get icons of worship at any non-tiny [[Temples|Churches]]. Other Titans have existed, such as those with [[Demideities|lesser power]], those who only recently gained a [[Mantle]] but have no proper following or prestige, or those situated in other [[Society/Religion|Religions]]. Only a few Titans throughout history have remained outside of the Twenty-One and yet wielded enormous power - the greatest example being [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]] - against which the Twenty-One served as more of a balancing coalition. 

Within the Twenty-One, the Titans have at times organised into [[Trinities]], which arrange themselves around a number of binaries. In these the enlightened third position is the synthesis or wise compromise between the two, usually represented by the oldest and wisest Titan. It is unknown whether this is merely a division of labour, a way to organise who gets to determine canon over which topics, or whether it has a real [[Divine Magic]] or [[Cosmology|Cosmological]] purpose.

Part of the inciting incident of the early [[War Over Haeven]] was the [[Triumphant Successor]]'s pursuit of a role in the Twenty-One, alongside his brother the [[Grand Scheme]].

It is speculated that the Twenty-One used to be Twenty-Nine in the past, but that it was changed after a troublesome set of Titans were removed and erased from history. These [[Forgotten Eight]] are a topic discussed in [[Occult Magic]] circles. The Twenty-Nine may have also been organised under a High Four representing the Arcane, Divine, Occult and Primal.
