Humans are a plentiful [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] of Vendrith known for having no specific home or language, but which has adapted to living across all manner of lands and societies still. While the exact origins of humans isn't known, it is commonly believed they are descendants of the [[Vanara]]-[[Hobgoblins|Hobgoblin]] precursor ancestry imbued with [[Divine]] aesthetics, perhaps by the [[First Ascended]] himself.
