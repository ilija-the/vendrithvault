Humans are a plentiful [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] of Vendrith known for having no specific home or language, but which has adapted to living across all manner of lands and societies still. As such, they are common across all of Vendrith. While the exact origins of humans isn't known, it is commonly believed they are descendants of the [[Vanara]]-[[Hobgoblins|Hobgoblin]] precursor ancestry imbued with [[Divine]] aesthetics, perhaps by the [[First Ascended]] while leading the [[Flock of Man]]. Humans are likely closely related to [[Orcs|Orcs]] as well, and have been compared to the look of the mysterious and constructed [[Elves]] (though humans lack the whole 'constructed' element).
