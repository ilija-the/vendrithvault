Religion is the tradition of [[Lore]] and interpretation of the [[Divine]], often relating to the subjects of existence itself, its purpose, and the origin of Vendrith (see [[Mythos of Vendrith]]). It has also played a central role in the organisation of [[Society]] since its earliest days. 

The latest and most prominent form of religion in [[Iduria|Iduria]] was that of [[Titans|Titan]] worship - though since the [[War Over Haeven]] this has become controversial and polarised.
