[[Mogxa]] under the [[Sanitorium]] relies on a number of "Secret Societies" carrying out recruitment, education, magic and covert action. The term secret may be misleading, as in reality most do not hide their own existence, though they are 'closed-off- societies The fact unsupportive adventurers ran many of the important societies of Mogxa contributed to the [[Adventurer Crisis]].
