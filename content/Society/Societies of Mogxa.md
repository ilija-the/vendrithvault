[[Mogxa]] under the [[Sanitorium]] relies on a number of "Secret Societies" carrying out recruitment, education, magic and covert action. The term secret may be misleading, as in reality most do not hide their own existence. They're something more like 'closed-off' societies, which might have public relations through the public institutions they run (food banks, fighting dojos, manufactories, libraries), and a well-known name, symbol and even motto and core values; though their inner workings, specific objectives and even leadership structure can be for internal eyes and ears only.

The fact unsupportive adventurers ran many of the important societies of Mogxa contributed to the [[Adventurer Crisis]]. Since then, the Sanitorium has worked to ensured there are spies in the most important guilds capable of warning the Sanitorium when a society turns rebellious.
