The Academie Peripherie Philosophia, Academie Peripherie, or "the APP," is a place of higher learning in the [[Philosophian Peripherie|Peripherie]] of [[Philosophia]]. It studies a manner of [[Magic]], mainly of the [[Wizard]] schools, and has a faculty of [[Alchemy]] and [[Crafting|Engineering]].

The Academy was where [[Magnus Strike]] graduated from, after having written a dissertation on the [[Leyline Problem]]. The [[Magus in the High Tower]] is known to have scouted for talent here, including finding and taking a liking to Magnus.
