Located on the far west side of the [[Isle of Flod]] hugging the long mountainside, the Nishi Sekai Clan is a well defended and lively territory. While its villages are said to look teeming with people, it is also said to be an eerie place that nobody wants to stay in besides its natives and the one remaining family member, [[Ansonī Fōdo]].

The Nishi Sekai Clan territories are known to host a couple of entrances to larger cave networks, and are surprisingly well fortified, with half a dozen scout towers using farlight stones, stationed miles ahead of the actual villages.
