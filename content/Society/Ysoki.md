The Ysoki, also known as Ratfolk, are a humanoid [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] reminiscent of rodents. They are often found in cities, where they have a reputation for belonging to the [[Underworld]], or in small rural shires, where they have a reputation for being pushovers.
