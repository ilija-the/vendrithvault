The Ysoki, also known as Ratfolk, are a humanoid [[Ancestries|people]] reminiscent of rodents. They are often found in cities, where they have a reputation for belonging to the [[Underworld]], or in small rural shires, where they have a reputation for being pushovers. Ysoki make up one of the primarily populations of the [[Hommagong]] of western [[Mogxa]], together with [[Rabbitfolk]] and other [[The Rend|Rend]] peoples.
