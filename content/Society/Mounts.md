Mounts, [[Animals|animal]] which are suited to being ridden to move fast or reliably over long distances, have been used throughout all of [[History]] of [[Society|Civilisation]]. They're rather essential for land-based [[Mercantilism]] and [[Agriculture]], and have gained a special role for logistics and elite units in [[Society/Warfare|Warfare]]. The most common example is Horses, though mounts exist in a variety of exotic flavours often suited to the specific climate and terrain. Mounts have to be larger than their rider, which makes smaller [[Ancestries|ancestries]] capable of riding a number of mounts not possible for others. 

Mundane mounts include:
- Horses (and war horses)
- Mules (and Augdunar)
- Goats
- Camels
- Llamas
- Corgis 
- Elephants
- Giraffes
- Pangolin
- Rhinoceros 
- Triceratops
- Tyrannosaurus

More exotic land mounts include:
- Axebeaks
- Terror Birds
- Dire Wolves
- Draft Lizards
- Monitor Lizards
- Giant Beetles
- Giant Frogs
- Riding Drakes

Some few mounts are capable of flight, though these require both the mount and the rider to be especially trained - and as such they're usually exclusive to praetorian guards or similar. Possible flying mounts include:
- Griphon
- Hippogriff

Others are aquatic in nature:
- Sharks

And some mounts are really the creation of [[Magic]], [[Alchemy]] or [[Inventor|Invention]]:
- [[Inventor|Construct Companions]]
- Legchair
- Rootball Chair
- Oozeform Chair
