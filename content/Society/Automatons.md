Automatons are a variety of constructed [[Ancestries|people]] following different designs that share many characteristics - all in some form or other trying to imitate the perfection of the [[Elves]]. A lot more crude than their ancient inspirations, they are most commonly found in [[Vilhun]] where many of the [[Steel Lord]] automatons are still well-maintained.

The [[Wereforged]] are another variant of Automaton invented recently in the city of [[Philosophia]] - it is uncertain to what extent their designs were influenced by their Vilhi counterpart. They were used as the [[Slavery|property]] of their creators, though some got pawned off on the [[Underworld|black market]]. The Wereforged project was shut down however, after it was revealed they used the souls of former convicts, and various other people simply caught in the production process, to serve as the soul required to power their cores. By decree of the Pactlaw Senate, the remaining Automatons were declared legally free persons and capable of attaining guild memberships.

The existence and treatment of Automatons is often a topic of debate, with a notable group opposing their continued "use" being [[Friends of Humanity]].
