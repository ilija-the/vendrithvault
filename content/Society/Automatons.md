Automatons are in reality a variety of constructed [[Ancestries|people]], all in some form or other trying to imitate the perfection of the [[Elves]]. A lot more crude than their ancient inspirations, they are most commonly found in [[Vilhun]] by nature of being invented there by the [[Steel Lord]].

Their existence and treatment is often a topic of debate, with a notable group opposing their continued "use" being [[Friends of Humanity]].
