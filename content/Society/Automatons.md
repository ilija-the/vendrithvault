Automatons are in reality a variety of constructed [[Ancestries|people]], all in some form or other trying to imitate the perfection of the [[Elves]]. A lot more crude than their ancient inspirations, they are most commonly found in [[Vilhun]] - where the most commonly still-around type of Automaton was invented by the [[Steel Lord]].

The [[Wereforged]] are another variant of Automaton invented recently in the city of [[Philosophia]] - it is uncertain to what extent their designs were influenced by their Vilhi counterpart. They were used as the [[Slavery|property]] of their creators, anThe Wereforged project was shut down however, after it was revealed they used the souls of former convicts, and various other people simply caught in the production process, to serve as the soul required to power their cores.

The existence and treatment of Automatons is often a topic of debate, with a notable group opposing their continued "use" being [[Friends of Humanity]].
