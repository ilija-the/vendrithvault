The "Rabbitfolk" are a rabbit-like [[Ancestries|people]] mostly inhabiting the [[Tributary Coast]] of [[Mogxa]], often not far from [[Ysoki]] and even known to make contact with [[Pnoll|Pnolls]]. Their societies aren't large, but they produce a relatively high number of off-spring that leave to see the world. They are known to have exceptionally good legs.
