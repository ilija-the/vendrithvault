# Overview
"Fleshwarps" refers to [[Ancestries|people]] whose bodies have been totally mutated or even grown through the use of vile [[Alchemy]], [[Magic]], [[Medicine]] and other similar arts. This is a rare occurrence, but the times it has happened have become quite infamous, creating precautionary stories lasting generations.
