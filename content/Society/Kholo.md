The Kholo are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] of Vendrith that has been described as humanoid hyenas, and as such has long been viewed as primitive and animalistic by other ancestries. While they used to be a rarity that occasionally was met to the south, bordering deeper [[Eydwin]], they have since migrated into [[Iduria]] in a number of waves - mostly ending up in the region of [[Galae]]. A smaller number also made their way forward to [[Horiza]] during the [[Horiza#Third Wave and Politicisation|third wave of settlement]].

Kholo culture lacks much of the taboos and fears of death, and always try to use all of the animal, eating all the way down to the bone and then using the bones, teeth and hides alike for [[Crafting]]. They also often engage in scrounging and scavenging across the ruins of Galae.
