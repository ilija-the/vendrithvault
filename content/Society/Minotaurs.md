Minotaur are an uncommon humanoid bull-like people, large and powerful in stature, that skirts the line between being considered [[Ancestries|people]] and [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]] depending on their context. While they are essentially always told of as man-hunters and villains in older stories, it is clear more and more have integrated into 'civilisation' over the course of history. Their roots decidedly lie in the region of [[Canath]], in which they were known to guard labyrinths from intruders.

Minotaurs possess a great sense of navigation, and together with their athletic builds, their civilised king often serve as guides or travelling companions. Their talent is greatly useful or even necessary towards travelling across the [[Cosmology|demi-plane]] of [[Labyrinthia]].
