Trinities are core to most society stemming from the flock of man, and most all holy numbers of Vendrith are multiples of 3. 9 has been more of a Canathi and titan-divine number, possibly coming from the original pantheon of 9. The Sanitorium has quietly ensured the number 9 comes up as little as possible, most notably in their changing of the calendar. 21 has also been a holy number since the existence of the [[The Twenty-One|Twenty-One]]. It is believed the Twenty-Nine preceded them, but that they were reduced down to Twenty-One after the removal of the [[Forgotten Eight]]. The Twenty-Nine may have also been lead by the High Four, representing the [[3+1 Theorem|3+1]] forms of magic - [[Primal Magic]], [[Arcane Magic|Arcane]], [[Divine Magic]] and [[Occult Magic]].
