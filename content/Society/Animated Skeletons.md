The Skeletons of dead bodies are frequently animated into [[Undead|Undeath]] by lingering energies, or intentionally by [[Necromancy|Necromancers]]. The vast majority of these, like most undead, lack any sort of intelligence beyond highly basic and violent instincts and the potential commands of their masters - though the skeletons of soldiers usually remember how to use their weapons, and some may even recall the ability to use spells. A very few animated skeletons retain their intellect and memories for unknown reasons, and stay as [[Ancestries|people]].
