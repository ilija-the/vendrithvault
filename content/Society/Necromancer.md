Necromancer refers to a broad category of [[Magic|mages]] that practice necromancy, which most famously makes and maintains the [[Undead]], though it also interacts with life energy and souls. Necromancy is for example technically used in most [[Resurrection]] magic, or that which speaks to the dead in the [[River of Death]]. The forms that use the undead are under social taboo and often against the [[Law]], whereas the forms that bring back the temporarily dead, especially when done by a [[Divine]] [[Cleric]], are much more accepted. The latter often makes use of diamonds, which has lead to the [[Diamond Crisis]] of [[Mogxa]].

Necromancer also refers to a more specific [[Classes|class]] that uses [[Occult]] magic and necromancy, and which makes use of a number of summoned and re-animated undead minions on the battlefield.

[[Liches]] are a type of [[Monsters of Vendrith|monstrous]] necromancer whom have changed their own bodies to support immortality.
