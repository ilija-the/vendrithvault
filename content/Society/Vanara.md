Vanara are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] with a monkey-like appearance, which usually lives in closed-off communes in isolated places practicing the arts and monasticism. They share a common ancestor with the [[Hobgoblins]], which served as [[Slavery|servants]] of the [[Hightower Civilisation]] - the Vanara today consider themselves the inheritors of the best of [[Elves|Elven]] culture. They maintain a rivalry with hobgoblins to this day.
