War and warfare has existed throughout all of the [[History]] of [[Society|Civilisation]]. It's strategy, tactics, logistics and weapons of war vary from place to place, and have changed drastically throughout time - though a few common wisdoms from ancient times still apply today. Wars can be formally done between sovereign states over disagreements, power or land. It can also be done on a smaller skirmish scale, without declaration, in ways more reminiscent of [[Banditry]] or [[Barbarian|Barbarianism]]. When a state or society collapses under the weight of its own disagreements or power struggles, a Civil War may ensue.

Many different fields and concepts have developed out of warfare - such as the organisation of levies, taxation to fund wars, blacksmithing, [[Mounts]], leatherworking and fletching to make weapons and armour, as well as various martial arts and traditions for using various weapons, tactics for on-the-ground battles, and strategy for planning wars and power plays as a whole. Many fields of magic also owe much to warfare.

Some wars that have happened include:
- [[War Over Haeven]].
- [[Sanitine Wars]].
- [[March of the Corpse Flowers]].
- Civil conflict in [[Rabarb|Rabarb]].
- [[Rosbit Period]] civil war over the throne.
