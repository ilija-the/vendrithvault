The Amurrun, also known as Catfolk, are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] that appear like humanoid felines and are typically quite slim and tall. Most amurrun are of bright and golden furs, there are also a few of darker shades. The amurrun are famous for being the builder class of the [[Hightower Civilisation]] under the [[Elves]], and continue to be known as visionaries when it comes to [[Crafting|invention]]. This is aided by due to their being born with [[Amurrun Markings]], blueprints of various things the respective amurrun often spend their parts of their lives trying to pursue and create.
