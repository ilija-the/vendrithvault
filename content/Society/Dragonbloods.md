Dragonbloods are humanoids with [[Dragons|Dragon]] blood coursing through them, often descendant from actual dragons that decided to become humanoid in response to the [[Draconic Concordat]]. As varied as the dragons themselves, they are held in high renown as well as infamy across Vendrith.

Mogxa has traditionally held the absolute largest amount of Dragonbloods, and still do in the form of the [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa|Draconic Tribes]], though the long-term suspicion and suppression of their kind is taking its toll. With [[Vilhun]] currently acting as somewhat of a safe haven for dragons, still revered and considered sacred, the population of dragonblood there has boomed recently. A handful also went to [[Horiza]].
