Dragonblood is a general term for offspring of [[Dragons]] that are not dragons in and out themselves. As varied as the dragons themselves, they are held in high renown as well as infamy across Vendrith.

Due to the [[Draconic Concordat]], dragonblood are currently the only possible kind of children dragons may have. With [[Vilhun]] currently acting as somewhat of a safe haven for dragons, still revered and considered sacred, the population of dragonblood there boomed.

For dragonblood characters, see #ancestries/dragonblood.
