Temples, also known as Churches or Cathedrals, refers to both congregations of people with a similar [[Religion]] as well as the physical buildings they gather at. Temples are led by Holy People such as priests or [[Cleric|Clerics]]. [[Champion|Champions]] are also often found at Temples acting as their protectors or swords to be sent out on missions.

Temples are either open to the public, to their members, or are highly exclusive to a specific ring or cult. Some are intentionally hard to reach or hidden, acting as Monasteries or hidden temples. Some guard secrets or artefacts, and as such are defended by various warriors, [[Creatures]] and traps of both mundane and [[Magic|Magical]] nature.

[[Funeral Rites|Funerals]] and [[Gender, Relationship and Marriage Customs|Marriages]] are often performed at temples. Many temples have either graveyards next to them or catacombs under them, often to protect the dead from grave robbing or [[Necromancy]].

[[Shrines]] of a religious nature are often related to temples.

Known physical temples include:
- That of the [[Icen Maiden|Icen Maiden's]] [[Universal Temple]] in [[Philosophia]].
- Possibly a lost Icen Maiden temple in [[Nagonkor]], [[Vön]] or further west.
- The [[Longdawn|Archduchy of Longdawn]] is like an enormous Cathedral of a city with settlement formed around it.
- A tiny mobile church in [[Vanderkact]], dedicated to [[Saint Clover]].
- A church in [[Twinberry]], which has been dedicated to both [[Saint Rowan]] and [[Saint Jonathan]].
- A small church once known as [[Haven]] in the demiplane of [[Labyrinthia]], ran by a [[Sister Amelie Kenton|sisterhood]] dedicated to the [[Golden Heart]].
