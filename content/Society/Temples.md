Temples, also known as Churches, refers to both congregations of people with a similar [[Religion]] as well as the physical buildings they gather at. Temples are led by Holy People such as priests or [[Cleric|Clerics]]. [[Champion|Champions]] are also often found at Temples acting as their protectors or swords to be sent out on missions.

[[Shrines]] are often related to them. [[Funeral Rites|Funerals]] and [[Gender, Relationship and Marriage Customs|Marriages]] are often performed at temples.

Known physical temples include:
- That of the [[Icen Maiden|Icen Maiden's]] [[Universal Temple]] in [[Philosophia]].
- Possibly a lost Icen Maiden temple in [[Nagonkor]], [[Vön]] or further west.
- A small church once known as [[Haven]] in the demipla
