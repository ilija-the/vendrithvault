Shoony are an anthropomorphic dog-like [[Ancestries|people]] with [[Undead]]-like features that are often viewed with great fear and mistrust. These features can be partial exoskeletons such as skulls, bones glowing through their flesh, ectoplasmic body parts, unusual eyes, eerily black or white fur, and quite commonly, luminescent fur markings. The shoony are said to simply walk out of the [[World Fleece|fogs]] of [[Ferweth]], and superstition states that they must be appeased by being granted hospitality in order to avoid disaster.
