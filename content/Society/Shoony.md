Shoony are a dog-like humanoid [[Ancestries|people]] with [[Undead]]-like features that are often viewed with great fear and mistrust. These features can be partial exoskeletons such as skulls, bones glowing through their flesh, ectoplasmic body parts, unusual eyes, eerily black or white fur, and quite commonly, luminescent fur markings. The shoony are said to simply walk out of the [[World Fleece|fogs]] of [[Ferweth]], the region in which almost all Shoony are found, and superstition states that they must be granted hospitality in order to avoid incurring a [[Curses|curse]].
