Poppets are a small constructed [[Ancestries|people]] made in similar traditions to those creating [[Familiars|familiars]], but also inspired by the attempt to recreate [[Elves]] (like their larger siblings, [[Automatons]]). They are thought to lack all of the elegance and otherworldliness of elves however. Many poppets act as familiars to their creators, quite similar to [[Leshys|leshys]].
