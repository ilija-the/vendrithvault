Gladiators are historically typically a type of [[Slavery|Slave]] made to [[Performance|perform]] fights against other [[Ancestries|people]], [[Animals|animals]] or [[Creatures|creatures]] as entertainment. They are often motivated by the ability to free themselves, but also often pursue money and glory.

[[Dragonbloods]] are often kept in [[Slavery]] and sold on the [[Underworld|Black Market]] by [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] [[Kobolds]]. This is a practice that has come about through the Kobolds' need to keep a powerful draconic creature in their hatcheries, in order to ensure their offspring maintain the perfect draconic appearance.
