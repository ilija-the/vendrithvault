## Goblinoid Taxonomy

![[Pasted image 20260412193549.png|right hsmall]]

Goblins and Hobgoblins can range from 'traditional' looks to those influenced by their animal heritage / respective environment. The chart on the right highlights each biome and animal-kind the goblinoids can take. The very center is the 'traditional' goblin, a stem, while going outwards has them increasing their animal characteristics. The middle ring is the 'traditional' hobgoblin, being more animalistic by default. 

While the hobgoblins have the same environment taxonomies, they do not have the same animals attached to them.

### Goblins
- Marsh Goblin (Tadpole/Fish)
- Highland Goblin (Goat)
- Canopy Goblin (Monkey)
- Boscage Goblin (Boar)
- Island Goblin (Chicken)
- Field Goblin (Wolf)
### Hobgoblins
- Marsh Hobgoblin (???)
- Highland Hobgoblin (Llama)
- Canopy Hobgoblin (Panda? Tiger?)
- Boscage Hobgoblin (Hedgehog? Panda?)
- Island Hobgoblin (Shark? Also became the shark orc heritage, and are another predator of the poor chicken gobs)
- Field Hobgoblin (???)
