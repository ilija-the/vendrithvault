Also known as Dynastic Cycle, Ascended. Wheel Societies were given close scrutiny under early [[Sanitorium]] rule, with much suspicion about their [[Divine Magic|divinity]]-like ideas. The Wheel Societies of today have reformed enough to have become rehabilitated, clarifying their separation from Titanic religion.

Once represented by three claws in a triangular cycle. Later a halo was added to (to represent the First Ascended) above the triangle and the bottom claw dropped.
