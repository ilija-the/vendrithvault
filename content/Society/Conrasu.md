Conrasu are a strange and rare type of [[Creatures|creature]] with a penchant for mathematics, astrologie, and order, sometimes considered a [[Ancestries|person]]. Their few appearances are shrouded in intrigue, often serving as [[Oracle|Oracles]] hidden away deep in coves or temples to provide secret insights or perhaps perform calculations. Some point out their way of thinking has been compared to a more sophisticated [[Automatons|automaton]].

Some stories say that Conrasu can serve as the prototype for a [[Reflection]], essentially a perfect copy made of another person.
