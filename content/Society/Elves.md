Elves are a mystical and constructed people who were at the centre of the advanced [[Hightower Civilisation]]. They are known to have an otherworldly beauty to them, but also for creeping many other ancestries out with their doll-like features. Their emotions, behaviour and once society are known to be aloof and somewhat hard to understand in its complexity.

![[Elf_example_02.jpg]]

Since the downfall of the Hightowers, elves have only become rarer in numbers. The formula for creating new elves appears to be lost - despite countless attempts to recreate it.

Not having a known definite lifespan (though dying if their bodies receive enough wear and tear), the elves that still exist likely do so from the hightower days or soon after them, but all exhibit elements of amnesia. It is thought that this amnesia either affects their earliest memories, or is specifically targeting the days of the Hightower Civilisation. The latter interpretation is often incorporated into conspiracies around the downfall of Elves and the Hightower Civilisations. 

The attempt to recreate elves can be said to have led to the creation of [[Automatons]] and [[Poppets]]. A much more common remnant of the elves is the [[Aiuvarin]] (also known as half-elves), which are the offspring resulting from procreation with an elf (or another aiuvarin). The [[Vanara]], while their own ancestry, claims to have inherited much of the best parts of elven culture.
