Athletics and Acrobatics are the cultivation, use and sometimes [[Performance]] of physical prowess - usually showing off different talents and traditions. Athletics is usually used to refer to pure strength, whereas acrobatics refers to precise acts that make use of as little force as necessary.

### Athletics


### Acrobatics
Closely related to [[Athleticism]], acrobatics is a form of physical ability or [[Performance]] that makes use of dexterity, speed, precision and flexibility. Whereas athletics can be considered using ones strength to perform feats, acrobatics is a way to use as little strength as possible in order to make the motions one wants.
