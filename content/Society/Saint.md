A semi-divine status given to mortals, often post-mortem. Saints are commonly loyal followers of a deity, such as a [[Titans|Titan]], or some other philosophy. The title and veneration required to become a saint comes with being an exemplar of the teachings of what they follow.

Recently, saints that aren't associated with titans have been allowed as 'secular' symbols of worship in the anti-theist [[Sanitorium]]. 

In [[Ferweth]] there is a tradition of worshipping Saints already when they're alive, instead of waiting for a 'confirmed' beatification. 

In [[Vilhun]], the most famous saints are the [[Hun Lords]], having achieved immortality via cultivation and been appointed as emissaries of [[One & Only|The One and Only]], becoming saints of that deity.
