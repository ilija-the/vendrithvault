Dromaar, also known as Half-Orcs, are a [[Ancestries|people]] that comes from the [[Orcs]] and are known for their green skin. A Droomar results when an Orc doesn't partake in the [[Lotus Orcus]] for an extended amount of time, or when their off-spring doesn't partake throughout early childhood. This sees the normally blue hue of Orcish skin become more like green instead. The Dromaar tend to lack much of the tranquillity and introspection of Orcs, though they maintain a sense of curiosity and discipline, which often makes them great warriors. Many Dromaar worship the [[Macabre Maelstrom]], a sort of twisted version of the Promised Revealer's teachings that embraces the inevitable entropy, decay and end of the world.

Dromaar have a reputation of being misfits, bandits and warlords - which are known for partaking in. A number of Dromaar roam the lands of [[Galae]].
