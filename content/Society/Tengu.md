Tengu are a mystical flightless bird-like [[Ancestries|people]] with long beaks known for their [[Performance]] and ties to folklore about [[Fate|Fortune]] - often serving on ships in order to 'eat' bad luck. They have roots likely spanning to the days of [[Mewji]], and are still found in [[Vilhun]].

> There also exists and has existed a number of long-nosed [[Poppets]] also called Tengu. It is uncertain which one came first or what connection they have, but the term is rarely used for them any more.
