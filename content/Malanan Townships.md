The Malanan Towns are a number of settlements in [[Mogxa|Eastern Mogxa]], down-river of [[Dreyus]], home to a people somewhat unique amongst the rest of the continent. It has long maintained more of a [[Feudalism|Tributary]] status under the [[Mogxan Empire|Mogxan Empires]] instead of direct rule, which it still has today under the [[Sanitorium]]. The towns sit on a number of rivers and marshes and are famous for their output of rice, being quite important to feeding Eastern Mogxa.
