The Prince is a [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] of [[Friedport]] with a love for theatre. Claiming to be a bastard child of [[Valfrid Veldaks]], he has acted much like a Landcrab leader (at least in [[Waterlogg Street]]) and advocated for the rights of his people. He was raised at the [[Theatre Orphanage]] with his childhood friends [[Su Na]], [[Etcetera]], and later, [[Saint Karvi Massiri|Karvi Massiri]].
