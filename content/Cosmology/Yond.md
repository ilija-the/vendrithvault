A great and dark planet that looms above Vendrith, often only visible because of how it blots out otherwise visible stars.

The much smaller [[Moons of Vendrith]] interact with it strangely, seeming to avoid overlapping with it. There is still reason to believe the moons are closer than Yond itself, so both the Moons and Yond should be visible if the moons were to enter between Vendrith and Yond.

Often connected to the (non-titanic) deity called the [[Daydreaming Truth]]
