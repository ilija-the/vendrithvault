#deities 
A [[Deity]] said to be the either the first of the [[Titans]] or something above it as its creator. One of the core stories of the [[Mythos of Vendrith]]. 

**Pantheon** Vendrith Mythos

**Trinity** Creation of Vendrith

---

Winged prime of humanity, guardian of the keys of creation, and architect of the role of godhood itself. A long faded deity, the first ascended creates common ground between those who worship him as a deity, or merely as a great example to all other humanoids.

---

**Areas of Concern** Aspiration, adaptivity, breaking boundaries, leadership.

**Edicts** Pursue your proper station, adapt and change throughout your life.

**Anathema** Lose your humanity in pursuit of power, disrespect a prostitute.
