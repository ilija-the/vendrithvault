---

The Immortal Memory is the weird deity of the family, but also one that is quite uncontroversial. They are a caretaker of the dead generally, and specifically their memories, which contain millenia of experience and knowledge. The Restored Memory is also known for planting the firearm the Hooded Saviour stole, archiving it in the same way it does with all knowledge.

---

**Areas of concern** Last words, burial sites, crypts, history.

**Edicts** Seek out, archive and safekeep the experiences of living beings with intelligent thoughts.

**Anathema** Destroy unique information, fail to document a dying person's words.
