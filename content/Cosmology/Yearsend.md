#cosmos
A season of the [[Calendar of Vendrith]] in which the night sky of Vendrith moves in an unusual manner, having many effects on the planet itself. Viewed as the start of a year, used to count one's age ("Seventy yearsends' old). 

A central part of it is the [[Sun of Vendrith]] temporarily disappearing behind [[Yond]].
