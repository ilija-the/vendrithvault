Also known as Helish Crusade. Helmarches are attempted incursions by armies or [[Adventurers|adventurers]] from [[Materia]] to attack, purge, destroy or occupy a plane of [[Hel]]. Many times this has involved taking a side in the [[Bloodwar]] by allying with [[Devil|Devils]] against [[Demon|Demons]] or vice versa.

Currently, the plane of [[Crimson]] is a plane of Hel. Some Crusaders have suggested doing a Helmarch to help destroy the [[Demonic Brigade]] once and for all.
