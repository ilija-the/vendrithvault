A great mysterious force of nature of Vendrith. Often considered the edges of the plane of [[Materia]]. Colloquially just called the fog.

Manifests more in some places than others. Regions known for struggling with the fog are [[Ferweth]] and [[Vilhun]], though the fog also once prevented the [[Discovery of Horiza]]. Reaching the [[New World - Horiza]] is still an arduous journey because of the difficulties of safely navigating the fog passage.
