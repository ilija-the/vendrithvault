#society 
One of the [[Ancestries of Vendrith]].

Once the elite of the [[Hightower Civilisation]] of [[Galae]].

A constructed people appearing like large but sophisticated and beautiful dolls or figurines.

Attempts to recreate elves have usually resulted in the creation of [[Automatons]].

Some smaller recreations of elves, or even possibly smaller original elves themselves, are called [[Poppets]].
