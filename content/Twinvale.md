Twinvale is a long valley on the border between the [[Canath|Painted Canath]] north of the [[Celene]] and [[Ferweth]]. It is home to [[Twinvale City]]. The smaller settlement of [[Twinberry]] sits atop a deep part of the vale, where the distance between the sides is small enough a rope bridge usually extends across it. While they can be seen from each other on days with a clear climate, there is little contact or interaction between the two settlements, or the two regions, due to the stark height difference and difficulties involved with climbing it.

Residents of Twinvale have on occasions sent complaints by courier to Twinberry regarding bodies and trash being dropped down the vale, but it is unknown if these have ever reached Twinberry. It is a common myth in Twinvale City that the backwards folk of Twinberry execute criminals by throwing them off the bridge.
