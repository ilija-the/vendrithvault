![[Amelie_Lastsummer.png]]
A sister of the [[Golden Heart]] that once resided in [[Cragmoore|Cragmoore]]. She was close to [[Antoine Dumas]] and [[Borgrumm Holdok]]. A humanitarian with expertise in covert action, she is believed to have helped many flee from Cragmoore during its crackdown on rebels. After supposedly leading these refugees to [[Riverside]] to start their new lives, she found a [[Labyrinthia|Labyrinthian Gateway]] to [[Friedport]] - that she dubbed [[Haven]]. Setting up a sisterhood and secretly creating a network in both cities, she allowed certain people to travel between the two worlds whilst trying to keep the gateway secret from the [[Crusaders]] (whom she believed would use it for warfare). Likely confirming this theory is that the Crusader known as [[Awaited Architect|The Architect]] planned her assassination. It was also in Friedport that she met [[Saint Karvi Massiri|The Scurvymaster]], whom she worked with for a period, and later where she was reunited with [[Ashur Holdok]].
