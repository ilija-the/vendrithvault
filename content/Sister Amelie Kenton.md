A sister of the [[Golden Heart]] that once resided in [[Cragmoore|Cragmoore]]. She was close to the disgrA humanitarian with expertise in covert action, she is believed to have helped many flee from Cragmoore during its crackdown on rebels. After supposedly leading these refugees to [[Riverside]] to start their new lives, she found a [[Labyrinthia|Labyrinthian Gateway]] to [[Friedport]] - that she dubbed [[Haven]]. Setting up a sisterhood and secretly creating a network in both cities, she allowed certain people to travel between the two worlds whilst trying to keep the gateway secret from the [[Crusaders]] (whom she believed would use it for warfare). Likely confirming this theory is that the Crusader known as [[Awaited Architect|The Architect]] planned her assassination.
