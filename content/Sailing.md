Sailing and other methods like rowing (or rarely propulsion) is a way of traversing the waters of the world, primarily the seas but also sometimes lakes and even rivers. This is done on a variety of types of ships, some for more [[Mercantilism|Mercantile]] purposes, some for [[Warfare]] or [[Banditry|Piracy]].

Many cultures and realms have gained a reputation for being especially good at naval matters. The [[Zardoban Crown|Zardoban Empire]] is one such example, as is [[The Vilhi Sultanate]] which continues to need sailing as an entirely island-based realm. The [[Rubyfirth Bay|Rubyfirthers]] are also talented sea-going merchants.

Sailing is required to cross from [[Iduria]] to [[Horiza]], which makes up perhaps the most challenging journey a captain or crew can undertake due to the [[World Fleece]].
