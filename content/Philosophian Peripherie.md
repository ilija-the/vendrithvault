The Philosophian Peripherie is the outgrowth of [[Philosophia]] that has expended beyond the [[Pactwalls]], mostly concentrated around its northern end. Due to their presence outside of the walls, the [[Pactlaw]] does not apply equally to these districts - but it has also politically been allowed to stay mostly under the autonomous leadership of Philosophia itself. It acts as a bit of a meeting point for the city state's people and the [[Mogxan Empire|Mogxan Empires]] they swear de jure allegiance to.
