#society 
A term used to described the last teachings of the [[Titans]] before being exiled from materia. In reality the term encompasses a combination of both literal speech and writing, but also many mysterious phenomenon that have to be interpreted. The latter is often found in types of celestials or artefacts discovered in hidden places.

The mythos of the [[Continental Army]] as well as the story about the [[God-King's Fleet]] are parts of the New Creed.
