The Sacred Path, also known as the Bronze Canal, is a marvel of [[Crafting#Engineering|Engineering]] that creates a narrow waterway between eastern [[Canath]] that reaches into the Galaen Wilds of [[Galae]]. It is narrow, and demands the use of various cranes and water locks, but it bypasses the need to ascend the many steps of the [[Path of Gold]].

On the path are a handful of Monasteries which maintain it, and who make up a place to stop for shelter and rest. This practical arrangement also became a rather religious one, as performing a pilgrimage along the path and contributing to its maintenance became a sign of religious duty.
