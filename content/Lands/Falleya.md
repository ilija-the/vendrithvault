The city of [[Falleya]] once sat on a passage point on the [[Young Bluff|Old Bluff]], which lost over half of its population and buildings to the disaster - seeing much of it fall to its death and destruction below. The ruins of Falleya provide a grim sight to this day. Many more of Falleya's residents have since fled for fear of further events like it, some of them settling the settlement of [[Bluffshire]]. Falleya is today reduced to a mere small town.
