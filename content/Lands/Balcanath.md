Balcanath, essentially meaning "end of Canath," is hilly and mostly wild countryside once home to a number of mining settlements before they ran dry. While it stays mostly unimportant, it must be passed through to travel between [[Babael]] of [[Galae]] and [[Canath]]. The city of [[Kallea]] attempts to maintain some control over at least the northern parts of the area, guarding the road towards Babael. A single military fortification sits here in case invasion could come from the mountains - though it has clearly not been considered much of a threat.

A number of [[Banditry|bandits]] and [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]] roam Balcanath, often hiding out in abandoned mines.
