The Electrum Mountains are a mountain chain that occupies the central-western side of [[Mogxa]], lying between the city of [[Zardoba]] and [[Philosophia]]. It separates the lands of what's now known as "Imperial Mogxa" and the [[Hommagong]] peninsula from the northern and Galaen parts of Mogxa. It contains large deposits of silver and gold, which was once used to build Zardoba into a "city of gold."
