Shirelock is an inland settlement in [[Hommagong]] of [[Mogxa]], not far down the road from [[Wedu]]. It serves as a [[Feudalism|tributary]] of the [[Sanitorium]] lead [[Mogxan Empire]]. 

The settlement is famous for a [[Performance|story]] of a [[Investigator|detective]] [[Rabbitfolk|rabbit]].
