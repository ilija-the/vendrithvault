Pezhu is one of the main settlements of [[Mogxa|Central Mogxa]], and is considered a twin settlement of the nearby [[Poli]]. As the two settlements have began to grow towards each other, some have started referring to the Pezhu-Poli (or Pezhupoli) Metropolis to-be, which could become the largest city in Mogxa besides [[Philosophia]].
