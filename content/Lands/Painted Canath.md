Painted Canath, also called Painted Canath, makes up the northern edge of [[Canath]], sitting between the [[Celene]] and beginning of [[Ferweth]]. Its nickname comes from the culture of the tribes which once inhabited it, whom painted their bodies in various patters - a tradition which some Canathis there have began to revive believing it to have [[Magic|magical]] powers.

Notable locations in Painted Canath include:
- [[Twinvale]]
- [[Sharghei's Landing]]
- [[Aramtun]]
- [[Haldrine Halls]]
- [[Shahale]]
- [[Elaidotel Wall]]
- [[Kiltox]]
- [[Gaonmeli]]
