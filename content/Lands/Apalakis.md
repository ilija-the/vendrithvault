Apalakis is an area and [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa|Draconic Tribe]] on the northern edge of the [[Sergetis|Sergetine Crescent]] in [[Mogxa]], officially swearing loyalty to the [[Sanitorium]]. Their lands encompass a path that runs across mountains in order to avoid the deep jungles of [[Depalakis]], which constitutes one of the paths to get up or down the peninsula (without the flight of [[Dragons]]).
