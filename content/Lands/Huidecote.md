Huidecote (pronounced Huidecott) is a coastal settlement on the easternmost edge of the [[Mogxa|Northern Mogxan Shore]], leading into the Mogxan Lakes when going inland or the mountains of [[Fendur Mountains|Fendur]] to the east. It can cross these mountains into the lands of the [[Fenrunr|Fenruni]] through the [[Greyhold]] pass.
