Transbluff is a settlement of [[Galae#Craggy and Eastern Galae|East Galaen]] nomads that were convinced to settle down by [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] authorities of the [[Mogxa]] - though the term is also used for the tribes that retain their older ways of life in the area around it still. It sits not far from the [[Dwarven Bluffs]] that denote the edge of Craggy Galae. 

They remain relatively free from the influence of [[Cragmoore]].
