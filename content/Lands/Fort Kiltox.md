Kiltox is a fortress settlement on the westernmost edge of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]], which lies north of the [[Celene]]. It is mainly staffed by warriors from [[Sharghei's Landing]]. Kiltox has long served as the most important bastion against the [[Forbidden Coast]] to its west, and has also played a mostly symbolic role in guarding the [[Celtwix|Celtwix Strait]] - together with the settlement of [[Isthram Sedu]] on the other side.
