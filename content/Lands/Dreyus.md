Dreyus is a settlement in [[Mogxa|Eastern Mogxa]] sitting in-between rivers running down into the [[Malanan Townships]]. Unlike the Malanan Towns, Dreyus is under direct [[Sanitorium]] control, and is home to a Sanitorium building in the hills to its west.

Dreyus is connected to [[Guaneste]] to the north. It also connected to [[Singsong]], though has to cross a number of very wide and difficult rivers to get there.
