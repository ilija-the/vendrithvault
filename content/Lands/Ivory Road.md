The Ivory Road is a long-standing trade route that stretches west-east through [[Galae]], starting around the slopes of the [[Galae#The High Plateau|Plateau]], and landing in [[Mogxa]] through the southern of its two land bridges around [[The Scar]]. As its name implies, it was once known for carrying Galaen Ivory into those lands. The [[Heavenly Wall]] was built up on this land-bridge over fears of an invasion through it. While trade continues to pass through, suspicion and checks of [[Mercantilism|merchants]] passing through has likely reduced the volume a significant amount.
