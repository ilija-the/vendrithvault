Sharghei's Landing, also known as just Sharghei, is a large settlement on the northern coast of the [[Celene]] of [[Canath]], which dominates the western edge of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]]. The settlement is named after its semi-mythical founder, a hero that [[Inventor|invented]] strange flying wings he crossed the Celene with against all expectations. Sharghei's Landin enjoys a sizeable population of [[Amurrun]] for Canath, just as Sharghei's Landing does.

Sharghei enjoys a bit of a rivalry with [[Twinvale]], which rules the eastern edge of the Painted Province. The central settlement of [[Aramtun]] is often drawn into this rivalry.

Up the mountainside wilds to the west lies the castle-town of [[Fort Kiltox]], which is staffed by many from Sharghei's Landing.
