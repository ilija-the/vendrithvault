Kallea is a large and central city in [[Canath]], straddling the [[Celene|Celestarian]] coast while also connecting to the neighbouring [[Vön]] and [[Galae]]. It is the most well-known city of [[Canath]], and as thus it is usually known as the 'capital of Canath' to outsiders. It is home to the largest [[Crusader Armies|Crusader Host]] which is camped around its periphery. Kallea has historically lead the Kallean Coalition, an empire of city states and only barely willing 'allies.' It honed its abilities through a number of wars against its more naval-focused rivals, during which it developed a relatively martial culture for a city.

Kallea dominates the eastern half of the [[Heroa]] area of Canath, also called Heroic Canath. It has great access to [[Babael]] of [[Galae]] through the lands of [[Balcanath]], as well as [[Vön]] through the nearby [[Vestitur]] and [[Riverside]].
