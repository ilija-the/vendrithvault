Kallea is a large and central city in [[Canath]], straddling the [[Celene|Celestarian]] coast while also connecting to the neighbouring [[Vön]] and [[Galae]]. It is the most well-known city of [[Canath]], and as thus it is usually known as the 'capital of Canath' to outsiders.
