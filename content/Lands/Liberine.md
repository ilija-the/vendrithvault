Liberine is a coastal settlement of [[Hommagong]] in [[Mogxa]], only a little smaller than the largest settlement of [[Tributaris]] to its east. It celebrates its founding history as that of an army that waged a war of liberation against the [[Mogxan Empire]] which temporarily routed the imperial forces and then settled down to create a new city of freemen.
