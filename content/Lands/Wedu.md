Wedu is a [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] settlement serving as the westernmost outpost up against the beginning of [[Zardoba]] and [[Hommagong]]. The governors of Wedu have throughout history been involved in a number of corruption scandals, such as embezzling the tribute from the [[Hommagong|Tributary Coast]], which has gained the settlement an ill reputation.

The lands of Wedu also stretch out to the sea, where some smaller coastal settlements grant access to [[The Rend]], [[Rubyfirth Bay]] and [[Sergetis|Mouth of Mogxa]].
