The Yumuri Kingdom lies on the westernmost edge of [[Hommagong]], and at the southern edge of historical [[Zardoba]]. It is one of the larger and more militaristic realms that have existed in the Hommagong peninsula, imitating the realms of [[Mogxa]] and [[Canath]] more-so than their southern neighbours. The royal family claims descendance from a trusted aide of [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]] that managed the province during the [[Idurian Empire]], though this is contested.

The land of [[Yumur]], the largest fortress and settlement of the Yumuri Kingdom, was handed over to direct [[Sanitorium]] control by the former king. It is uncertain how the current king feels about this arrangement - with many citizens at least feeling slighted that they lost their heartlands without a fight. Some fear that this discontent opens up the door for [[Crusaders|Crusader]] influence on the Yumuri.
