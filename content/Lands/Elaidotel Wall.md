The Eiladotel Wall is a fortress settlement up the road from the settlement of [[Gaonmeli]] and [[Shahale]], and denotes part of the end of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]] towards [[Ferweth|Far Ferweth]]. Like [[Twinvale|Twinvale's]] famous "Battle of a Hundred Heroes," the Elaiodtel Wall serves as a defence against tribal and [[Monsters of Vendrith|monster]] incursions from the north.
