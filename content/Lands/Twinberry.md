Twinberry is a town that straddles two sides of a ravine, the [[Twinvale]], usually connected by a rope bridge. The settlement in a sense serves as the edge of [[Ferweth]], both east where the edge of [[Galae]] begins, as well as south and down the vale where the Painted Province of [[Canath]] sits. The town finds itself on the lesser used mountain route through Ferweth, and serves as the definitive starting point of the region.

The [[Twinvale City]] can be seen from Twinberry on the rare good weather days, but there is little contact between the two due to the stark height difference and difficulties in climbing it.

Twinberry has been known to observe superstition, such as using garlic to ward off bloodsucking creatures and showing hospitality to [[Shoony]] to avoid being cursed. The town has experienced various attacks from [[Vampyr]], which get worse during [[Blood Rain]].

Nearby the town is a [[Shrines|Shrine]] to [[Saint Jonathan]] at a depleted silver mine.

Notable figures of Twinberry include:
- [[Father Sullivan]], a leader figure and priest. His teachings are that of Saint Jonathan.
- [[Beast King Luca|Lucas]], Sullivan's son killed by [[Valentine Volmari|Valentine]].
- [[Kaya Venstock]], owner of the Vennstock Pot tavern.
