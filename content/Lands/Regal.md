Regal is a large island in [[The Rend]], covered by dense vegetation much like the mainland whose sphere it has usually belonged to - [[Nagonkor]]. Today, it serves as site of conflict whereupon the [[Crusaders|Crusaders]] attempt to lead the [[Eternal Crusade]] from, to both local resistance and period [[Sanitorium]] naval attacks.

Regal is also home to many of the [[Rend Peoples]].
