Deria encompasses a variety of smaller islands outside the immediate shores of [[Lorgun]] to its north, scattered around the seas on the way to [[Horiza|Horiza]]. Often claimed only by [[Dragons]] and the animals that live on them. These dragons govern only themselves as individuals, though are often at war with the peoples of Lorgun. The islands are a beautiful tropical sight, incredibly full of varied [[Animals|animal]]-life that - many of which cannot be found (easily) anywhere else. Some few hunters have dared to go to Derian isles to bring back rare animal pelts or other parts, only to meet their ends to a dragon.

Sailing to the [[Horiza]] can be challenging due to the presence of the Derian islands on the way.

Dragons of the Derian islands have sometimes offered work as [[Mercenaries]] to [[Mogxa|Mogxans]].
