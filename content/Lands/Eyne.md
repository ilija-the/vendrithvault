The Eyne, or Celesteyne, are a series of floating and sometimes moving islands mostly centred above and around the [[Celene|Celestarian]] sea of [[Canath]]. Due to their presence, this is one of the few areas in the world that makes use of costly and cumbersome [[Flying Ships]].
