Guanghold is a village high in the mountains of [[Mogxa]], which can only be reached by a series of narrow mountain paths and a number of rope bridges. It traditionally revers a Sun God, or just light itself, as a folk religion (which tends to escape the ire of the [[Sanitorium]]). It currently finds itself in darkness however, as an [[Eyne]] has drifted in front of it and blocks its sunlight most of the day. The beautiful garden at its centre, with an ancient Peach tree, is slowly dying in the shade.

The former Mayor, and his pet [[Dragonet]], went missing 15 years ago.
