Guaneste (pronounced more like Xuaneste) is a settlement in inland [[Mogxa|Eastern Mogxa]] that once served as the Imperial Capital of the [[Eastern Kobold Dynasty]]. It serves as an administrative, military and commercial hub (for inland routes) in the east, connecting to [[Singsong]], [[Dreyus]] and the [[Apalakis|Apalakis road]] down to the east and south - and the central Mogxan settlements of [[Pezhu]] and [[Poli]]. [[Pezhu]]

The city maintains a bit of a historical rivalry with the [[Imperial City]] due to them claiming the title of Imperial City from them.

[[Mundane Materials|Guano]] is named after this settlement, due to traditionally being mined in large quantities in a number of caves around the city (though the pronunciation has diverged).
