Windfall Commune is a small farming community in the [[Friedport#Phyrraumid|Phyrraumid]] district of [[Friedport]]. They are officially ruled by the mayor, [[Austa]], whom also has a heritage entitling him to a [[Landcrabs|Landcrab Patriarch]] title, but there is little need for use of power and the residents help make decisions together. The commune has taken over the former dilapidated mansion of [[Sir Halvinos]], which they are renovating to serve as an [[Orphanage|Orphans]].

![[Windfall_Farm_Rooftops.png]]

Windfall commune has been the site of a mysterious [[Divine Magic|divine]] fire likely related to the [[Devil Queen of Crimson|Oathbound Firestorm]]. They have also experienced an attack by [[Leyline Creatures]], after [[Magnus Strike]] attempted to tap into local [[Leylines]].
