The Sophine Hills is the outgrowth of [[Philosophia]] that has expended beyond the [[Pactwalls]] to its south. That which has grown outside the walls to the north is called the [[Philosophian Peripherie|Peripherie]]. 

The Sophine Sanitorium is much less dense than Philosophia itself and even the Peripherie, but still maintains a good view over and access to it. It reaches up into the hills, in which a number of castles, manors and estates are built. Most importantly perhaps though is the [[Sophine Sanitorium]] overseeing everything below - the exact one where the Sanitorium Empire itself was proclaimed and the main gathering place that the [[Philosophers of the Round|Philosophers and Knights of the Round]] gather.

Following the Sophine Hill's road to the south leads into Imperial Mogxa, including the [[Imperial City]] that the [[Western Kobold Dynasty]] once sat in.
