Branxi, also known as the Branx Steppe, is a region connecting the [[Guardian Districts]] to core [[Mogxa]]. It skirts the areas between [[Galae#Bluff Galae|Bluff Galae in Eastern Galae]] and the [[Zardoba#Zardoban Desert|Zardoban Desert]]. An area usually settled by nomads throughout its pre-Mogxan history, its settlements are today almost purely Mogxan. An influx of Galaens have arrived however.
