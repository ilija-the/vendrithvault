Branxi, also known as the Branx Steppe, is a region on the western edge of the [[Mogxa#Mogxan March|Mogxan March]] connecting the [[Mogxa]] to core [[Mogxa]]. It skirts the areas between [[Galae#Bluff Galae|Bluff Galae in Eastern Galae]], the Mogxan steppe around the [[Young Bluff]] and the [[Zardoba#Zardoban Desert|Zardoban Desert]] - all rather sparsely populated. Branxi was always an area primarily settled by nomads throughout its pre-Mogxan history, so its settlements are today almost purely Mogxan. An influx of Galaens have arrived more recently, however. 

Trade that comes from the [[Ivory Road]] and through the [[Heavenly Wall]], as well as that coming from [[Cragmoore]], all usually passes through Branxi.
