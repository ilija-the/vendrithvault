Kathayle is the main coastal city of [[Mogxa|Imperial Mogxa]], to the south of the [[Imperial City]] and [[Philosophia]]. Most of the [[Sanitorium]] fleet is based here, and most all [[Mercantilism|trade]] sailing over the [[Rubyfirth Bay]] and [[The Rend]] goes through here. It was once a mere small fishing town of the [[Draconic Empire]]. The [[Underworld]] of Kathayle is infamously active, with a large amount of smuggling, and perhaps even illegal [[Slavery]] happening. Underground [[Gladiators|gladiator]] matches are a big draw of tourism and money.

It contains a lot of [[Kobolds]], and a sizeable minority of [[Dragonbloods]] despite attempts to limit their influence.
