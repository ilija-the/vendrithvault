Titansfell, formerly called Chenlieu, is a settlement in the [[Mogxa#Mogxan March|Mogxan March]] famous for having fielded a battle that [[Titans]] directly participated in, and lost, during the [[War Over Haeven]]. This is celebrated annually as part of the imperial Titanslayer Festivals, which is especially elevated and lively in Titansfell. A number of the [[Titanslayers]] were given manors, castles and estates in the settlement after the war. A number of the veterans of the [[Sanitine Wars]] hoped to earn land in Titansfall, being a prestigious settlement known for being luscious and luxurious.

Titansfell lies on top of a slop, giving it a beautiful view of a large valley of flowers below. This valley served as the battlefield during the settlement's famous battle. Large marks in the ground from the destructive powers used to fight the Titans still mark the landscape, as do a number of swords, spears and other [[Warfare|military]] equipment that can be found sticking out of or being hidden amongst the flowers.

Titansfell is off the biggest main path of the Mogxan March connecting central Mogxa to the [[Mogxa]]. The act of marching through it instead of the main path caught the [[Kobold Throne|Kobold]] leadership off-guard during the War Over Haeven, leading to the Titans having to fill in directly.

The settlement was at the centre of the [[Adventurer Crisis]], being home to many of the important [[Adventurers]] which threatened to dissolve the [[Sanitorium]].
