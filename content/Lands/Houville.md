Houville is a settlement on the [[Mogxa#Mogxan March|Mogxan March]], lying in-between [[Farxeau]] and Central Mogxa. It was famously almost entirely burned down during the revolts and invasion during the [[War Over Haeven]], which is usually blamed on the [[Kobold Throne]] engaging in scorched earth. The evils of this act and their subsequent defeat is celebrated annually in the city as part of the Festival of Fire - during which many [[Charisma|performers]] trained in various acts of fire show off.

Other nearby settlements are [[Farxeau]], [[Titansfell]] and [[Xialray]]
