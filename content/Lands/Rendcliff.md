Rendcliff is a cliffy shore on the eastern [[The Rend|Rend]] that is more hospitable than the [[Zardoba|Zardoban Desert]] around its southeast. While it has often been rather unimportant, the place and its few islands has been patrolled more since the [[Eternal Crusade]] started. It remains under the authority of the city of [[Zardoba]].
