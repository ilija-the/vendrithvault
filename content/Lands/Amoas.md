Amoas is a settlement on the [[Celene]] of [[Canath]], famously enveloped by the cliffs of an essentially impassable terrain called the [[Pyliamoas]] around them, guarding them from land invasion. While it has attempted to use this advantage by building a sizeable navy, it has many times lost it and had to retreat to taking defensive positions. It has built a highly impressive set of coastal fortifications, loaded with magical [[Cannons]], intended to prevent invasion from the sea as well.

While the land around it cannot be reached by regular trade or armies, it can still be traversed by individual [[Adventurers|adventurers]] or those with the right skills for it. A number of tunnels help connect the bluffs, ravines and cliffs of this strange terrain, making for a route that can be used to deliver messages for couriers or such even while under naval blockade.
