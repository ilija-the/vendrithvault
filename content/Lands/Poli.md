Poli is a large settlement in [[Mogxa|Central Mogxa]], often considered a twin settlement of [[Pezhu]]. As the two settlements have began to grow towards each other, some have started referring to the Pezhu-Poli (or Pezhupoli) Metropolis to-be, which could become the largest city in Mogxa besides [[Philosophia]].
