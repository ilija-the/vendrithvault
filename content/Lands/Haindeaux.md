Haindeaux is a settlement on the northern coast of [[Mogxa]]. It is a hub for the sale of fish and other products from the sea ([[Sailing]] and [[Aquatic|Diving]]), including a small amount of [[Mercantilism|trade]] from [[Vilhun]]. To its south is the Mogxan Steppe, which is a long journey to pass through. It can reach the settlement of [[Meizhine]], [[Pezhu]] and [[Poli]] through the settlement of [[Cavalma]]. It can also reach the settlement of [[Divatun]] following the coast to its west, or [[Dehorhain]] following the coast to its east.

The Islands of [[Enui]] are also reachable from Haindeaux, not far to its north.
