Tower Canath, also known as Tower Canath, covers the eastern [[Celene]] coast of [[Canath]] - up against the [[Hightower Mountains]] of [[Galae]]. A small amount of [[Hightower Civilisation|Hightowers]] were present in Tower Canath, though its nickname might just as well be a reference to the prevalence of unique looking watchtowers meant to spot enemy invasion from far - many even sitting on small [[Eyne]].
