Yumur, also known as the Jade Wall, is a fortified settlement on the westernmost coast of [[Mogxa]] not towards the [[Galae||Galaen border]]. It lies in lands which have traditionally skirted the border between old [[Zardoba]] and [[Hommagong]], but which was seized by the [[Mogxan Empire]] directly at one point. It has been developed heavily by the [[Sanitorium]] as an outpost on [[The Rend]] against the [[Crusader Fleet]].

The lands around are still owned by what's sometimes called the [[Yumuri Kingdom]], which is a tributary of the [[Sanitorium]] like the rest of Hommagong.
