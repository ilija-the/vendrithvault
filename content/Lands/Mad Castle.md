The Mad Castle is a castle in inland [[Heroa]] of [[Canath]] famous for repeatedly falling to various monsters such as [[Undead]], and has due to the strong superstitions around it eventually been abandoned to whichever [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]], [[Banditry|bandits]] or [[Barbarian|barbarians]] dare to claim it. [[Adventurers|Heroes]] are occasionally sent by neighbouring city states to clear it when it begins to pose a threat - and some have called for the need to destroy the whole thing for everyone's sake.
