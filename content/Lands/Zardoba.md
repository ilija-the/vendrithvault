Zardoba is a western port city in the [[Sanitorium]] of [[Mogxa]], and has been an important trade hub for centuries. It once served as the only legal port of foreign trade in the Mogxan Empire. Today, its trade has dried up due to the [[Eternal Crusade|Crusader Conflict]], but it remains a port used to build, safekeep and then repair the Navy of the Rend. It also serves an important role feeding and supplying the [[Heavenly Wall]].
