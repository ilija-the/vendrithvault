Zardoba is a region centred on a port city of the same name in western [[Mogxa]], ruled by the [[Sanitorium]] but enjoying considerable autonomy. It has been an important trade hub for centuries, being viewed as a bit of a rival by [[Kathayle]]. It once served as the only legal port of foreign trade in the Mogxan Empire. Today, its trade has dried up due to the [[Eternal Crusade|Crusader Conflict]], but it remains a port used to build, safekeep and then repair the Navy of the Rend. It also serves an important role feeding and supplying the [[Heavenly Wall]].
