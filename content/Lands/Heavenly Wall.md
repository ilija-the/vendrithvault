The Heavenly Wall, also known as Zhanguerrie, is a highly fortified castle-settlement named after a series of large walls that divide it. It lies in the [[Guardian Districts]] of the [[Mogxan Empire]]. The fortifications and settlement was constructed this way in order to guard the [[Mogxa|Mogxan continent]] from invasion across one of the two land-bridges to [[Galae]] - the [[Ivory Road]]. It has been revitalised by the [[Sanitorium]], which fears [[Crusaders|Crusader]] invasion.

Contrary to popular belief, none of the walls actually stretch completely from [[The Scar]] to [[The Rend]] - though a number get close. Their purpose is rather to funnel potential invaders into a number of terrains that are highly favourable to the defenders on the eastern side. There are 3 main tiers of walls, one of which cuts through what is now the core part of the city.

The other possible land route between Mogxa and Galae is north of [[The Scar]]. However, it is especially unstable, and leads into the difficult terrain of [[Cragmoore|Cragrock]] and its Sanitorium ally [[Cragmoore|Cragmoor]]. Consequently, the Crusaders have not seriously attempted to pass it, and instead hold out hope to get through the Grand Gate one day.
