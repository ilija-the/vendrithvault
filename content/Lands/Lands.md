The lands of Vendrith constitute one of its main parts, and is often what is referred to when the term Vendrith is even used. Other [[Cosmology of Vendrith|Planes]] of existence than [[Materia]] are usually not included, as the limited knowledge and study of them are an entirely different field.

>  In the folder structure of the vviki, the >Lands folder contains all manner of locations in the material plane ranging from Continents, Regions, Areas, Settlements and specific landmarks of a natural or man-made variety. It also includes the /current/ or very recently gone polities that Governs it - historical governments and empires meanwhile should go into >History.
