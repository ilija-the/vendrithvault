The Hommagong, also known as Tributaris or the Tributary Coast, refers to the peninsula at the south-western edge of [[Mogxa]]. It has never had any large unified realms, and thus has mostly had to swear allegiance and pay tribute to the larger [[Mogxan Empire]] neighbouring it. It has remained remarkably quiet and rural, usually free of conflict. The Hommagongar residents include many [[Rabbitfolk]] and [[Ysoki]] living in what's called shires. The rare [[Pnoll]] are also often sighted around its lands. 

Hommagong enjoys some level of cultural community across [[The Rend]], though it has lost some of this element due to Mogxacisation over the centuries.
