Shargei's Landing, also known as just Shargei, is a large settlement on the northern coast of the [[Celene]] of [[Canath]], which dominates the western edge of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]]. The settlement is named after its semi-mythical founder, a hero that invented wings he flew across the Celene on.
