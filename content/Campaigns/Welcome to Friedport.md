**Setting** Friedport and adjacent isles, 1274 ADc.
**Players** Donna, Niko, Morgan, Aeon and Panda.
**Time** 2 sessions, in late 2022.

Welcome to Friedport was the first game ever set in Vendrith. It was a [[Campaigns|oneshot campaign]] that ran two sessions in late 2022, including the players Donna, Niko, Morgan, Aeon and Panda.

The opening of Welcome to Friedport is concurrent with that of [[The Landcrab Plot]], taking place exactly when a [[Horizan Guild Association|HGA]] convoy arrives in [[Friedport]] spring of 1274 ADc - carrying the party members of both campaigns.
