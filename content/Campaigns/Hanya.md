Hanya is a (mostly) retired knight who has been in [[Sora Lockneed]]'s retinue for much of her entire career in [[Kantby]]. After being in an advisory role for many years, she has found herself appointed to the role of commander of the Kantby milita, taking it away from [[Haru Lockneed]].
