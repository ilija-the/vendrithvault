**Setting** Mogxa.
**Players** Aeon, Neldu, Crysanthos and Lycoris. 
**Time** 4 session, in Autumn 2023.

A oneshot ran in-person for an anonymous party of 3. Begins unravelling the mysteries of a dying mountain village named Guanghold, once worshipping a folk religion of the sun itself but now laid in darkness in the shadow cast by an [[Eyne]].
