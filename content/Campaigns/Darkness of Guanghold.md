
Set in a Mogxan mountain, path to a village once known as the village of light because it caught the earliest rays of sunlight in the valley - but now lies in the shade of an [[Eyne]] that has drifted after an earthquake. Above the now shade-covered village also looms various metal chains, from attempts to pull the Eyne away, but which make the place look imprisoned. 


## Pregens

Dwarf Rogue
A thief turned undertaker to rob customers, corpses, caskets and catacombs when convenient, whom also got good at the job, as well as fighting off undead, in the process.

Half-elf Cleric
A scholar and servant of the sun dragon of local myth, has been hunting for forgotten rites and traditions to restore the village.

Human Fighter
A veteran whom got injured in the last war and was disillusioned with the empire, but who still staunchly stands in service of the people of his home.
