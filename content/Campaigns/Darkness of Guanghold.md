**Setting** The mountains in which Guanghold resides, Mogxa.
**Players** Anonymous.
**Time** 1 session (so far), summer of 2026.

A oneshot ran in-person for a party of 3. Begins unravelling the mysteries of a dying mountain village named Guanghold, once worshipping a folk religion of the sun itself but now laid in darkness in the shadow cast by an [[Eyne]]. Likely to be followed up with another 2 games.
