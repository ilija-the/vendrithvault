A group of migrants to [[Friedport]] that were recruited by [[The Lord]] to do a mission. The mission involved stealing back a [[Mysterious Iron Crate]] that had been taken from the Lord's associates by an unknown assailant, which the party had to identify. After investigation the crime scene, the party deduced the culprit as [[Nazaar Eviljaw]] (aided by his bodyguard dromaar). The Welcoming Party set off to Oozemaw Isle, a small island with a sea-accessible cave in which the mysterious iron crate was being traded to a masked individual. Nazaar was knocked out, the crate secured, and the welcoming party was given their reward from the Lord.

Part of the welcoming party was:
[[Pike]], an amurrun swashbuckler that has made enemies hunting pirates.
[[Ergruff Ward]], a crusader kitsune gunslinger and rare survivor of [[Veridica]].
[[Gen]], a poppet-tengu monk that was shipped off to Horiza by a village he believes didn't appreciate his theatrics.
[[Boney]], a kholo alchemist that fled Galae to seek a better future with his friend [[Bloodpaw Bonepaw]].
[[Kholek]], a kobold thaumaturge that was once hired by the Sanitorium as an archaeologist.

Unbeknownst to all in the welcoming party but Gen, the masked individual turned out to be the kobolds [[Odd]] and [[Chimney]] in a trenchcoat, working for [[Vendy Vendetta Rapidly]].
