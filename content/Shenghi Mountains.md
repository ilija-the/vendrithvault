The Shengi Mountains are an enormous mountain-range that divides [[Mogxa]] into east and west, perhaps amongst the highest mountains in the world. The peaks surround something of a high Plateau in the middle of them. Making traversal even more difficult, the mountains and plateau are covered in thick jungle all over - full of dangerous wildlife and over-sized flora and megafauna. The [[Lakes of Life]] exist at its centre.
