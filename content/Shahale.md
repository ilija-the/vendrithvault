Shahale is a middling settlement in the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]], and exists a bit up a navigable river that leads out into the [[Celene]]. Further up the river and road lie [[Gaonmeli]] and the [[Elaidotel Wall]]. Following the road or coast west takes you to the hub of [[Shargei]].
