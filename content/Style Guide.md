### Quick Obsidian Guide


### Titles
Names are important.

Since proper quotation marks (") cannot be used in titles, use a double apostrophe ('') instead.

Make sure to add aliases to your pages. If you're writing about a location, try to include any historical or alternative names for the place. If you're writing a character, you'll usually want to include their most commonly used name (first name or nickname) as an alias. If you're writing about a concept, the title might be something like ''Ancestries of Vendrith,'' but you'll be remis not to add an alias for just ''ancestry.''

### Tags
Adding proper tags to your article should be top priority, as it is easily forgotten later if not. Always get the most appropriate tag down if possible - either people, places, events, society or cosmos. You are also recommended to do a nested tag for places, such as places/iduria/mogxa/philosophia for a location in Philosophia (do not include the philosophia part of the tag if you are describing philosophia itself however). 

To keep things simple, the format remains-
places → 1) continental region, that being either Iduria, Eydwin or Horiza → 2) region, such as Canath, Mogxa or Horizan Peninsula → 3) settlement, if applicable → 4) specific locale, such as a tavern, a famous rock or an academy.

For planes other than that of [[Materia]], use the cosmos tag.
cosmos → plane → region, if applicable → settlement, if applicable → specific locale.

Articles highly related to a campaign should be tagged with its campaign name, such as landcrab-plot or road-to-longdawn.

### Description
The exact ordering of information is highly dependent on how much info there is, and which parts may be most relevant, but there are some general guidelines to be aware of.

Before anything you may need clarifications such as "not to be confused with..." to distinguish it from other similarly titled articles. Beyond that, start with simple and short description.
