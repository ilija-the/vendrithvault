### Quick Obsidian Guide


### Titles
Names are important.

Since proper quotation marks (") cannot be used in titles, use a double apostrophe ('') instead.

Make sure to add aliases to your pages. If you're writing about a location, try to include any historical or alternative names for the place. If you're writing a character, you'll usually want to include their most commonly used name (first name or nickname) as an alias. If you're writing about a concept, the title might be something like ''Ancestries of Vendrith,'' but you'll be remis not to add an alias for just ''ancestry.''
