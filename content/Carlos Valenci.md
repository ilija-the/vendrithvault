Carlos Valenci was a secular priest who played an important role in early history of [[Friedport]], and would later become a hated individual forced to live in hiding until his death. 

During a period of decline and a mysterious ailment affecting the land and its flora, Carlos Valenci played the role of an inquisitor trying to find who was responsible - blaming it on lying witches, rituals and evil sorcery. One day him and some [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] followers of his were delving through tunnels under the city searching for the crisis' source, whereupon he found a young girl using strange magical tools to try to break into an enormous network of roots. Blaming her for the crisis and being ignored as he asked for her to stop (not understanding or hearing the words she rambled through tears), he pulled out a [[Firearms|firearm]] and shot her.
