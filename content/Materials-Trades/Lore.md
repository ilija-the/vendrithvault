Lore is accumulated knowledge, usually delivered through tradition, or later, scribing, that provides insights into the past and wisdom about the [[Mythos of Vendrith]], how to organise [[Society]], and the purpose of [[Religion]]. There are also subsections of lore for pretty much all specific practical topics, such as [[Medicine]] and [[Survival]] to name a few.
