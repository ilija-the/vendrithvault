Note: Not to be confused with [[Redpowder]]. There is no blackpowder or gunpowder in Vendrith.

A golden powder infused with [[Divine|divine]] explosive properties when in the hands of a religious person. The rituals of its creation were taught in the [[New Creed]], alongside the instructions to grow the [[Firearms]] the powder is usually used for.
