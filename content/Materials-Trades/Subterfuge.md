Subterfuge is a vast field encompassing the many talents useful in performing crimes, and earning otherwise advantages over others in a way that avoids direct and/or fair confrontation. 

### Stealth
Stealth is the art of remaining, ideally, undetected, or at the very least hidden or unseen.

### Thievery
Thievery entails lockpicking, the disabling of traps, and pickpocketing.

The underworld is a term referring to the criminal side every society has.
