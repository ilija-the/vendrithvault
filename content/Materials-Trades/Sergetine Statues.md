The Sergetine Statues are large statues that were made for the purposes of [[Sergetis|Sergetine]] Theatre, during which the statues, often made of stones, were spectacularly animated as actors in the performance. They often played roles of ancient wise mentors aiding the protagonist(s), or of the villains.

These statues now lie at the bottom of the [[Sergetis]] since the [[Sinking of Sergetis]].
