"The underworld" is the side of society that operates outside of the bounds of the law, either fragrantly or partially, engaging in a variety of crimes. This can range from crimes such as theft, smuggling and corruption, to conspiracy, kidnapping and murder. While underworld trades can encompass a variety of skills, the most commonly used ones are [[Thievery]], [[Stealth]], and [[Deception]].

Some of those who live in the underworld as a lifestyle are classed as [[Rogue|Rogues]].
