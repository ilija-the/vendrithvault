Jed is an island that divides [[The Rend]] and/or the [[Rubyfirth Bay]]. It has long been considered the ultimate crossroads between the civilisations of [[Canath]], [[Mogxa]] and [[Talvas]], as well as perhaps the people of [[Galae]] and [[Nagonkor]].

The island of Jed has since the conquests of [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]] served as an official place of exchange between civilisations, in terms of commerce, language and culture and more. Jed predominantly speaks the [[Languages|Idurian]] language that serves as the court and commercial language of many of its neighbours, even amongst the common folk. Jed can be considered the last inheritor of a uniquely mixed Canathi-Mogxan culture. 

The largest settlement on Jed is the city of Jedaia. Jedaia is home to the official headquarters of the [[Horizan Guild Association]], as well as a large [[Forgotten Dawn Society|Forgotten Dawn]] office.
