> [!important] History rewriting-shenanigans.
> At the end of the Landcrab Plot campaign, a phenomenon potentially related to the Daydreaming Truth occurred in which most of the city of Friedport remembered the details of Karvi Massiri's influence in the city differently from the [[Newcomers of Friedport|Newcomers]] that stopped it. In short: the newcomers remembered him for having lead the [[Landcrab Plot]], taken over command of the local [[Desecrators]], and murdering dozens towards his end of an independent Friedport where lies could no longer be told. Towards this end he had obtained the [[Vestitur Relics]] and prepared the local [[Leylines]] to essentially cast an enormous spell over the city. What follows in this article is the version of events that most citizens remember, however.
