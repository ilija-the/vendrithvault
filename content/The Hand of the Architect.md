The Hand of the Architect is a person rumoured to aid the fabled [[The Architect (Friedport)|Architect]] of Friedport.

The Hand of the Architect (from here on just "The Hand") is the Architect's twin brother, and a blind man. Sympathetic to his sister not being taken seriously enough to have her architectural plans turned into reality, he began playing a falsified identity and made himself out to be a respected male architect, while in reality just forwarding her designs. Doing so, the twins saw many of the Architect's plans come to fruition.
