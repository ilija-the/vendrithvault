Greenkleks is the island directly west of [[Isle of Flod|Flod]], known for its impeccable green forests that hardly shrink down with exploitation. With this industrial strength, it is also the primary manufacturing center of ships for [[Vilhun]], with the [[Dragonbreath Island]] being a far second. Primarily populated by [[Goblins]], this gives them outsized strength in naval logistics and warfare, one which the clans associated with the [[Horde Lord]] gladly use to their advantage.
