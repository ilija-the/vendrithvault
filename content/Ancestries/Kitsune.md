Kitsune are a shape-changing [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] believed to originate in [[Vilhun]] or [[Mewji]]. Their primary forms are either like humans, sometimes with fox traits, or as anthropomorphic foxes, often with fur colouration considered rare with actual foxes. Their secondary forms can either be that of an anthropomorphic fox as described previously, or as an actual fox, which appears similarly to a primary form. Kitsune are known tricksters, and are generally mistrusted by the peoples aware of their mythology. They are also only rarely able to have kids, making them few and far inbetween.

Many kitsune become travellers, either as traveling merchants, sailors, pilgrims or [[Adventurers|adventurers]].
