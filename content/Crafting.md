### Overview
Crafting is a skill and trade encompassing the use of materials to create, manipulate and repair items, objects, buildings and the like. Those who craft as a trade often specialise in specific fields, but may have a wide competency. Common specialties are those used in the pursuit of specific trades - such as blacksmithing, fletching, ceramics and glass-working, and the like.

Some special fields of Crafting require special competency to be done. Namely - Alchemy and Artifice. Others are the specialties of [[Inventor|inventors]], which can make things as varied as mechanical weapons, construct companions and power armours. 

#### Alchemy
Alchemical crafting revolves around the use of various elements, often fluids, salts, and minerals, that are commonly made into drinkable liquids or explosive bombs. 

#### Artifice
Artifice, or [Magical Crafting](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5178), refers to types of crafting that incorporate magic. Due to their similar nature following rules, equations and systems, artifice often specifically makes use of [[Arcane Magic]]. 

#### Engineering
Engineering is crafting specifically focused on the making of machines and large structures.
