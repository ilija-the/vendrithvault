Official rules page on Crafting.

Crafting is a skill and trade encompassing the use of materials to create, manipulate and repair items, objects, buildings and the like. Those who craft as a trade often specialise in specific fields but have a wide competency.

Some special fields of Crafting require special competency to be done. Namely - Alchemy and Artifice.
