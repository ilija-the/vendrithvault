The Sophine Sanitorium is an ancient Sanitorium building that sits in the [[Sophine Hills]] - famous for becoming the place the [[Sanitorium]] was proclaimed in, and to this day perhaps the most important gathering place of the [[Philosophers of the Round|Philosophers and Knights of the Round]]. The Sanitorium has the massive jungles and mountains of the [[Shengi Mountains]] behind it, and in front enjoys a gorgeous view over [[Philosophia]] and Imperial Mogxa. A number of other important estates, monasteries and castles also sit on these hills.
