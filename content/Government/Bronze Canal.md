The Bronze Canal is a marvel of [[Crafting#Engineering|Engineering]] that creates a narrow waterway between eastern [[Canath]] (Encanath) that reaches into the Galaen Wilds of [[Galae]]. It is narrow, and demands the use of various cranes and water locks, but it bypasses the need to ascend the many steps of the [[Path of Gold]].
