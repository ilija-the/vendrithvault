The Tributary Coast refers to the peninsula at the south-western edge of [[Mogxa]], which has never had any large unified realms, and thus has mostly had to swear allegiance and pay tribute to the larger [[Mogxan Empire]] neighbouring it. It has remained remarkably quiet and rural, usually free of conflict. It's residents include many [[Rabbitfolk]] and [[Ysoki]] living in what's called shires. The rare [[Pnoll]] are also often sighted around its lands.
