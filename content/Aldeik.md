Aldeik is a province towards southern [[Fenrunr]], before the start of the southernmost marches and [[Reivlands]]. While it is somewhat protected by the [[Fendur Mountains]] to the west, the largest and easiest passage through the mountains passes into the province of Aldeik. The province is somewhat poorer and less settled than the north.

Aldeik has some of the oldest historical artefacts of the Fenruni people, and is considered their 'arrival point' from wherever they originated from.
