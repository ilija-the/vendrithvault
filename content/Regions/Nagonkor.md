A small region bordering [[Encanath]] and [[Galae]]. Its inhabitans are said to be uncivilised barbarians, hiding in jungles and mountains to ambush unwelcome outsiders.

This is despite the fact they once hosted an advanced civilization that stretched into Galae - then known as [[Galaekor]].

This people is said to come from the south - possibly [[Veducia]].
