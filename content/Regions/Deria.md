A series of smaller islands far out at sea to the east of [[Mogxa]] and the [[Old World - Iduria]] at large. Its closest neighbour however is the island-chain of [[Lorgun]].

Often claimed only by [[Dragons]] and the animals that live on them. These dragons govern only themselves as individuals, though are often at war with the peoples of Lorgun.

Sailing to the [[New World - Horiza]] can be challenging due to the presence of the Derian islands on the way.

Dragons of the Derian islands have sometimes offered work as [[Mercenaries]] to [[Mogxa]]ns.
