

Inspired by the [[Sibling Dragons]], many titans have at some point or other entered into a Titan Trinity, consisting of three titans whose [[Mantle|mantles]] have some overlap. Traditionally, two titans have occupied two mostly opposite positions, while the third titan stands atop with a synthesis of the two others. This is thought to give the top titan power over the other two, especially as they become more like the top titan, though it is also believed to strengthen the two titans below over time.
