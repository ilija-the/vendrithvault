The First Kobold Dynasty is the kobold dynasty that succeeded the [[Draconic Empire]] immediately following the [[Draconic Concordat]], formally founded in 0 ADc (though it took a few years for their rule to become accepted). Their early rule is characterised by conflict against remaining hostile dragons, which eventually took the imperial province of [[Lorgun]] as their own, effectively breaking it off from the Mogxan Empire. The First Kobold Dynasty subsequently entered an era of flourishing arts, culture and diplomacy as the veil between them and the world the dragons had once terrorised was lifted.

The First Kobold Dynasty met its end quite violently and suddenly during the Trimillennial Revolution in 333 ADc. The [[Eastern Kobold Dynasty]] followed.
