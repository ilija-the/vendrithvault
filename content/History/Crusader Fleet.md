The [[Crusaders|Crusader]] cause is most known for its large [[Crusader Armies|Armies]], but it also maintains a sizeable fleet built along the southern coasts of [[Canath]] leading into the [[Rubyfirth Bay]]. This fleet is meant to project power into [[The Rend]] and the [[Sergetis|Mouth of Mogxa]], and therefore to threaten the [[Sanitorium]] with invasion. The navy has for a long time built up without any large confrontations, as part of an arms race with the Sanitorium. It was used in a rapid operation in order to try to conquer [[Jed]], after the Crusaders turned hostile to the [[Horizan Guild Association]] based there.
