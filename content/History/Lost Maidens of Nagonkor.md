An expedition of talented and spiritually important members of the [[Universal Temple]] of [[Philosophia]], famous for going missing. There is much speculation and many stories and rumours going about - both about their eventual demise, and as to why they decided the need to build a temple, and so urgently that they had to go directly through Nagonkor at that.
