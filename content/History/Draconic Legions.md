The Draconic Legions were army corps largely filled with [[Dragonbloods]] after the [[Draconic Concordat]] in the [[Kobold Throne|Kobold Empire]]. They famously wore flat metal hat-like helmets with horns. The armies of the [[Sanitorium]] were modelled on the Draconic Legions, which is visible in their uniforms. The [[Knights of the Round]] also copied the famous hat into their armour.
