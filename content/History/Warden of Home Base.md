"Warden of Home Base" is the title used to refer to the person that runs the near-mythical secretive [[Home Base]] where the [[Crusaders|Crusaders]] supposedly make their plans. While the [[Iron Lotus]] is the broadly accepted figurehead of the Crusaders and would likely have the loyalty of such a base too, in practice she'd need a second-in-command to manage the place and keep it secret, even as she moves around to do other things.
