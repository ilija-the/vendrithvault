While many Mogxan Empire have existed throughout [[History]], it is a remarkable constant that there almost always is a Mogxan Empire of sorts that dominates most of the peninsula. The few exceptions are those in times of civil conflict, such as the [[Mogxan Interregnum]]. 

Mogxan Empires that have existed are:
- The [[Draconic Empire]] is usually at the beginning of history books. Pre-Draconic Mogxan Empire certainly existed, but little remains and little is known.|
- The [[Kobold Throne]] started with [[First Kobold Dynasty]].
- ...continued with [[Eastern Kobold Dynasty]].
- was interrupted by the [[Idurian Empire]].
- and then after the interregnum, returned a [[Western Kobold Dynasty]].
- Before the Mogxan Empire was taken over by the [[Sanitorium]].
