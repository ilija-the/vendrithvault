The "March of the Corpse Flowers" is a title given in hindsight to a [[Crusader]] crusade that attempted to open the [[Regal Path]] to the Crusaders for the future. Taking heavy attrition and facing opposition by the [[Nagonkor|Nagonkori]] whom continued to attack despite (forced) agreements with religious leaders, the operation secured no lasting benefit and resulted in the effective destruction of most of the [[Crusader Armies|Crusader Army]] that performed it. The commander in charge got demoted partway through, informed by the arrival of a messenger bird. The replacement was [[General Architrave]].

The (anti-)climax of the March was the siege of [[Galaekor|Genxoqul]] in early 1252 ADc, a part of the heavily jungled region nested in a mountain valley known as Galaekor.
