The dromaar (also known as half-orcs) are one of the newer [[Ancestries of Vendrith]]. While [[Orcs]] are the result of ritual use of the Lotus Orcus, Dromaar are the result of non-lotus users procreating either with another non lotus-using orc or human-like ancestries.
