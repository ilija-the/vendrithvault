Halflings are a short [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] often reminiscent of young humans. What sets them apart is seemingly random features such as hooves, thin tails and horns (often compared to [[Devil|devils]]), the rubbery texture of their body, and the [[Halfling Markings]] which appear on their skin as they live life - documenting important events in the form of hieroglyphs. While halflings don't tend to visually age like humans, one can usually tell a halfling's rough age by the amount of markings on them. Importantly, halflings are not born, but rather found wrapped up like "rubber eggs." They tend to only awaken once another ancestry has found them, many of which end up adopting the addled and inexperienced halfling.

![[halfling_example_from_ico_concept_art.png]]

Halflings are usually viewed with mistrust however, and many are either left behind by their finders or go on to live lives in which they have to learn to be quiet and take up little space. It is perhaps because of this that halflings are known to be stealthy. One job they often end up taking is that of helping in kitchens, away from the scrying eyes of customers.

Some halflings, left behind after being discovered, "hatch" and come to with nobody to guide them or teach them other languages. Usually staying in the ruins or wilderness they're found in, these halflings have an ominous reputation, often dubbed "feral halflings."
