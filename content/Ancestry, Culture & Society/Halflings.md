Halflings are a short [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] often reminiscent of young humans. What sets them apart is seemingly random features such as hooves, thin tails and horns (often compared to [[Devil|devils]]), the rubbery texture of their body, and the [[Halfling Markings]] which appear on their skin as they live life - documenting important events in the form of hieroglyphs.
