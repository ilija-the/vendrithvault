Elves are a mystical and constructed people who were at the centre of the advanced [[Hightower Civilisation]]. They are known to have an otherworldy beauty to them, but also for creeping many other ancestries out with their doll-like features. Their emotions, behaviour and once society are known to be aloof and somewhat hard to understand in its complexity.

Since the downfall of the Hightowers, elves have only become rarer in numbers. The formula for creating new elves appears to be lost - despite countless attempts to recreate it. One tradition that can be said to have descended from trying to recreate elves is that of [[Automatons]]. Another, is the much smaller [[Poppets]].

Not having a known definite lifespan, the elves that still exist likely do so from the hightower days or soon after them, but all exhibit elements of amnesia - either affecting their earliest memories, or specifically targeting the days of the Hightower Civilisation. 

A much more common remnant of the elves is the [[Aiuvarin]] (also known as half-elves), which are the offspring resulting from procreation with an elf (or another aiuvarin).



A constructed people appearing like large but sophisticated and beautiful dolls or figurines.

Attempts to recreate elves have usually resulted in the creation of [[Automatons]].

Some smaller recreations of elves, or even possibly smaller original elves themselves, are called [[Poppets]].
