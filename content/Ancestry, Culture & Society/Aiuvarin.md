The aiuvarin, also known as "half-elves" are one of the [[Ancestries of Vendrith]]. They are the result when [[Elves|elves]] procreate with humans or closely related ancestries. Unlike the elves, the aiuvarin consist of regular flesh and blood, though they still share much of the bodily size and form of elves. They are known to be skinnier and vary more in height than humans, have pointy ears, and usually have black sclera (especially those with darkvision).
