The amurrun (also known as catfolk) are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] that appear like bipedal anthropomorphic cats, and are typically quite slim and tall. Most amurrun are of bright golden fur colours, there are also those of darker shades. The amurrun, famous for being the builder class of the [[Hightower Civilisation]] under the [[Elves]], continue to be known as visionaries when it comes to [[Inventor|invention]] - in particular due to their [[Amurrun Markings]]. In short, Amurrun are born with complex magical glyphs (akin to tattoos that glow through their fur) that represent various items, buildings or even rituals. Many amurrun find it of utmost importance to create what their markings represent, such as by taking up a trade related to it.
