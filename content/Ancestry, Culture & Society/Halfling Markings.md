Halfling Markings are the coloured hieroglyphics which appear on a [[Halflings|halfling]] throughout their lives. These appear to represent various important events that the halfling in question experienced first-hand, such as winning a competition, getting married, or experiencing a war or assassination of a king. Due to its function in recording history, many halflings are known to go into genealogy, history, and journalism. 

There is some debate as to whether the creation of these marks is done by the force of the halflings themselves.
