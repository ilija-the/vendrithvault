Animal Companions is a term used to refer to a variety of animals that follow and assist people, usually [[Adventurers|adventurers]]. These companions often are often well trained or in tune with their instincts. Many animal companions are raised from a young age by their masters.

[[Familiars|Familiars]] and [[Eidolon|Eidolons]] are similar companions, though familiars tend to be infused with magic and Eidolons are bound to summoners.
