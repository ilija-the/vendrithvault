The Wan Islets is a collective name for all tiny islands surrounding [[Vilhun]] on the outside, and thus also closest to the [[World Fleece]]. They are known for being particularly bleak and washed of color. Any civilization on them is sparse, primarily taken up by pastures and [[Lighthouses from Hell]].
