#deities
A term for the deific figures at the centre of [[Canath|Canathi]] religion. Said to have originated from the [[First Ascended]]. 

The term titan has also been applied to figures outside of Canathi religions, such as the [[One & Only|One & Only of Vilhun]]. This is not a term usually used by local practitioners of such religions, though scholars may agree on the similarities and use the term.

It is said all titans were exiled from materia with the destruction of the [[Rainbow Bridge]], marking the beginning of the [[The Great Silence]].
