A type of weapon made by growing a specific type of plant in a particular ritualistic way as instructed by the [[New Creed]]. As a form of [[Divine Magic]], this has been limited to religious people, particularly the [[Crusaders]].

Non-divine ways of recreating firearms have mostly failed, in particular due to them not being able to use the [[Godpowder]] which usually fuels them. The alternative, [[Redpowder]], is quite unstable and uneconomic due to it sharing a root component with [[vitality]] [[Vitality Potion|potions]].
