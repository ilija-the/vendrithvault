A medical academy in the eastern [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] port city of [[Singsong]]. Constructed after the establishment of the [[Sanitorium]] and its anti-worship laws, the Singsong Medical Academy is quite rare in not having been built on a religious tradition or in a specific healer-deity's image. Its principles of teaching and helping were instead however based on Mogxan philosophy.

The academies' doctors and trainees were essential to helping the wounded during the [[Singsong Port Fire]], which earned it the trust of much of the citizenry. Despite this, the Academy is struggling for resources.
