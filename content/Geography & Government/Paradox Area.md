The paradox area is a mysteriously inhospitable area that may not too far away from [[Friedport]]. It supposedly causes [[Spellburn|Spellburning]] (a painful backlash when using spells), contains a vividly different flora from its surroundings, and has next to no local fauna. It is believed that Friedport authorities under the [[Horizan Guild Association]] have kept the area off-limits, and its exact whereabouts a secret.

The [[Newcomers of Friedport]] know this area to contain the lost [[Crusader Sects|cult]] village of [[Veridica]].
