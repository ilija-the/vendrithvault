Archangel, also known as "Archie" by his friends, was a minotaur involved in [[Sister Amelie Kenton|Amelie Kenton's]] refugee moving network. As a young and inexperienced humanitarian, he was placed to maintain [[Haven]] instead of the in-the-field missions once it was established.
