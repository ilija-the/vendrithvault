Materia is the material world, the world of Vendrith that surrounds one unless one travels to other [[Planes of Vendrith|Planes]]. In the [[Great Flow]] theory, the plane of [[Materia]] is believed to (roughly) lie at the centre of the flow, as it is characterised by a balance between matter and nothingness, pressure and free flow, light and darkness. 

ot just one of the [[Planes of Vendrith]] but the central one which most everything exists in.
