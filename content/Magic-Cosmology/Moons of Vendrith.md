Vendrith has three moons - Velf, Tritas and Kiya. They are named after the [[Sibling Dragons]], whom they are sometimes considered the still-looking eyes of.

In sparse [[Vilhun]] mythos, the three moons are also said to be how the [[Hun Lords]] scry on their people.
