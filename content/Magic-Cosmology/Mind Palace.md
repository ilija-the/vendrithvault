A strange [[Planes of Vendrith|Plane]] that [[Bande Aparte (DB)]] reached while [[Lykas]] communicated with him remotely. The Mind Palace appeared as a meteorite (floating rock) in space (an empty void with only the stars in the background) with the planet of [[Yond]] looming over even larger than it is seen from Materia. Contains a throne like that of Vestitur, which Bande Aparte first woke up chained to.

Also home to the coloured threads - a representation of the many mysteries of the Landcrab Plot as the investigator and his party attempted to unravel them all. In the end, all threads led to the Minotaur [[Saint Karvi Massiri|Karvi]].
