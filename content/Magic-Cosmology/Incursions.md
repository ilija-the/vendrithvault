Incidents involving the appearance of creatures thought only mythical in [[Ferweth]], usually in a violent way. They are seen as a manifestation of the myths becoming real through magic. Some speculate it may be connected to the [[Feydark]].

Known to be brought on by the [[Wyrm Knight]] of Ferweth.
