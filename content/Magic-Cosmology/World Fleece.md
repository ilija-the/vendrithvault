A great mysterious force of nature of Vendrith. Often considered the edges of the plane of [[Materia]]. Colloquially just called the fog.

Manifests more in some places than others. The fog exists in its main and most dangerous form out at the "edges of the world," such as out at sea,  known for struggling with the fog are [[Ferweth]] and [[Vilhun]], though the fog also once prevented the [[Discovery of Horiza]]. Reaching the [[Horiza]] is still an arduous journey because of the difficulties of safely navigating the fog passage.
