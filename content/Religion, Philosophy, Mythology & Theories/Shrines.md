Shrines are found in many forms over Vendrith, and act as sacred locations that venerate family, history, nature, [[Saint|saints]], [[Icon|icons]] and the like. Many shrines are known to grant [[Blessings|blessings]] to those who visit them. Break the shrine's anathema however, and one may be [[Curses|cursed]] instead.
