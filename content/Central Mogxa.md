Central Mogxa is one of the areas of [[Mogxa]], encompassing the settlements of [[Meizhine]], [[Pezhu]], [[Poli]]; and sometimes [[Cavalma]] depending on whether [[Steppe Mogxa]] is considered. It contains a decent amount of relatively flat lands compared to the otherwise extreme terrain of the continent, though is still home to a number of rolling hills, smaller cliffs, caves and smaller lakes.
