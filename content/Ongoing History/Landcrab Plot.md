The Landcrab Plot was a recent series of conspiracies, murders and rebellions with the aim of breaking [[Friedport]] free from the [[Horizan Guild Association]]. While the [[Landcrabs]] that supported the plot saw it as a way to regain their tribes and political power, the [[Desecrators]]were so heavily involved in the plot it is likely that it would've lead to [[Crusaders|Crusader]] dominance of the city rather than Landcrab liberation. Related is [[Operation Blackout]], a Crusader plot to separate Friedport from Sanitationist control that eventually morphed into the brewing landcrab rebellion.

Despite many efforts to uncover and stop the plot, the rebellion started during the [[Summer Games]]. However, likely unbeknownst to most who supported it, a large amount of fungus-bearing [[Golem|Golems]] were released upon the city to break the ranks of the guards. These golems seemingly slaughtered indiscriminately, however, and quickly turned the situation into one of terror. A magical sphere was put over the city by the [[Magus in the High Tower]], preventing any people or golems for leaving until the threat was contained.

The [[Newcomers of Friedport]], having just won the Summer Games, rapidly returned to the city to try to save it. Most visible of them was [[Cardinal Rackawn]], whom arrived early and made a spectacle of killing the golems.
