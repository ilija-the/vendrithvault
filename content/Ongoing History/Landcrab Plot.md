The Landcrab Plot was a recent series of conspiracies, murders and rebellions with the aim of breaking [[Friedport]] free from the [[Horizan Guild Association]]. While the [[Landcrabs]] that supported the plot saw it as a way to regain their tribes and political power, the [[Desecrators]]were so heavily involved in the plot it is likely that it would've lead to [[Crusaders|Crusader]] dominance of the city rather than Landcrab liberation. Related is [[Operation Blackout]], a Crusader plot to separate Friedport from Sanitationist control that eventually morphed into the brewing landcrab rebellion.
