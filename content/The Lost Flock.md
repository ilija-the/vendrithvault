The Lost Flock is a mythological lost tribe that separated from the [[Flock of Man]], refusing its leadership. It is difficult to confirm the veracity of the Lost Flock. If it did exist, then mentions of it appear to have been intentionally scrubbed and damaged. Some speculate this was done to erase the memory of rebellion or heresy.
