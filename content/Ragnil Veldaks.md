Wife of [[Valfrid Veldaks]]. Widely popular but not as frequently in the spotlight, she maintained a lot of sway over the [[Landcrabs]] of [[Friedport]] even after being widowed. Some called her the Shadow over Friedport referencing her informal control over the city.

Related to the [[Old Crone]], and is the likely mother of [[Mistress Crimtone]].
