Gunslinger, Sharpshooter, Marksman, all often refer to a specific kind of warrior who is an expert in ranged weaponry that isn't bows. Such weapons are most commonly [[Firearms]] and thus most associated people with them are [[Crusaders|The Crusaders]], but the term can also refer to other ranged ammunition-based weapons such as crossbows, though they will often not refer to themselves as any of the above terms.
