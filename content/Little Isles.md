The Little Isles are a set of islands off the southern coast of [[Mogxa|Imperial Mogxa]] which were once inhabited by the [[Draconic Empire]] and then [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa|Draconic Tribes]]. It has more recently been seized and settled by those more loyal to the [[Sanitorium]], which has caused conflict with the local [[Dragonbloods]]. The [[Red Gown Tribes]] have taken in many whom were forced to relocate from the Little Isles, and remain an ally whose threats make further escalation difficult.
