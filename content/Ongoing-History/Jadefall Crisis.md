A crisis in which a [[Sanitorium|Sanitationist]] (a philosopher of the [[Sanitorium]]) by the name [[z_Circles Re-Traced/Jade the Just]] controversially pushed to reduce or end the autonomy of [[Philosophia]] but was met with resistance, including armed resistance.

A retired knight of the roundtable of philosophers named [[Dimitri the Deadly]] was also implicated, causing problems for the otherwise reputable station of warriors.

Often compared to the earlier [[Adventurer Crisis]] and [[Tower Hostage Crisis]].
