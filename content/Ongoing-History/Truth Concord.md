The Truth Concord is an agreement that allows worshippers of the [[Daydreaming Truth]], a non-titanic deity, to join the [[Crusaders]]. It was signed by the [[Iron Lotus]] and a handful of important religious leaders of Truth. Still, many Crusaders remain suspicious of those who would worship something other than a titan, and only a limited amount of followers of Truth have actually joined forces with the Crusader Armies.

One prominent follower of Truth that did join the Crusaders is [[z_Other Campaigns/3.1 Landcrab Plot/Bande Aparte]].
