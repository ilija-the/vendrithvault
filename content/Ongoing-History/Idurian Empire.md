The Idurian Empire is a term used for the realm ruled by the conqueror that became known as [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]], spanning large parts of [[Canath]], [[The Rend]] and [[Mogxa]]. The Empire reached its most important milestone with the conquest of the [[Eastern Kobold Dynasty]] in 769 ADc. The Idurian Empire laid the foundations for the [[Languages|Idurian]] court language, and a number of standards, law, institutions and such shared across merchants, adventurers and governments. The island of [[Jed]] had a special role as a hub between the Canathi and Mogxan parts, and today remains a successor of the mixed culture of the empire.

The Idurian Empire ended unexpectantly with the abdication of Idur in 796 ADc. The region of Mogxa consequently entered the [[Mogxan Interregnum]].
