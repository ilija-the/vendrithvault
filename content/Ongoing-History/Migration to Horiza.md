### Initial Exploration & Settlement

These people brought Idurian as a common language. [[Friedport]] and most of the [[Chocolate Isles]] speaks it as such.

### First Wave of Migration

These peoples ended up developing the [[Bay Pidgin]] language, serving as the common language for commoners in outer (coastal) but mainland Horiza. Guilds and government still speaks Idurian.

### Second Wave of Migration

These peoples ended up developing the [[Harza Pidgin]] common language in the deeper parts of Horiza.
