The March of the Roses, or Dawn of Roses, was a [[Crusaders|Crusader]] operation that took advantage of [[Espionage Crisis|leaked intelligence from Mogxa]] to swiftly capture a covert [[Sanitorium]] camp that had been wreaking havoc from behind enemy lines. The capture of the camp also led to the imprisonment of [[Chaucer]], a Sanitationist that is infamous for a number of extremely brutal attacks on Crusaders and civilians alike.  Having been the first decisive victory for the Crusaders in a while (especially in the background of the recently disastrous [[March of the Corpse Flowers]]), it was hailed as the beginning of a new dawn of success and momentum. Unfortunately, Chaucer would end up going missing, likely escaping, from the [[Crusader Sects]] he was [[Veridica|hidden away at]].

Lieutenant [[Bande Aparte]] played an important role in this operation.
