The Mogxan Interregnum is a period that started with the abdication of [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]] from his [[Idurian Empire|throne]] in 796 ADc, causing the rather unexpected and sudden fracturing of his empire including most of Mogxa. The period is said to have come to an end with the Unification Proclamation of the [[Western Kobold Dynasty]] in 856 ADc.
