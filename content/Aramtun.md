Aramtun is a settlement in the centre of the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]], on the [[Celene]] coast. It is 'shielded' by the low-lying and wet basin to its north, which retains very little settlement and which armies cannot effectively pass through. Going more towards the eastern inland from it you can reach [[Tallface]], [[Fort Ferr]] and the [[Haldrine Halls]]. Following the road or coast west takes you to [[Shahale]].

Aramtun has for a while enjoyed the presence of the large [[Eyne]] of [[Leyn]] not far off its shores, an island that can either fly or float in the waters. Its leaders have an understanding and alliance with that of Aramtun, which is itself a player in Celene naval warfare and politics. Aramtun attempts to balance or remain out of the rivalry between [[Twinvale]] and [[Shargei's Landing]], but isn't always successful.
