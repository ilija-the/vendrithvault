A devastating urban fire in the city of [[Singsong]] that originated in the harbour district, hitting the homeless and poor labourers whom resided there especially hard. The [[Singsong Medical Academy]] was especially important in treating the wounded. Some have speculated that the brutal criminal gang-leaders that once dominated the city's underworld, the [[Iron Brothers]], were directly or indirectly responsible for the fire.
