*Often referred together with [[Monstrous Fungus]].*

Monstrous Plants are generally [carnivorous plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnivorous_plant) that have grown to large sizes either by magic or plain unstopped growth much like a tree is theoretically able to grow forever given the conditions. Identified non-carnivorous monstrous plants are few in number, presumably due to the required amount of nutrients and much lower risk of harm (at most being poisonous as a means of protection from being eaten) that makes such classification gray compared to plain wide-spread infestation of a single plant.

While rare to find in places with humanoid populations who are able to quickly cut down on a given plants spread, such plants are massive threats in jungles and uninhabited forests where they are able to spread unopposed.

Monstrous plants are generally mindless much like [[Construct|constructs]] or most [[Undead|undead]], only going off basic response stimuli, such as warmth, specific smell, or physical touch.
