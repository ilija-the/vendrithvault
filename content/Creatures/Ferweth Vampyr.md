Animalistic and horrifying creatures with a lust for blood and meat yet that are normally incapable of entering places uninvited. Blind, these Vampyr often have to rely on [[Vampyr Eyes]] (also known as Vampyr Bats) to locate their prey, which have a sort of symbiotic relationship with the Vampyr.
