A [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] given form through [[Druid]] rituals, leshies often serve as [[Familiars|familiars]] to their creators. Others traverse the world as spirits safeguarding nature and helping people who respect it, perhaps after their creators passed away. Leshies are thought to have immortal spirits, though they are not known to ever have been recreated with the exact same memories or personality as who they were before.
