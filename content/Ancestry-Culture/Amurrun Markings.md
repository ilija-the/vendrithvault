> [!important] Similar to...
> Not to be confused with [[Halfling Markings]], which are hieroglyphic markings that appear on halflings' skin throughout their lives representing important events they experienced.

Amurrun are born with complex magical glyphs (akin to tattoos that glow through their fur) that represent various items, buildings or even rituals. Many amurrun find it of utmost importance to create what their markings represent, such as by taking up a trade related to it. An amurrun with a building will likely become a mason or architect, whereas one with a fancy sword will likely become a smith or enchanter.
