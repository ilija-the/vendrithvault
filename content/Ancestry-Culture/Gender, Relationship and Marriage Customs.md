There's a range of views on gender, relationships and marriage customs across Vendrith, but there are some commonalities that extend across it.

Marriage and life-long (or indefinite) pairing with a partner exists in most cultures. The views on how mandatory this is to engage in romantic and sexual activity varies, as does standards of when it should happen. It's common to envision life as a 3 step path, first as alone

While monogamy is likely the most commonly practiced form of romantic relationship in Vendrith, the world has a long and deep history of polyamory and polygamy. Some cultures revere the practice of three-person-polyamory as a part of the [[Trinities]].

Gender as a social role matters to most culture's customs and expectations, but the ability to change ones gender is widely accepted so long as one adopts the customs of the new gender one takes on. While it varies in relevance, the concept of a 3rd Gender has existed and continues to exist in many cultures in Vendrith.
