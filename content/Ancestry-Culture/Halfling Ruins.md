Halfling Ruins is a term used for a series of ruins spread far across [[Iduria]] that have similar throughlines and seemingly come from the same culture. They're called Halfling Ruins due to being the location in which [[Halflings|halflings]] tend to be discovered, whom are found as rubber eggs that unfold only once they've been found and disturbed, usually by being taken. While the connection between the ruins and Halflings is not certain, most accept that they must have some connection, which also makes them the possible domain of [[Yond]] and the [[Daydreaming Truth]].

Halfling ruins are concentrated around [[Canath]], [[Talvas]] and [[Galae]], though some can also be found in [[Mogxa]]. A very few reach all the way to [[Fenrunr]] to the northeast.
