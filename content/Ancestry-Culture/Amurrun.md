The amurrun (also known as catfolk) are a [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] that appear like bipedal anthropomorphic cats, and are typically quite slim and tall. Most amurrun are of bright golden fur colours, there are also those of darker shades. The amurrun are famous for being the builder class of the [[Hightower Civilisation]] under the [[Elves]], and continue to be known as visionaries when it comes to [[Inventor|invention]]. This is aided by due to their being born with [[Amurrun Markings]], blueprints of various things the respective amurrun often spend their parts of their lives trying to pursue and create.
