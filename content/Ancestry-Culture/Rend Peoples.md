The Rend Peoples refers to the various cultures, small kingdoms, sailors and merchants that have long traversed the various coasts of [[The Rend]] and the [[Rubyfirth Bay]]. Through the Rend Peoples we find a connection of language, culture and ancestry between [[Nagonkor]], [[Talvas]], [[Veducia]] and southwestern Mogxa (and through Nagonkor, some influence into [[Galaekor]]).

The [[Gnomes]], [[Trip-kee]], [[Nagaji]], [[Anadi]], [[Rabbitfolk]] and [[Athamaru]] are among the ancestries often considered Rend Peoples. The [[Kholo]] were traditionally not considered one of the Rend Peoples, but have since in a way become a modern Rend People through their migrations northward from deeper [[Far World - Eydwin|Eydwin]] and Talvas.
