"Son of a witch" is an insult used in parts of Vendrith, due to stigma against [[Witch|witches]].
[[Trolls|Troll]], [[Drakes|Drake]], and other names for lesser and/or dumber monsters are used as insults

 [[Goblins#Island Goblins|Island Goblins]] are often called gobblers, gobble goblins, gobbillin goblins, and the like as an insult.
