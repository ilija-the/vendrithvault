Very WIP, mostly just ideas. Feel free to add your own suggestions.

[Impending] trait - doesn't take place immediately, though a dreadful feeling or signs of the upcoming fortune or misfortune may be felt. The GM saves this as a loaded gun until an appropriate moment. Comes with a Fortune or Misfortune trait implying whether its a positive or negative effect (and consequently, cannot happen at the same time).

[Token] trait - the recipient of this buff chooses when to use it. They can save it for as long as they'd like, but may lose it if they become cursed by the same deity.

## [[Saint|Saints]]
Saints can only provide a buff 1, or a curse 1 if their sanctified holy sites or protected spaces (such as their village) is desecrated by you in some way (such as breaking their tenets). Gaining the favour or ire of a saint dedicated to a deity may also incur the same from the deity. You may for example earn a buff 1 from a saint, and then the deity later recognises you and gives you a buff 1 and 2 as they recognise your already existing efforts (instead of starting at 1).
