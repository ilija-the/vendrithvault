Wizard is a [[magic|spellcaster]] categorization, where the spellcaster primarily uses known magical exploits to harness and redirect magic without much if any natural magical inclination (like a [[Sorcerer]]) or third party helping them (like a [[Cleric]]).

It is also a colloquial term for any spellcaster who happens to study magic.
