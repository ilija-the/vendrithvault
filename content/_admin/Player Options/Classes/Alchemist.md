> The text below is the description of the role and status of a certified Alchemist.

A professional Alchemist is a highly-educated master of alchemy, the art of combining elements and substances to create new items. Often hired in conjunction with professional [[Wizard|Wizards]], they are behind many noble houses, ones who are able to afford such personal assistants at least.

The role of a hired alchemist is often to aid with creation of any medicaments for the house members, as well as oft repairing and restoring old constructs. As such, trained alchemists are trained in a wide variety of medicinal and mechanical disciplines to fulfill their role.

This close proximity to upper echelons of power is also one of the few merit-based ways of gaining influence in the world, attracting many schemers who study alchemy to be able to influence the political world around them. This leads to stigma against professional alchemists as scheming servants, much like their magical counterpart. But so long there is demand, there will be people to fill that need.

There are few noteworthy academe that Alchemists are proud to hail from:
- [[Academie Peripherie Philosophia]] of Mogxia (Alchemy Wing)
- Alkahestral College of Vilhun
