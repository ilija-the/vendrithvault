Now, the scale of Vendrith has not been totally consistent, nor should we expect it to. There's a lot of hassle involved in keeping that exact, and the maps may essentially need certain adjustments to scale to make it work as best as possible for actual play.

However, we should still at least try to keep it within a certain range of itself.

![[Pasted image 20260709174316.png]]
This section of the Miro used to be the chief tool of scale, with each red square consisting of 500 square miles. However I have since decided this should be scaled down to 2/3rds, meaning each red square is 333.333333333 (continuing) miles (no, getting it to be a holy number was not intentional but it does seem fitting).

This means that the west to east end of Galae is about 750 miles.
