An event in which [[Saint Karvi Massiri]], at the time going under the name [[Krynn Meister]] was implicated in having leaked a number of state secrets of the [[Sanitorium]] to [[Crusaders]].

As a result he was forced to leave the [[Academie Peripherie Philosophia]] before being able to graduate. He left the family that had taken him in at the time to spare them from public flack and seek a new beginning.

Likened to the [[Adventurer Crisis]], [[Tower Hostage Crisis]] and [[Jadefall Crisis]]; the latter two of which both also happened in [[Philosophia]].
