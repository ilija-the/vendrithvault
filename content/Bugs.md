Bugs the Clown is a mentally disturbed [[Goblins|Goblin]] that has enjoyed life as a circus performer. He  in particular has a thing for eating coins. He usually wears a broken mouth-cage-like device. He was hired alongside [[Beau Gaux]] the Clown by a circus-owner in [[Friedport]], before it got taken over by [[Gator Grippli]]. As Gator set out to travel the world with [[Cardinal Rackawn]] after the conclusion of the [[Landcrab Plot]], Bugs went with them.

Both Bugs and Beau served as minor witnesses in the trial against [[Bande Aparte]].
