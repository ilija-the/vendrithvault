Bishop's Disciples refers to a group that stayed at the monastery of [[Bishop the Peacemaker]] in the [[Ivory Crown Plateau]] when he got kidnapped. After pursuing the kidnappers, revealed to be [[Desecrators]] lead by [[Cardinal Rackawn]]. The Disciples successfully stopped the desecrators from bombing a glacier that may have unleashed a flood upon [[Newshore]], but Bishop lost his life in the process.
