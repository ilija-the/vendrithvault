Egruff Ward is a kitsune supposedly found half-feral in the woods and raised by [[Chatwood]], becoming a crusader. Somewhat overweight and with white fur, Ward is usually dressed or armoured lightly, preferring not to cover himself up or weigh himself down too much. Despite his hampered intelligence, Ward has proven his physical prowess and aim. 

Around his late teens and early adulthood, he stayed in the secret [[Crusader Sects|cult]] town of [[Veridica]]. Here, experiments were being done by attempting to infuse Ward with energy from [[Leylines]], hoping to uncover the ability to realise [[Utopian Theory]]. The cult town is also said to have imprisoned [[Chaucer]], an infamous sanitationist that slaughtered many in the name of demoralising the crusader cause. One day, something went wrong and Ergruff Ward had to confront the cult leadership alongside the [[Fools of Veridica]]. The town was subsequently vanished, and only a very few are known to have made it out alive.

Resurfacing after the Veridica incident, Ward appeared without his previous ability to cast spells. He instead donned divine firearms and served as muscle, and occasionally, an assassin, for the crusaders.
