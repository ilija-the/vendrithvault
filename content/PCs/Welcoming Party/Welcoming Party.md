A group of migrants to [[Friedport]] that were recruited by [[The Lord]] to do a mission. The mission involved stealing back a [[Mysterious Iron Crate]] that had been taken from the Lord's associates by an unknown assailant, which the party had to identify. After investigation the crime scene, the party deduced the culprit as [[Nazaar Eviljaw]] (aided by his bodyguard dromaar). The Welcoming Party set off to Oozemaw Isle, a small island with a sea-accessible cave in which the mysterious iron crate was being traded to a masked individual. Nazaar was knocked out, the crate secured, and the welcoming party was given their reward from the Lord.

Unbeknownst to all in the welcoming party but Gen, the masked individual turned out to be the kobolds [[Odd]] and [[Chimney]] in a trenchcoat
