A group of cultists that were at [[Veridica]], together with the [[Warden of Home Base]], during the village's undoing. Together with the others the group kept the infamous sanitationist killer [[Chaucer]] imprisoned, and performed various experiments on [[Leylines]] as part of their [[Utopian Theory]]. The group is said to have confronted the [[Crusader Sects|sect's]] leaders, including [[Mask]], [[Dominus]], and [[Karnal]].
