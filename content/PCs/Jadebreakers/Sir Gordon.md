A [[Wereforged|wereforged]] of [[Philosophia]] that defected from [[The Physician]] on [[Backbreaker Island]], joining forces with the [[Jadebreakers]]. He became a popular icon of resistance against Jade the Just. As a wereforged, he had a particular interest in unravelling how the wereforged truly were made.
