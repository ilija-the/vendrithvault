A constantly smiling halfling follower of the [[Wheel of Death]]. As an Animist Seer, he's capable of seeing and helping communicate with lingering spirits and haunts of the world, a talent he trained at a Wheel of Death monastery. After about two years of training, he set out to go join the larger congregation of the faith in [[Longddawn]]. Has travelled [[Ferweth]] on the road to Longddawn as part of the [[So-Called Heroes]].
