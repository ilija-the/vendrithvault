A friendly, small and green trip-kee known for wearing a cowboy hat. As a summoner, he is usually accompanied by his alligator-like eidolon [[Schnappi]]. Raised by animals in the [[Veducia|Veducian Wilds]], he bonded with his eidolon to save it after its mother was killed by a trophy hunter - later revealed to be [[Gozi Windaji]] trophy hunting on the commission of [[The Fat Tiger]]. 

After leaving the jungle and traversing civilization, Gator took on small jobs for various villages and learned to speak Idurian whilst doing so. Gator Grippli eventually sought his fortunes in the new world, as he travelled to [[Friedport]]. Here he would meet [[Ashur Holdok]] and [[Bande Aparte]], with whom he formed the [[Newcomers of Friedport]]. 

Together with the newcomers Gator worked on uncovering the brewing [[Blackout Parade|Landcrab Plot]]. Gator played a leading role in having Ashur [[Reincarnation|reincarnated]] after 

Believing Bande Aparte to have been killed and his body stolen, Gator, [[Cardinal Rackawn]] and Ashur sought out and destroyed a Desecrator camp hidden near Friedport to get the corpse back. The [[Old Crone]] as well as the lingering spirit of [[Auldwell Fareawel|Auldwell]] were put to their ends here, but not before the Newcomers were nearly almost all killed. In a desperate bid to save his friends, Gator Grippli made a [[Devil Deal]] with the [[Good Doctor]], and managed to turn the tide around.

As a result of the devil deal made, Schnappi was threatened by also having its soul claimed by the Good Doctor. Not wanting to let this happen, Gator eventually bid his lifelong companion goodbye and released Schnappi as his eidolon.

Gator is nowadays believed to be travelling with [[Cardinal Rackawn]] and [[Princess Ionelle]], as well as [[Mistress Crimtone]].
