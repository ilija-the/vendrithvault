A well-known rabbitfolk inventor, and the adoptive son of the equally famous inventor [[Borgrumm Holdok]]. After he was framed for Borgrumm's murder he fled the city of [[Cragmoore]] with the aid of [[Antoine Dumas]] and [[Sister Amelie Kenton|Amelie Kenton]], eventually safely reaching [[Friedport]]. Here he met [[Bande Aparte]] and [[Gator Grippli]] with whom he formed the [[Newcomers of Friedport]], together with which he'd eventually go on to save the city from the [[Blackout Parade|Landcrab Plot]] with. In the process, he won the [[Summer Games]] and earned the title [[Herō of Friedport]].
