A powerful figure that appears akin to a tiger-like [[Amurrun]] (though it is uncertain if he is one), that is most known for his collection of historical, magical and otherwise prestigious artefacts. He is of quite a bit of influence in [[Talvas]], in which he has multiple times been appointed the [[Rajistrate]] of [[Rraka]], and he has a palace to go with said title. His mansions are known to be gaudy and full of symbols of his endless wealth and curiosity for artefacts.

![[Fat_Tiger_Lastsummer.png]]

His reach also stretches to surrounding regions such as [[Rubyfirth Bay]] and [[Veducia]], which he's been known to hire people from and occasionally travel to. One person he has hired is [[Gozi Windaji]], which he asked to hunt for trophies in Veducia. Gozi, succeeding in his task, gravely affected the life of [[Gator Grippli]].

The Fat Tiger would eventually appear travelling to [[Friedport]], during which he was almost robbed by the [[Queen of Grog]] but narrowly saved by [[Magnus Strike]] and [[Roslyn Chatkur]]. Impressed, the Fat Tiger ended up hiring Roslyn to help him track down the [[Vestitur]]. Initially unbeknownst to her, the Fat Tiger already had two agents working for him towards this purpose, [[Vendy Vendetta Rapidly]] and Gozi Windaji.
