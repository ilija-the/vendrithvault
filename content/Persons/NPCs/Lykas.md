Lykas is a former [[Crusaders|crusader]] soldier that once served [[Bande Aparte]] and [[General Architrave]]. After nearly being assassinated by the General (sustaining a massive injury to his throat in the process), Lykas went underground. Together with other former soldiers of Bande Aparte, Lykas would recover and found the [[Moonless Night]] - eventually succeeding in bringing the General to justice.
