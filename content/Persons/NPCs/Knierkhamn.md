Knierkhamn is a [[Gnomes|gnome]] deep sea [[Diving|diver]] and explorer of underground ruins, shipwrecks and nature - almost always seen in her diving suit. Her life is absorbed by her interest, and to outsiders it may seem she's very cold about her colleagues whom have lost their lives doing this very dangerous profession. She is however sociable, and always interested in educating or learning about the subject from others. She sells equipment and artefacts to fund her expeditions. She resides in [[Horiza|Horiza]], but has previously dived in the [[Sea of Sargathis|Sargathian Sea]].
