A group of 4 brothers that were nearly inseparable as they ruled the underworld of [[Singsong]] with an iron fist. It is speculated they may have had a hand in causing the [[Singsong Port Fire]].

![[TheBrothers.png]]

Later, they went to [[Horiza|Horiza]] and were recruited by the [[Mariners]] of [[Friedport]] under [[Kiam Lockneed|Kiam Lockneed]], likely due to their ferocious reputation and ability to instil discipline in their subjects.

Their sister, the [[Queen of Grog]], had long since disowned the brothers. Not long after learning of their presence in her [[Piracy|pirate]] waters, the Iron Brothers were beaten by [[Roslyn Chatkur]] and the [[Newcomers of Friedport]], and delivered to the Queen for punishment. Taught a lesson and forcefully separated to various parts of the world where they could repay their 'debts' with labour, the Iron Brothers are unlikely to ever reunite.
