Since the disappearance of the beloved King of [[Kingdom of Vestitur|Vestitur-Riverside]], a [[Goblins|Goblin]] relative of his with little prestige has been thrust into the role of Prince-Regent. With a supposedly eccentric personality and few interests beyond that of [[Theatre]], he has little popular support and discontent is brewing.
