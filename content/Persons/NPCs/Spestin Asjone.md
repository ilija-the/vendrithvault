Miss Spestin Asjone is a noble woman with aspirations to become an explorer, and has sought out tutors and high-quality equipment and planning to do so. She attended the [[Riseagain Exhibition]] when [[Mag Gafn]] was killed. In the aftermath she delayed her plans for going on an adventure and instead bought a flower shop.
