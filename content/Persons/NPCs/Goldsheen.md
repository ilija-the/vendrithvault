Goldsheen was a former crusader turned rogue that worked with [[Bande Aparte]] as part of a heist to steal the [[Vestitur]] from a [[Kallea|Kallean]] collector. Goldsheen died mysteriously after reaching the room with the safe containing the relics, likely to poison. He never got to experience Bande Aparte being betrayed by the supposed buyer of the relics, [[Auldwell Fareawel|Perckle d'Rits]].

Goldsheen's body was eventually recovered by the [[Moonless Night]] and given proper burial.
