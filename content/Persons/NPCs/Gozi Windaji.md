Gozi Windaji is a human [[Ranger|ranger]] whom is experienced at trophy-hunting exotic [[Animals|animals]]. From the [[Talvas]]-[[Veducia|Veducian]] border, he today roams [[Horiza]].

Gozi was formerly a [[Druid]] in-training when living in his birthplace. It was during a famine he decided to abandon his druidism and hunt animals for his village, before then also becoming a thief-for-hire to pay for their continued well-being. He would end up stuck in a contract under [[The Fat Tiger]] together with [[Vendy Vendetta Rapidly|Vendetta Rapidly]], whom he bonded with as a partner-in-crime. He appreciated her smarts with city life, while she appreciated his smarts with the wilderness. Their [[Slavery|servant]] position under the Fat Tiger took them to Horiza when he wanted them to capture the [[Vestitu]]
