Karnal was a council member of [[Veridica]]. He was one for life's simple pleasures as well as unquestioned power, but was not necessarily so intelligent as to be able to play power games on his own very well. He relied on [[Mask]], whom he supported as a loyalist.

He once had a wife at the cult, but she died, was buried, and forgotten about.
