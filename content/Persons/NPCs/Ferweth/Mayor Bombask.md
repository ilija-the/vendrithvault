Mayor Bombask is the former mayor of [[Cocatun]] in [[Ferweth]], whom died to an [[Incursions|incursion]] and fire brought on by the [[Wyrm Knight]] while protecting a child. [[Malachai]] has since taken over from him. He cared a lot for [[Boubo]] and [[Kicky]].
