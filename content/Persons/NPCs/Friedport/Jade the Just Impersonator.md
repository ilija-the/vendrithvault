Al Justin, a fan and impersonator of [[Jade the Just (DB)]], roams the [[Friedport]] party streets of [[Crabraven]] in his costume. He has extremely poor eyesight, and is totally reliant on his spectacles to see. He played a role in the trial [[HGA v. Bande Aparte]], in which he served as a witness based on his erroneous perception of events while without his glasses.
