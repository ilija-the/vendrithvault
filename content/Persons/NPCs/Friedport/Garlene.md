Garlene is the [[Medusa|Medusan]] leader of the [[Quietgraves|Quietgrave]] [[Landcrabs]]. As a [[Monsters of Vendrith|monster]] that is feared by most, her leadership has kept the Quietgraves from being bothered by others - as is their goal. Her and the Quietgrave's background is deeply tied to early [[Friedport]] history, as well as that of [[Carlos Valenci]] and the [[Reedmother]].

She appreciates those that can follow through on their goals despite their fear.
