Falos Bikbeak is a [[G'Mayun]] [[Friedport|Friedporter]], who usually works as a waiter at [[Merrick Auver Julian|Merrick's]] tavern - the Merry Clove. He also participates in brawling in his spare time, and has fought in the tournaments of [[Develyn Barbie]] amongst others. Of rather short statue, he likes to punch above his weight and prove his physical and charismatic achievements.

He's been known to hit it off with [[Ashur Holdok]] and [[Magnus Strike]].
