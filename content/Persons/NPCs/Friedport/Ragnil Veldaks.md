Ragnil Veldaks is a [[Kholo]] matriarch from a once-influental dynasty, that attempted to establish a glorious house through her union with [[Valfrid Veldaks]] (whom took her last name). Even after her husband, the [[Herō of Friedport]], died, Ragnil maintained much sway over the city. She was sometimes called the "Shadow Over Friedport," in reference to her ability to influence the city's affairs from the shadows of her manor.
