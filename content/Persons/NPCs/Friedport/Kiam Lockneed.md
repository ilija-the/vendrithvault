Kiam Lockneed is an influental [[Dragonblood]] noble that built his wealth and network in [[New World - Horiza|Horiza]]. He served as the Harbourmaster of [[Friedport]] for decades, and briefly as the Governor of the city after the abdication of [[Captain Dobi]]. 

Kiam Lockneed's origins are not well known, though he began to appear in the ranks of the [[Horizan Guild Association]] while stationed in [[Singsong]].

Kiam Lockneed was also often seen perusing the items of [[Auldwell Fareawel]]'s auctionhouse, whom would eventually shut down after its owner's fall from grace.

As Harbourmaster, Kiam Lockneed founded and lead a personal guard of elite warriors called the [[Mariners]]. The Mariners were especially trained in amphibious and coastal battle. A well exercised and dextrous swimmer, Kiam Lockneed also personally instructed the Mariners in swimming technique, as well as other (primarily beach-friendly) sports. While he owned a small coastal manor in [[Portaupearl]], he was rumoured to own (potentially multiple) beachside resorts around the [[Chocolate Isles]].

Kiam Lockneed also had an interest in fine arts and theatre. After serving as the Harbourmaster Kiam had at one point built upon enough personal wealth to fund the creation of an Operahouse in Portaupearl, to the surprise of many. Perhaps to repair the corrupt reputation he had earned, Kiam Lockneed funded the creation of an orphanage in Friedport, which produced a number of theatre performers.
