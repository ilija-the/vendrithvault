Vialstone is the Principal of [[Harbourtower Academy]], a small mage's school in [[Portaupearl]], [[Friedport]]. He is a [[Dwarves|dwarf]] and a wizard, and is interested in the overlap of use of magic and [[Engineering]] (see [[Artifice]]). He cares much for safety, especially that of his students. Using a safe-room he had constructed out of an abundance of caution, he kept all his students alive during the golem attacks of the [[Landcrab Plot]].
