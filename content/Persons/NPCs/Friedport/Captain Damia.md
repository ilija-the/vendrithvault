Captain Damia was a [[Ysoki]] of eastern [[Mogxa]] origin, which migrated to and became a leading guard and political figure in [[Friedport]]. Here, she cultivated a "family" of guards helping keep the city under control which she named her [[Patrollers]]. With this power base behind her, she eventually earned a spot on the council of three ruling Friedport, together with [[Kiam Lockneed]] and [[Captain Kard]]. She briefly served as the city's governor, before stepping down in favour of Kiam after a political plot.

Damia was also the secret half-sister of [[Ragnil Veldaks]], the widowed wife of the former Hero of Friedport. Knowing her sister (and not being all too dissimilar herself), Damia long suspected Ragnil was manipulating the city as the "Shadow Over Friedport." Damia fought to keep her sister in check and prevent the rise of a rebellious [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] populace, fearing disaster if the city's institutions were to collapse or end up in Ragnil's hands.
