Captain Damia was a [[Ysoki]] of eastern [[Mogxa]] origin, which migrated to and became a leading guard and political figure in [[Friedport]]. Here, she cultivated a "family" of guards helping keep the city under control which she named her [[Patrollers]]. These Patrollers were not entirely committed to the law, acting much like a mafia at times, but valued community and stability over all else. With this power base behind her, Damia eventually earned a spot on the council of three ruling Friedport, together with [[Kiam Lockneed]] and [[Captain Kard]]. She briefly served as the city's governor, before stepping down in favour of Kiam.

![[Damia_DrinkOfPower_1.0.png]]

During her later life, she would (surprisingly) entrust much of importance to a relatively new recruit in the Patrollers - [[Captain Dobi]]. Some suspect this is because Damia no longer trusted her own force, and/or that Dobi reminded her of her younger self. Dobi would go on to play an important role in the reforms to Friedport following the conclusion of the [[Landcrab Plot]] and ousting of Kiam Lockneed.

Captain Damia lost her life during the landcrab rebellion and golem attacks on Friedport.

Damia was also the secret half-sister of [[Ragnil Veldaks]], the widowed wife of the former Hero of Friedport. Knowing her sister (and not being all too dissimilar herself), Damia long suspected Ragnil was manipulating the city as the "Shadow Over Friedport." One such example was the verdict of the [[Titanaurk Kaesalver|trial against]] [[Bande Aparte]], which nearly contradicted the evidence shown if not for the timely intervention of Dobi. Damia fought to keep her sister in check and prevent the rise of a rebellious [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] populace, fearing disaster if the city's institutions were to collapse or end up in Ragnil's hands.
