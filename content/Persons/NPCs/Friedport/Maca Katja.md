Maca Katja is a spellcaster with abilities of necromancy and summoning. In her youth she was part of an [[Adventurers|adventurer]] party with [[Varvevan]], [[Xvavos]] and [[Zcaldon]] where they all made a name for themselves and learned most of what they know. She was dropped from this party after she contracted [[Lycantrophy]], causing especial heartbreak due to her romantic ties with Zcaldon, whom also left. Since this she plotted a revenge against the party, and especially Zcaldon, but was stopped by the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] and managed to repair relations with them. Today she fights with the party once more, usually to defend and solve problems in [[Friedport]].
