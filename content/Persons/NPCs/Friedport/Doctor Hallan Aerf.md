Hallan Aerf was a highly accomplished doctor that graduated from [[Singsong]], well known for the generous help he often gave for little to no pay. He moved to [[Friedport]], where he started up a clinic. His clinic was raided by the [[Patrollers]] once, after which he sold it off.

![[Hallan_in_Tunnels.png]]

A trial was brought against Hallan Aerf by the Patrollers after a series of murders were traced to him, and he was publicly hanged.

The Hallan Aerf that was hanged by the Patrollers was really just a body constructed from various [[Animals|animals]] and [[Monsters of Vendrith|Monsters]], with his head seemingly stitched on.

The secret half-brother of [[General Architrave]], Hallan was really Hallan Aerf Architrave. He was trusted enough by the General that, when he seemed to realise he was going to be punished for his crimes, he entrusted [[Operation Blackout]] to his brother to oversee. Unfortunately for him, the plan would really end up being co-opted by [[Auldwell Fareawel]], and then by [[Saint Karvi Massiri|The Scurvymaster]].
