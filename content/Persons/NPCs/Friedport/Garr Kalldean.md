Garr Kaldean is a homeless [[Friedport|Friedporter]] that wanders the streets and reads people's fortunes. He appears to lose his short term memory after doing so. Some remember him being part of the [[Little Eyes]] of Friedport, an organisation of scouts and spies for hire nowadays mostly consisting of [[Halflings|halflings]].
