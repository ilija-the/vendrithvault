Davian is a [[Friedport|Friedporter]] apothecary, and newly, doctor. He is the widower of the late [[Melda]], a veterinarian Davian greatly admired for her care for animals, inspiring his interest in [[Medicine]] to begin with. Initially educated as an apothecary and working at [[Amberhole]], he later got further medical training under [[Doctor Hallan Aerf|Doctor Hallan Aerf]], until he got revealed as a monster.

Davian worked with the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] to keep [[Develyn Barbie]] alive in her comatose state. Once [[Roslyn Chatkur]] entered the scene, the two cooperated to plan for a surgery to reattach a new arm in the hopes of awakening her. Roslyn's surgery was a success, and with some further recovery time under Davian's watch, Barbie made a near-full recovery.
