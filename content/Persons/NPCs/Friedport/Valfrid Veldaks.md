Valfrid was an [[Amurrun]] [[Adventurers|adventurer]] that made a name for himself, especially as he became known as "The [[Herō of Friedport]]." In [[Friedport]], he became an icon of the [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] and [[Horizan Guild Association|guildsmen]] alike. He would marry [[Ragnil Veldaks]], and take her last name as he did.
