Valfrid was an [[Amurrun]] [[Adventurers|adventurer]] that made a name for himself, especially as he became known as "The [[Herō of Friedport]]." In [[Friedport]], he became an icon of the [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] and [[Horizan Guild Association|guildsmen]] alike. He would marry [[Ragnil Veldaks]], and take her last name as he did. One famous adventure of his resulted in a Cloud [[Dragons|Dragon]] swearing its eternal loyalty to his house (see [[Veldaks Dragon]]), becoming a guardian of their manor on [[Everdry Isle]]. 

As an amurrun, Valfrid had [[Amurrun Markings]] of an enormous wooden tower on his back. The city would eventually construct this tower in his honour. [[Mag Gafn]] had taken credit for its architecture, but was revealed as a fraud.

He was known for wielding a [[Firearms|firearm]] powered or enhanced by a fire [[Elementals|elemental]] (which would later end up in the hands of [[The Prince]], whom claims to be his bastard child).
