Zcaldon is a gnome spellcaster which together with [[Varvevan]] and [[Xvavos]] formed a band of heroes beloved for helping those in need in [[New World - Horiza|Horiza]]. Another fourth member, [[Maca Katja]], had once been part of the group and romantically involved with Zcaldon, but was abandoned after it was revealed she suffered from [[Lycantrophy]]. They would eventually face eachother again as Maca sought revenge, but after being stopped by the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] the two eventually made up and she re-joined the party of heroes.

Zcaldon suffers from insomnia, and a form of narcolepsy brought on by it. While asleep, Zcaldon's subconscious can activate a form of
