The Veldaks Dragon is a cloud [[Dragons|Dragon]] that swore to serve the Veldaks family of [[Valfrid Veldaks]] and [[Ragnil Veldaks]], after the former met and did something for or against him in an adventure once. He introduced the [[Summer Games]] of Friedport directly preceding the [[Landcrab Plot]]. The dragon is fiercely protective of their manor, family members and legacy - though has now been left with little purpose after the couple died heirless (as far as anyone knows).  

[[The Prince]] claims to be the bastard child of Valfrid, which may either irritate the dragon or lead him towards serving him as the last remnant of the dynasty.
