Develyn Barbie is a former competitive brawler and sex worker in [[Friedport]], now retired to work a farm she bought with her savings.

During the [[Landcrab Plot|Landcrab Plot]], Barbie faced off against [[Gator Grippli]] and [[Louen Leonceur]] of the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] in a training tournament preparing for the eventual [[Summer Games]]. In it, she lost a match for the first time in a long while, earning the newcomers her respect. 

As Barbie went to retrieve the prize money from her secure stash she was assaulted by a mysterious assailant - later revealed to be the [[Saint Karvi Massiri|Scurvymaster]] and Doctor [[Doctor Hallan Aerf]]. Inside, before the Newcomers realised what was happening, Barbie was pinned down and her arm surgically removed before she eventually fainted from the pain - the Scurvymaster would go on to use this arm as a replacement for his long lost left-arm. As the Newcomers entered and found her unconscious, they failed to chase after the Scurvymaster or fully identify him.

The Newcomers moved Barbie's unconscious self to trusted friends, first [[Desmet Gaius]], then the apothecary and doctor-in-training [[Davian]]. Barbie was diagnosed to be unlikely to wake up after the many nerves expertly removed from her, but was kept alive in the hopes of bringing her back. After a long coma, the Doctor [[Roslyn Chatkur]] entered the picture and dared to try bring her back with surgery. Securing a replacement arm (from [[Carlos Valenci]]'s grave), she attached a new arm to Barbie's stub and nerves. Barbie went on to awaken in periodic short bursts soon thereafter, and would eventually make a near-full recovery.
