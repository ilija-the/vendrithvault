Captain Kard was a [[G'Mayun]] soldier turned general, whom eventually commanded [[The Starfish]] of [[Friedport]], and for a long time served as its governor. An injured veteran and A bit of a recluse, even as an incredibly important public figure, Kard often spent his time retreating away to go fishing on his own. He instead delegated much to his subordinates, including the young but ambitious [[Lieutenant Aeries]]. Still, he was known to be careful, and a popular myth went around that he was so confident in his governorship because he both had dirt on [[Kiam Lockneed]] and [[Captain Damia]], as well as a Dead Man's Switch.
