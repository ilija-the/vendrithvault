Tunnels Keysman was an illegal occupant of [[The Starfish]] that had stolen and carried around a set of most, if not all, of the keys to its many underground tunnels and rooms. He lost all his purpose as [[z_Other Campaigns/3.1 Landcrab Plot/Bande Aparte]] stole them from him on their first visit, however.
