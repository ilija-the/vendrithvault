Redd is a councilman of the [[Horizan Guild Association]] and [[Crafting|crafter]] in [[Friedport]]. As a veteran of [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] wars, he has particular experience working with the explosive [[Redpowder]] (often used in fireworks or as a replacement for [[Godpowder]]), specialised crossbows, and with commanding [[Navy|ships]]. A paraplegia, he moves around with self-crafted custom wheelchairs fit for various purposes.

Having developed a contempt for elitist generals, he had a grudge with the Friedport system before the Landcrabs rebelled. He Redd in particular had history with [[General Kard|Captain Kard]]. Together with [[Ashur Holdok]] and [[Captain Dobi]], he played a part in reforming the city's system.
