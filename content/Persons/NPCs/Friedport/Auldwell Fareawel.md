## Head of the Blackout Crusaders
> [!important] Secrets ahead!
> While anyone is free to read what's ahead, everything else in this article is privileged knowledge only known to a few specific people in the world of Vendrith. Take particular caution to not assume your character knows these things, or avoid it entirely if you're worried about keeping your own and your characters' knowledge separate!

Auldwell Fareawel grew up as Aldting Fritz d'Yumur, part of the extended royal family of the [[Yumuri Kingdom]] of western [[Mogxa]]. His family had amassed wealth, as well as a number of invaluable artefacts, over the years while retreating from politics after the main lands of [[Yumur]] were handed over to the [[Sanitorium]]. Young Fritz took an interest in the various artefacts the family had, but resented his family otherwise - especially around how they self-censored themselves and him regarding discussing the artefact's connections to [[Titans]] and [[Religion]]. After a ferocious [[Crusader Pirates|Crusader Pirate]] ship supposedly had raided a nearby monastery-turned-lookout fortress, Fritz planned to run away. He collected a number of artefacts and left his family for good, finding where the crusaders had docked in the darkness and threw them a bag of gold, silver and religious artefacts while asking to join.
