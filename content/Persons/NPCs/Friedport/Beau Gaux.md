Beau Gaux is a grizzled clown that acted alongside [[Bugs|Bugs the Clown]] at a circus act in [[Friedport]]. This circus was taken over by [[Gator Grippli]], but it was left to Beau as Gator and Bugs left to travel. Beau's past is shrouded in mystery, but whatever it is, it has given him a  deep need to smoke to calm his nerves.

Beau and Bugs served as witnesses in the trial against [[Bande Aparte (DB)]].
