Foreman Garlos is an [[Elves|Elf]] that has a great adoration for both large scale [[Crafting|engineering]] as well as small-scale tinkering. He has worked as the foreman of the [[Amberhole]] quarry and mining operations (employed by the [[Horizan Guild Association|HGA]]), and on his own time, collects and appreciates rare [[Firearms]]. He is quite unlike other elves in how much he partakes in 'rough' labour, due to his interests.
