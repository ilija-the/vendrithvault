Hiries is a secretary and behind-the-scenes mover and shaker of the [[Horizan Guild Association]]. She is known to take her work very seriously, and ensures people get things done, and in the right way at that. Some equate her to a sort of "shadow-Viceroy" of the [[New World - Horiza|Horizan]] cities.
