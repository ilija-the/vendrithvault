Lima Limestone is an [[Underworld|underworld]] [[Orcs|Orc]] and [[Athletics|triathlete]] that has worked both innocent jobs such as [[Ferries|ferrying]], as well as security for [[Piracy|pirates]] and [[Banditry|bandits]]. She has a quiet demeanour, not speaking much, but the words she does speak come with much weight. She tries to keep people she thinks couldn't survive the underworld out of it. She is an excellent swimmer, and wears a specially woven swimsuit at most times. She even has a pet manta ray.

She was last seen working security for [[Nazaar Eviljaw]] during an artefact trade of his, but this was broken up and stopped by the [[Welcoming Party]].
