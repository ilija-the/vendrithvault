A small but fearsome kobold [[Banditry|pirate]] that sails [[Friedport]] and [[Horiza|Horiza]].  He usually wears an oversized trench coat, hiding his one arm that's been replaced with an arm cannon. On his back he also wears a [[Inventor|contraption]] that looks like a ship's wheel with an assortment of [[Firearms|barrels]] sticking out of it, which all fire in a rapid volley when the wheel is spun.

![[f0336b1f-95d1-4c04-9df3-70c4c74dfd82_2 (3).png | center]]

Nazaar is known to have worked with [[Lima Limestone]] and to have been bested by the [[Welcoming Party]] whilst trying to sell a [[Mysterious Iron Crate]] he stole from [[The Lord]].
