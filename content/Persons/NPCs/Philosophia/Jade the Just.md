Jade the Just was a highly popular and simultaneously controversial [[Sanitorium|Sanitationist]] of the [[Philosophers of the Round|the Round]], whom made it his life's work to tear down the [[Guilds of Philosophia]] and integrated [[Philosophia]] directly under the empire's rule. He had a background in [[Law]], where he'd earned his nickname, possibly sarcastically but co-opted for propagandistic purposes nonetheless.

He worked with the retired Knight [[Dimitri the Deadly]] to do so. During this work, he oversaw the creation of the [[Wereforged]] project, which created a Philosophian make of [[Automatons]]. Unbeknownst to most at the time, he also cracked down hard on crime, even of the most petty variety, in order to gather more prisoners whose souls could power the automaton cores.
