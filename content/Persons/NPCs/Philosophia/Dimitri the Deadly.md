Dimitri is formerly one of the [[Knights of the Round]] of the [[Sanitorium]]. He was known for his brutally effective leadership in putting down a Crusader invasion, earning him the nickname "the Deadly." He retired with honours, but then chose to get involved with [[Premier Force]], [[Jade the Just]] and the [[Wereforged]] project - before then being embroiled in the [[Jadefall Crisis]].
