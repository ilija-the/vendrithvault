A [[Hobgoblins|hobgoblin]] general of the [[Crusaders]] that helped oversee recent moves in [[Nagonkor]] and [[Galae]]. He was ultimately tried for massive crimes of corruption and sentenced to death by hanging. His crimes were secretly brought to light by the [[Moonless Night]].

Before his death the general put together the plans for [[Operation Blackout]], sending his half-brother [[Doctor Hallan Aerf]] to oversee the mission's success in his absence. This plan was ultimately thwarted by [[Bande Aparte]], a former subordinate of his he punished for almost uncovering his corruption. Likewise, the Moonless Night was lead by [[Lykas]], a trusted companion of Bande Aparte that Architrave thought he had had assassinated.
