Mask was a council member of [[Veridica]], and essentially its unofficial leader. 

 >[!important] Secrets ahead!
> While anyone is free to read what's ahead, everything else in this article is privileged knowledge only known to a few specific people in the world of Vendrith. Take particular caution to not assume your character knows these things, or avoid it entirely if you're worried about keeping your own and your characters' knowledge separate!

When Veridica was going under, Mask pushed to use [[Leylines|Leyline]] infusions to self-destruct and take the attackers down with them. While he initially had the backing of his most loyal councillors [[Dominus]] and [[Karnal]], the mood turned against them as the [[Fools of Veridica]] argued against it.
