An alligator-like [[Eidolon]] whom was the lifelong friend, companion and summon of [[Gator Grippli]] until they parted ways. He was known to scare citizens that didn't expect to see an alligator waddling in, and enjoyed chomping on bad guys as well as bathing in and drinking swampy water.
