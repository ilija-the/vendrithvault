An [[animated skeleton]] summoner of the [[Fools of Veridica]], joined by their eidolon companion [[Weatherman]]. Their current whereabouts or status are unknown.

> Character Sheet Biography:
> Born on the day of a solar eclipse; followed by the abandonment of the holy Gods, Wick's birth was seen as a bad omen to their village of Gnolls. Because of over-protective parents, they weren't banished, but still grew up without being allowed to play with other kids their age (only in secret).
>
   One day, a demonic presence attacks the town, hurting or killing many in the process; except Wick, who was left unscathed. This was because they were hiding well, but the other townsfolk assumed it was because Wick was the eclipseborn and on the side of evil. When everyone else abandoned them, the demonic presence took pity on Wick- or simply found their situation amusing, he let them live. He asked Wick if they should team up and get revenge on his kin who left him to die, but they turn it down. Intrigued, the demon still follows Wick on throughout their life. Afraid of causing more hurt,
>
   Wick lives the rest of their life alone (besides their demonic companion they didn't ask for). When faced with death, Wick feels ready to go, but the demon companion isn't done with them- thus cursing them with eternal undeath.
>
   They keep trying to stay out of other people's way to not cause harm, but eventually stumbles upon a group seemingly in need of people. When Wick isn't faced with disgust over their undead appereance, they feel inclined to help out. Now they've lived with this group since early days, and takes on jobs others often don't want; moving corpses, tending to very ill people, and cleaning.
