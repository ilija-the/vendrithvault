Valeira was once an aiuvarin urchin of [[Philosophia]], later adopted by the famous [[Necromancer|necromancer]] [[Paeul]]. She learned of his necromantic powers despite his attempts to keep her at arm's length from it. Valeira is known to have been imprisoned on [[Backbreaker Island]], where she co-founded the [[Jadebreakers]].
