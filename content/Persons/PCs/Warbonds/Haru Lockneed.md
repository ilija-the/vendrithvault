A brine Dragonblood [[Commander]], brother of [[Sora Lockneed]], Haru commands the citizens militia of [[Kantby]], having taken that role from [[Hanya]]. Intelligent and charismatic, he inspires confidence in many, although his near-dragon level ego can be grating to others. He also sports incredibly tough scales and a great affinity for swimming, hunting fish recreationally.

He has a penchant for shouting "HARUUU" whenever succeeding at something.
