A rescue party created by [[Sora Lockneed]] to save the [[Regent's Legation]] from a failed raid on the opposing army's camp. Composed of commander [[Haru Lockneed]], [[Hanya]] the champion, and three previously caught mercenaries, [[Din & Degu]] and [[Zahia Lodi]].
