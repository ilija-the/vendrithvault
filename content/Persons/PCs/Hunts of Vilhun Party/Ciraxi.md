> A Strix blessed with the animalistic form of a sparrow, from a highly isolated and rather obscure tribe who all bear similar gifts. She's been sent by her tribe to make some kind of first contact and observe the outside world in order to make a decision that will see her tribe either slowly try to join the diplomatic world or hide even further from it.

A member of the [[Regent's Legation]], she is a *[[Beastkin]] [[Strix]] Thief/Scout [[Rogue]]*

Her drives are (loosely):
- Improving Diplomatic Relations
- Scouting out the land
