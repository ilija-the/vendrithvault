A kobold fey-blood sorcerer that freaks onlookers out as he talks to his [[Occult|occult]] dolls, which he calls his "friends." Well-versed with fey-like trickery such as mental manipulation, and capable of keeping a secret if need be. Despite this, Minky is rather friendly and open-minded to those who do dismiss him as crazy.

Has travelled [[Ferweth]] on the road to [[Longdawn]] with the [[So-Called Heroes]].
