A group of adventurers that have travelled [[Ferweth]], all seeking to reach [[Longdawn]] for various reasons. Known for having aided the settlements of [[Cocatun]] and [[Vanderkact]] in their times of need.

Consists of:
[[Minky]], a kobold fey-blood sorcerer.
[[Sheppie]], a halfling seer animist.
[[Cantro]], a dragonblood cold-blooded barbarian.
[[Kai]], an amurrun investigator.
[[Valentine Volmari|Valentine]], an eerie shoony rogue.

Has also been accompanied on their journey by:
[[Captain Doherty]] (totally spelled Dorothy)
[[Kelvin]] the Gnome Crafter (whose name Ilija forgot)
