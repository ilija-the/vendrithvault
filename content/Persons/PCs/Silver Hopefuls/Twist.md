A leshy swashbuckler from an [[Canath|Encanathi]] village producing abundant amounts of grains. After his druid creator fell ill, threatening the collapse of the local food productivity, he set out on a quest to earn the money or status needed to get him medical aid. Towards this end, he travelled to the cold lands of [[Vön]] and played a part in escorting a fair maiden to safety as part of the [[Silver Hopefuls]]. He also helped save a town that was reliant on food deliveries from his home. For his deeds here he earned silver-class rank in the [[Canathi Caste System]], and got the medicine to save his master.
