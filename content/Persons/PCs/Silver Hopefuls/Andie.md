A white-furred rabbitfolk of [[Vön]] and brother of [[Andia]]. More bossy than his sister. His earth (ice?) kinesis has allowed him to make practical solutions to many a challenge. Played a part in escorting a fair maiden to safety as part of the [[Silver Hopefuls]] - earning him a silver-class rank in the [[Canathi Caste System]].
