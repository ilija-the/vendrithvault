A kholo ranger of [[Nagonkor]] that ventured out of his warm jungle home climate to escort a fair maiden in the freezing lands of [[Vön]], as part of the [[Silver Hopefuls]]. For this, he technically earned a silver-class rank in the [[Canathi Caste System]], though it is unclear how much this will apply or matter to an outsider.
