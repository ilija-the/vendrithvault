A fleshwarped created to endure visible injuries as part of gladitorial combat through his translucent body. He played a role in escorting a fair maiden to safety in [[Vön]] as part of the [[Silver Hopefuls]] - earning him recognition as a person and a silver-class rank in the [[Canathi Caste System]]. He was briefly kidnapped by [[Crusader Pirates]] during this quest.
