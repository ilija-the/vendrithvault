A group of adventurers that either came from or ventured to [[Vön]], partaking in a quest to escort the fair maiden [[Ayne]], granddaughter of the Shaman of [[Town of Ire]], safely back to the town. Throughout this, they endured a blizzard, had to stop [[Nagonkor|nagonkori raiders]] that attempted to kidnap the maiden, and stop an attack that could've burnt Ire and its food supplies to the ground, as well as putting a finishing blow to a dying crew of flying [[Crusader Pirates]]. For their deeds, the silver hopefuls were all granted silver-rank class in the [[Canathi Caste System]].
