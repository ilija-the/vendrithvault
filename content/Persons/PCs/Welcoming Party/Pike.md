An orange and maned lioness amurrun swashbuckler. While young and perhaps a bit irresponsibly brave, she earned a name for herself hunting [[Piracy|pirates]]. She eventually was allowed to leave for [[Friedport]], where she managed to defeat the pirate captain [[Nazaar Eviljaw]] as part of the [[Welcoming Party]].
