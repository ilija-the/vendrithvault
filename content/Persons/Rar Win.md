Rar Win is a [[Friedport|Friedporter]] [[Crafting|craftsman]] and [[Tinkering|tinkering]], with a particular interest in the use of wood for almost all projects. Friedport, a capital of woodworking, is thus the perfect home for him. He has been a member of the [[Holdok Crafting Guild]].

He easily gets ill with a cold, and is often sniffly. He tends to speak shortly and to the point, with little attention to social nuance and emotions.
