Vann is or was a [[Goblins|Goblin]] [[Underworld|criminal]] of the [[Bemdali Fireworks]] in [[Philosophia]]. Old but wise, Vann has been a semi-paternal figure in the gang for many. He has likely sired some children in the gang, but since they're raised communally that isn't necessarily kept track of.
