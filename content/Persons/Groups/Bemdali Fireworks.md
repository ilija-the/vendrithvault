The Bemdali Fireworks is a [[Underworld|criminal gang]] of [[Philosophia]], primarily consisting of [[Goblins|Goblins]]. They grew out of stage [[Performance|performances]] including the use of [[Alchemy|alchemical fireworks]] - which they kept the culture of. They've found various ways to use fireworks and similar explosive alchemy to their advantage, such as in the on-going [[Goblin Gang War]].

They were previously lead by a trio of Goblin Brothers known as "The Shattered" on the outside.

Their main rival is [[Yellow the Goblin]] and his gang.
