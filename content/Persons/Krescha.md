Krescha is a tadpole-like [[Goblins|Goblin]] from [[Philosophia]] that escaped the city and the [[Bemdali Fireworks]] gang with the help of [[Desmet Gaius]], whom has taken on an adoptive father-like role in her life. Similar to how Gaius is a craftsman that makes beautifully engraved armours, Krescha is a painter and tattoo artist. She is also known to enjoy the party streets and gambling on her own in her spare time. She has a bit of a talent for the rogueish, using her meek stature and personality to put people at ease and get away with it.

She has one at least one occasion partaken in [[Survival|fishing]], as she tried out Gaius' fishing rod he got from his father.
