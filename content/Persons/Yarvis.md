Yarvis is a [[Friedport|Friedporter]] [[Landcrabs|Landcrab]] that participated in the [[Summer Games]], together with his best friend [[Avexe]] and their bosses [[The Prince]] and [[Su Na]]. Before this, Yarvis realised he had inherited [[Giants|Giant's]] blood, which after tapping into caused him to grow in size. Yarvis at one point struggled with alcoholism.
