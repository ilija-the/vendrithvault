Dimitri is formerly one of the [[Knights of the Round]] of the [[Sanitorium]]. He was known for his brutally effective leadership in putting down a Crusader invasion around 1242 ADc, earning him the nicknames "the Deadly" or "General of Death." He retired with honours due to the toll the wars had taken on him. He then later chose to get involved with [[Premier Force]], [[Jade the Just]] and the [[Wereforged]] project - before then being embroiled in the [[Jadefall Crisis]].
