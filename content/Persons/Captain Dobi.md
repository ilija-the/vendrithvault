Dobi was the trusted [[Ysoki]] aide of [[Captain Damia]] that has since taken over the [[Patrollers]] of [[Friedport]]. After the passing of Damia and the end of the [[Landcrab Plot]], Dobi, [[Ashur Holdok]] and [[Councilman Redd]] were instrumental to a series of reforms to the city of Friedport.
