Desmet Gaius is a furred [[Goblins|Goblin]] and former blacksmith of the [[Bemdali Fireworks]] of [[Philosophia]], Desmet Gaius fled the gang life together with his adoptive daughter [[Krescha]]. They now live in [[Friedport]], both running businesses out of their home. Desmet Gaius still makes artistically engraved armours. Gaius is very much one to enjoy the intimate company of others, and does not keep a hard distinction between his professional and private relations - perhaps a leftover of his [[Underworld|gang]]. life. He is ex-boyfriends with [[Merrick Auver Julian]], from when they both lived in Philosophia as young adults.

Gaius was at one point hunted down by an assassin from the Fireworks, [[Old Detsjen]], but the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] saved him and Krescha's lives. 

Gaius owns a masterpiece [[Survival|fishing rod]] he inherited from his father, but has not used much.
