Mangx is a young but ambitious animal tamer and wilderness explorer, largely operating alone on his special projects in [[Horiza|Horiza]]. One project he has attempted to make work is the [[Mounties|"Mounties,"]] his project to tame and use [[Flight|flying]] [[Animals]] or [[Beasts]] (such as [[Hippogriffs]] or [[Gryphons]]) in order to create a network of fast travel between settlements and hubs.

He was briefly kidnapped by [[Mercenaries]] outside of [[Friedport]], but was saved by the [[Newcomers of Friedport]] on the imperative of [[Louen Leonceur]].
