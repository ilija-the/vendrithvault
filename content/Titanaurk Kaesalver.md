Titanaurk Salvo Kaesalver is an [[Orcs|Orc]] [[Scribing|librarian]] and [[Laws|Judge]] working for the [[Horizan Guild Association]] in [[Friedport]]. He oversaw the trial [[HGA v. Bande Aparte]], in which he briefly made a verdict against all the evidence previously shown. This was speculated to be because of the influence of the [[Ragnil Veldaks|Shadow over Friedport]]. After the timely intervention of [[Captain Dobi|Dobi]], however, he corrected his verdict and saw Bande Aparte a free man.
