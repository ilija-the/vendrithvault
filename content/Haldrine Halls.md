The Haldrine Halls is a settlement akin to a monastery for warriors, monks and [[Adventurers|adventurers]], which lies in the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]], north of the [[Celene]]. It sits upon a heightened area with a difficult path to it, and lies rather far from the proper settlement of [[Tallface]]. [[Fort Ferr]] technically lies closer, but is divided by a thick forest and a number of river valleys in the middle of it. So does [[Twinvale]], but the Halls and it can only access each-other by passing through the Twin Forest, with many superstitions around it. Still, some aspiring adventurers seek to make it to the Haldrine Halls from Twinvale as a rite of passage - which the elders of Haldrine may reward.
