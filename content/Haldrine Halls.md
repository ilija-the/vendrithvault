The Haldrine Halls is a settlement akin to a monastery for warriors, monks and [[Adventurers|adventurers]], which lies in the [[Painted Canath|Painted Province]] of [[Canath]], north of the [[Celene]]. It sits upon a heightened area with a difficult path to it, and lies rather far from the proper settlements of [[Twinvale]] and [[Tallface]].
