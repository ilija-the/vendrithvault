The First Kobold Dynasty (name WIP) is the kobold dynasty that ruled immediately following the [[Draconic Concordat]]. Their early rule is characterised by conflict against remaining hostile dragons, which eventually took the imperial province of [[Lorgun]] as their own, effectively breaking it off from the Mogxan Empire. The First Kobold Dynasty subsequently entered an era of flourishing arts, culture and diplomacy as the veil between them and the world the dragons had once terrorised was lifted.
