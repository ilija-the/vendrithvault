The Eastern Kobold Dynasty was the second dynasty of the [[Kobold Throne]], taking over from the [[First Kobold Dynasty]] after the Trimillennial Revolution.  

The Eastern Kobold Dynasty moved its capital from the [[Sergetis|Sergetine]] mainland of the former Dragon Empire, instead locating itself in [[Dreyus]] outside of [[Singsong]]. The Eastern Kobolds initiated large reforms, finally abandoning elements of the empire that were shaped for the benefit of raider dragons and their hoards, instead favouring a [[Small Folk]] elite with education to direct the [[Big Folk]] to do labour for everyone's benefit.

The Eastern Kobold Dynasty secretly recognised the right to independence of the peoples of [[Lorgun]], and aided their coming rebellion against the former imperial dragons by sending [[Adventurers|adventurers]] and weapons. Doing so, they secured a lasting friendship with the islanders.

The Eastern Kobold Dynasty was eventually conquered by the conqueror who would become known as [[Resting Crown|God-King Idur]], forming the first empire spanning [[Canath]] and [[Mogxa]].
