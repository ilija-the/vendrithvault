The Eastern Kobold Dynasty (name WIP) was the second dynasty of the [[Kobold Throne]], following the [[First Kobold Dynasty]] and being succeeded by the [[Idurian Empire]] and [[Western Kobold Dynasty]].

The Eastern Kobold Dynasty moved its capital from the [[Sergetis|Argathian]] mainland of the former Dragon Empire, instead locating itself in [[Dreyus]] outside of [[Singsong]]. The Eastern Kobolds initiated large reforms, finally abandoning elements of the empire that were shaped for the benefit of raider dragons and their hoards, instead favouring a [[Small Folk]] elite with education to direct the [[Big Folk]] to do labour for everyone's benefit.

The Eastern Kobold Dynasty secretly recognised the right to independence of the peoples of [[Lorgun]], and aided their coming rebellion against the former imperial dragons by sending [[Adventurers|adventurers]] and weapons. Doing so, they secured a lasting friendship with the islanders.
