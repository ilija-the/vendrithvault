The Western Kobold Dynasty was the third and last dynasty of the [[Kobold Throne]], said to have ended the [[Mogxan Interregnum]] with their Unification Proclamation in 856 ADc. It was characterised by rehabilitating the [[Dragons]] and their language, glorifying the kobolds' draconic past, and giving many concessions and privileges to the [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa]] of [[Mogxa]]. 

What it is most known for however is its fate in 1193 ADc. The Kobold Throne long decayed to corruption and weakness under them, and after they had been working to put down a series of peasant rebellions at a high cost, the [[Sanitorium|Anti-Titan Army]] marched into their lands. Attempting to fight off the unwelcome host, the Kobold-lead armies failed miserably and routed. Not long after, the [[Rainbow Bridge]] was found and destroyed, and the victorious army of the [[War Over Haeven]] met at a Sanitorium outside of [[Philosophia]] to discuss its future. Deciding to take over the Kobold Throne, the Western Kobold Dynasty put up little fight after its emperor abdicated, and many of its governors and bureaucrats simply continued to work for their conquerors.
