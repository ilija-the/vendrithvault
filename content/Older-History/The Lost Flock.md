The Lost Flock is a mythological lost tribe that separated from the [[VVIKI/Older-History/Flock of Man]]. It is difficult to confirm the veracity of the Lost Flock. If it did exist, then mentions of it may have been intentionally scrubbed and damaged. Some speculate this the lost flock left due to religious or political disagreements, and that done to erase their memory was erased to quell possible rebellion or heresy.
