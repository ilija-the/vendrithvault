Feudalism encompasses a range of [[Caste Systems|Social Hierarchies]] and [[Government]]. It usually consists of smaller, weaker lands being ruled by a noble which then pleads their loyalty, some taxes, and levies in the event of war, to a more powerful sovereign above them. This can be multi-layered.
