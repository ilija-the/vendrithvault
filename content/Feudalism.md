Feudalism encompasses a range of [[Caste Systems|Social Hierarchies]] and [[Government]]. It usually consists of smaller, weaker lands being ruled by a noble which then pleads their loyalty, some taxes, and levies in the event of war, to a more powerful sovereign above them. The sovereign above them again then owes their subject protection from outside invasion. These arrangements often lie across many layers, with for example the mayors of cities serving barons, barons serving the count, the count serving a duke, and a duke serving the king.

> Archduchies are a type of duchy held by certain duchies that reach a large size, comparable to a kingdom, but which for some reason or other don't claim the moniker of Kingdom or King.

Empires and emperors are usually something special proclaimed only by those who have a great impact on the trajectory of world history - or those cocky enough to claim they do/will. Rarely, empires and emperors might have kings sworn as their subjects underneath - though more typically emperors tend to also hold kingdom titles directly.
