The Reivlands are a somewhat marshy and hilly land, though with some good [[Agriculture|farmland]] further inland, that exists on the edge between [[Fenrunr]] and the rest of [[Mogxa]]. Whereas the rest of that border has been firmly defined by the [[Fendur Mountains]] to the north, the Reivlands border has often shifted and has been settled by both Mogxans and Fenruni throughout history. The Reivlands have often seen incursions by people from west and east coming to raid.
