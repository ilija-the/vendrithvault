A set of ships built around a flagship known as the [[Monstrous Ark]] is believed to have sailed a variety of [[Creatures]], including many [[Monsters of Vendrith|Monsters]], from their natural habitats of [[Iduria]] to [[Horiza]] - before running aground in the Chocolate Isles. It is unknown who ordered and manned this, and for what purpose, as no high [[Horizan Guild Association|HGA]] official have recognised or discussed it.
