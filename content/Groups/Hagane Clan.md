The Hagane Clan is the clan associated with the [[Steel Lord]] on the [[Isle of Flod]], having claimed most of the northern provinces as well as having famous sea observatories, a.k.a. [[Lighthouses from Hell]].

They are the leading lizardfolk clan on Flod and have allied or at least have backing of many minor clans across Flod, particularly to the east of their territories, such as the [[Senso Clan]].
