Su Na is a water-[[Druid]] from [[Friedport]], and a close friend and aide of [[The Prince]]. He grew up at the [[Theatre Orphanage]] together with The Prince and [[Etcetera]], and later, [[Saint Karvi Massiri|Karvi Massiri]]. Su Na was particularly close to Etctera, and was grief-strucken when she disappeared. He became suspicious of Karvi, and became ever-loyal to The Prince, helping boost his confidence and guide him on a path to popularity amongst the [[Landcrabs]] of the city. This would eventually result in their shared dominion over the Landcrabs of [[Waterlogg Street]].
