The Silver River is a major river of [[Mogxa|Imperial Mogxa]] that starts near the [[Philosophian Peripherie]], passes through [[Avrantes]], and runs out through the city of [[Zardoba]]. Much of it runs down the shade of the [[Electrum Mountains]] to its north, and helps irrigate a large amount of [[Agriculture]]. Some [[Alchemist|alchemists]] engage in attempting to gather silver and gold flakes from the water.

While the river is rather wide, it is also highly shallow and runs down a number of cliff-like steps, making navigating the river on boats impossible.
