Guanghold is a village high in the mountains of [[Mogxa]]. It has a culture revering a Sun God, or just light itself. It currently finds itself in darkness however, as an [[Eyne]] has drifted in front of it and blocks its sunlight most of the day. The beautiful garden at its centre, with an ancient Peach tree, is slowly dying in the shade.

The former Mayor, and his pet [[Dragonet]], went missing 15 years ago.
