A group of 4 brothers that were nearly inseparable as they with iron fists ruled the underworld of [[Singsong]]. It is speculated they may have had a hand in causing the [[Singsong Port Fire]]. 

Later, they were recruited by the [[Mariners]] of [[Friedport]] under [[Harbourmaster Kiam Lockneed|Kiam Lockneed]], likely due to their reputation as ferocious and disciplined. 

Their sister, the [[Queen of Grog]], had long since disowned the brothers. Not long after learning of their presence in her [[Piracy|pirate]] waters, the Iron Brothers were beaten by [[Roslyn Chatkur]] and the [[Newcomers of Friedport]], and delivered to the Queen for punishment.
