A group of 4 brothers that were nearly inseparable as they ruled the underworld of [[Singsong]], and then, the shores of [[Friedport]], with their iron fists. As the siblings of the [[Queen of Grog]] whom hated them, the brothers were eventually handed to the Queen of Grog, punished and separated forcefully. It is speculated they may have had a hand in causing the [[Singsong Port Fire]].
