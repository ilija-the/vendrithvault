A powerful figure that appears akin to a tiger-like [[Amurrun]] (though it is uncertain if he is one). He is of quite a bit of influence in [[Talvas]], in which he has multiple times been appointed the [[Rajistrate]] of [[Rraka]]. His reach also stretches to surrounding regions, and he has been known to travel to and hire peoples in [[Rubyfirth Bay]] and the [[Veducia|Veducian Wilds]] for his purposes. His mansions are known to be gaudy and full of symbols of his endless wealth and curiosity for artefacts.
