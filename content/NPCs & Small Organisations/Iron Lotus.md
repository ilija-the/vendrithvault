The Iron Lotus is broadly considered to be the main leader or figurehead of the [[Crusaders]], as a woman with many religious-like beliefs centred around her and her destiny. She is by many considered a living [[Saint]].

Strong, stoic and said to be part of a [[New Creed]] prophecy about the return of the Titans. Formerly of Philosophia, but left as a result of the [[Tower Hostage Crisis]].

Signed the [[Truth Concord]].
