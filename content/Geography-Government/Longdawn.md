The Archduchy of Longdawn is the largest and possibly oldest city in [[Ferweth]], and is famous for its design reminiscent of an enormous cathedral. As the historical regional capital, most Ferwethi settlements have been known to swear loyalty and get aid and soldiers from Longdawn and its many [[Saintly Churches]].
