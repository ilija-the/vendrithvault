The Rend is the northernmost section of the [[Rubyfirth Bay|Rubyfirth Sea]], and is named so for the belief that this once consisted of land which was sliced apart in a great conflict. The island of [[Regal]] lies to its west, which is home to peoples of [[Nagonkor]] as well as a number of [[Crusaders|Crusaders]].

The [[Rend Peoples]] are named after this sea.
