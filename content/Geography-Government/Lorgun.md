While it enjoyed independence and served as a safe harbour for people from all walks of life initially, it then became the most distant longstanding imperial province of the many Mogxan based empires. After the [[Draconic Concordat]], the island was cut off from the mainland as rebel dragons directly seized the various islands as their own personal fiefdoms. It was in response to this that the Lorguni became talented dragon-slayers, and today it enjoys a de facto independence as they continue to intermittently kill dragons and unify more of the island chain.

Lorgun was initially settled by an early contingent from Mogxa which took to the islands for a variety of reasons, including escaping law and old families. As it grew new settlers and traders from Mogxa would eventually show up. Eventually, the [[Draconic Empire]] of Mogxa came knocking and demanded Lorgun be treated as an imperial province. After a short war, the largely harmonic and unarmed island society surrendered, much laid in flames.
