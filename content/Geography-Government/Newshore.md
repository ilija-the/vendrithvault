Newshore is one of the major settlements of [[New World - Horiza|Horiza]], which has alongside [[Adamsworth]] overtaken [[Friedport]] in importance. It is characterised by being settled on a large floodplain, with buildings built into the river itself, inspired by a base of [[Horizan Precursors|Horizan Precursor]] buildings already sitting in it when settlers found the place. A series of rope cars connect to the Newshore Tower, allowing easier travel for those who can afford it.

Overlooking the city is the mountains and glacier of the [[Ivory Crown Plateau|Ivory Crown]], from where the settlements' rivers originate. Newshore is surrounded by farming of floodable crops. The river continues down into a marshy area that is particularly difficult to traverse, including a marshy forest, where few go except criminals, hunters and witches.

Newshore does have somewhat of a rivalry with Adamsworth, but is amenable to Friedport due to its importance as a hub connecting to the old world.
