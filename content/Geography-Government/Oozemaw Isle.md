Oozemaw Isle is a mysterious islet near [[Friedport]] that is difficult to disembark upon due to its cliffside edges and rocky shallow approach, but which hides an underground tunnel connected to the sea big enough to house a ship. Inside, the landmass on two sides serve as a potential point for trade deals, which it has been used for by pirates. The surface of the island is forested but reaches up to a peak with a pond on it. It's also full of [[Slimes]].
