Littlehorne is a sizeable settlement in the Galaen Wilds that was created after the [[War Over Haeven]], and contains a sizeable number of migrants from other destroyed [[Galae|Galaen]] settlements, as well as [[Kholo]] migrants from the south. It sits on the northern coast, looking out upon [[Blissfeldt]].
