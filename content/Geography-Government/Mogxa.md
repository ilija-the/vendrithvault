A highly populated region of the [[Old World - Iduria|Iduria]], bordering [[Galae]] and the [[Rubyfirth Bay]], as well as being close to the island-chains of [[Vilhun]] and [[Lorgun]]. 

Mogxa is the home of the [[Sanitorium]], the state formed after the [[War Over Heaven]] by taking over the [[Kobold Throne]]. Previously, said Kobold Throne had ruled a large but weak and fragile empire. Before that again, Mogxa was home to the infamous [[Draconic Empire]] based in [[Argathia]].
