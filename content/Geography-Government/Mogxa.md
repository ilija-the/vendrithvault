Mogxa is a densely populated subcontinent and has historically been home to various centralised empires - the [[Draconic Empire]], the [[Kobold Throne]], and now currently, the [[Sanitorium]]. The Mogxan region borders [[Galae]] (with which it has a blurry border) to the northwest, the rubyfirth sea to its west and south

, bordering [[Galae]] and the [[Rubyfirth Bay]], as well as being close to the island-chains of [[Vilhun]] and [[Lorgun]]. 

Mogxa is the home of the [[Sanitorium]], the state formed after the [[War Over Haeven]] by taking over the [[Kobold Throne]]. Previously, said Kobold Throne had ruled a large but weak and fragile empire. Before that again, Mogxa was home to the infamous [[Draconic Empire]] based in [[Argathia]].
