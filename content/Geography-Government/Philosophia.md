Philosophia is an enormous city at the centre of [[Mogxa]], and is by tradition considered both the first city and the biggest city in the world. It was founded by the [[Flock of Man]] under the leadership of the [[Icen Maiden]]. Philosophia is many ways built like a fortress that could never in a million years be taken by an outside attacker. The city has a unique form of self-governance under the [[Guilds of Philosophia]] as part of the [[Pactlaw]]. Despite being the largest city in Mogxa and part of the [[Sanitorium]], its autonomy persists.

![[Punk_Fight.png]]

Recently saw the [[Jadefall Crisis]].

The [[Dragontears of Philosophia]] are speckled over the city, which gives the appearance of a starry night sky despite the light pollution.


>In the end we will be one. [...] Indeed. Till all are one.

*-Founding slogan of Philosophia, engraved in city stone.*
