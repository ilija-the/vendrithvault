A large and ancient city at the centre of [[Mogxa]]. Said to have been co-founded by the [[Icen Maiden]]. In many ways built like a fortress that could never in a million years be taken by an outside attacker. The city has a unique form of self-governance under the [[Guilds of Philosophia]] as part of the [[Pactlaw]]. Despite being the largest city in Mogxa and part of the [[Sanitorium]], its autonomy persists.

Recently saw the [[Jadefall Crisis]].

The [[Dragontears of Philosophia]] are speckled over the city, which gives the appearance of a starry night sky despite the light pollution.

>In the end we will be one. [...] Indeed. Till all are one.*

-Founding slogan of Philosophia, engraved in city stone.
