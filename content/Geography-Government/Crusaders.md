The "Crusaders" are the holy warriors and their supporters that seek to bring back the [[Titans]] and end the [[The Great Silence|Great Silence]]. To do so, they have engaged in various armed conflicts and acts of sabotage against the their mortal enemy, the [[Sanitorium]], as part of the [[Eternal Crusade]]. The Crusaders have a shared goal, but consist of many radically different factions.

The most important faction is the [[Crusader Armies]] located around [[Canath]] and parts of [[Galae]]. While these armies don't directly control territory, pretty much all city states, duchies and kingdoms in Canath they have stationed themselves around have accepted their demands for food, supplies and the right to make camp. This has not been friction-less, however (see [[Camp Conflict]]).
