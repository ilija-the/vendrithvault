The "Crusaders" are the holy warriors and their supporters that seek to bring back the [[Titans]] and end the [[The Great Silence|Great Silence]]. This effort of theirs, which has now gone on for about 80 years, is dubbed the [[Eternal Crusade]].

Soldiers fighting the [[Eternal Crusade]] against the Sanitorium in order to bring the [[Titans]] back.
