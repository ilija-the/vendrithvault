The "Crusaders" are the many holy warriors, auxiliaries and supporters of the effort to bring back the [[Titans]] and end the [[The Great Silence|Great Silence]] as part of the [[Eternal Crusade]].

Soldiers fighting the [[Eternal Crusade]] against the Sanitorium in order to bring the [[Titans]] back.
