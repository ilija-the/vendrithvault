Adamsworth is a major settlement in [[New World - Horiza|Horiza]], which has recently alongside [[Newshore]] bypassed the importance of [[Friedport]] in many ways. Adamsworth is home to much of the [[Horizan Guild Association]] leadership in Horiza. The settlement is characterised by its series of walls surrounding an enormous statue of a man at its centre, left by [[Horizan Precursors]].
