Deria is a term encompassing the smaller islands outside the immediate shores of [[Lorgun]], scattered around the seas on the way to [[New World - Horiza|Horiza]]. Often claimed only by [[Dragons]] and the animals that live on them. These dragons govern only themselves as individuals, though are often at war with the peoples of Lorgun. 

Sailing to the [[New World - Horiza]] can be challenging due to the presence of the Derian islands on the way.

Dragons of the Derian islands have sometimes offered work as [[Mercenaries]] to [[Mogxa|Mogxans]].
