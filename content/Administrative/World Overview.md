### Mythos
*[[Mythos of Vendrith|main article]]*

[[Trinities]] lie at the core of much of Vendrish society.

Vendrith means 'world of three dragons,' stemming from the trio of [[Sibling Dragons]] that supposedly clawed each other to death before becoming the earth itself as corpses. The [[Moons of Vendrith]] are named after them, each believed to be a dragon's eye still looking down upon us.

The siblings are but one of three founding myths for many people of Vendrith, the others being the tale of the [[Creator Monkey]] that painted all life on the dragons as a canvas, and the [[First Ascended]] that protected humanoids and created the title of [[Titans]].
