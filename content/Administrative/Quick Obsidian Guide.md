For those who haven't used obsidian - the quickest possible rundown is...

- Reading the [official Obsidian wiki](https://help.obsidian.md/)!
- Bookmarks can be a useful way of tagging stuff highly relevant to you, the writer, that stays only on your local computer. I.e. Vauxs bookmarking [[Vilhun]], his characters, and organizations.
- Recommend installing [Tag Wrangler](https://github.com/pjeby/tag-wrangler) for mass tag preview and editing.

> [!tip] Vauxs' Recommendations
> Besides the above that can apply to everyone's workflow, there are also optional addons that Vauxs finds useful but are not necessary for working with this wiki.
