### [[Mythos of Vendrith|Mythos]]
Trinities are seen as holy in Vendrith, so its no wonder its origin mythos comes in three parts: 

First, harmony was disturbed as the dragon siblings Velf, Kiya and Tritos fought eachother to death. Their slashed and huddled up corpses became the world of Vendrith. Three moons bear their names today. 

Second, a creator wandering the stars happened upon the corpses and wept at their beauty. Painting the world alive, he created beautiful landscapes and the first humanoids - painted in his monkey-like image. 

Third, as humanity was assailed by mysterious Hypertyrants - a great hero arose as the First Ascended. After his mission was complete, he venerated to a higher plane of existence, leaving space for the many Titans to follow that would share divine mantles amongst themselves
