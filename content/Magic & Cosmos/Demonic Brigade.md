Perhaps the most infamous soldiers of the anti-titan armies of the War Over Haeven. They were known and feared for their horrific violence against their enemies, their prisoners of war and the civilians whose territories they passed through. Towards the end of the war, they were lured into the [[Crimson Desert|north Galaen desert]] by the [[Devil Queen of Crimson|Guardian Angel of Heaven]]. Turning out to be a trap, she carved a scar in the desert which ate most of the soldiers, and most of the remaining worn out and fleeing solders shot. Falling through into the newly created [[Crimson|plane of Crimson]], a number of the demonic brigade survived and retreated into its desert. There, they would become actual [[Demon|demons]] and fight the [[Devil|devils]] of the Guardian Angel turned Devil Queen. This has been dubbed the latest example of a [[Bloodwar]]. Some blame the Demonic Brigade for the [[Blood Storm|Blood Storms]] that pass through parts of Iduria, speculated to steal away people to serve as reinforcements to their ranks.

The demonic brigade was forgotten and disowned by many in the successful armies which would become the [[Sanitorium]]. Some more infamous and warlike figures, however, have claimed the Brigade was essential and effective, and that they were to be emulated. Some claim the [[Knights of the Round]] have learned from the Demonic Brigade's tactics.

Some [[Crusaders]] have argued for a making a [[Helmarches|Helmarch]], that is to say an armed march into Crimson (a plane of [[Hel]]), to help eradicate the Demonic Brigade once and for all.

Famously wears the tattered remnant uniforms of the [[Draconic Legions]].
