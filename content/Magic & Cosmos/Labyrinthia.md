A [[Planes of Vendrith|plane of Vendrith]] that manifests only in small portions described as 'corridors,' 'bubbles' or 'pocket planes.' Labyrinthia is entered by performing certain actions while traversing a specific spot in [[MateriWhile it is rare, it is one of the more well established planes on account of the ease of traversing into it once an entrance is discovered. A corridor or gate of Labyrinthia is one which connects to multiple locations in [[Materia]] at once, sometimes allowing fast and teleportation-like travel between distant areas. Labyrinthine planes are unstable, however, and may disappear and 'move' just as they appeared.

Animals (and especially birds and fish) have been thought to have a sense for Labyrinthine corridors, and are speculated to have spread out and migrated across the world using them.
