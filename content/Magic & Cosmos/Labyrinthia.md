A [[Planes of Vendrith|plane of Vendrith]] that manifests only in small portions described as 'corridors,' 'bubbles' or 'pocket planes.' While it is rare, it is one of the more well established planes on account of the ease of traversing into it once an entrance is discovered. As per the corridor Animals, and especially birds and fish, have been known to sense out the existence of openings into Labyrinthia. Some speculate that animals have spread across the world of [[Materia]] by migrating through Labyrinthine corridors.


One of the [[Planes of Vendrith]].
