An eidolon is a specific and powerful type of [[Companions|companion ]]creature that is bound to a [[Summoner]], often extraplanar or [[Monsters of Vendrith|monstrous]] in nature. The Eidolons are believed to temporarily return to their 'home plane' whenever desummoned, though this has not been confirmed and many questions have been raised what could constitute as a home plane to many types of Eidolons (for example, where do animal-like eidolons go?).
