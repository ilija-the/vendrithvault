An eidolon is a type of [[Companions|companion ]]creature that is bound to a [[Summoner]], often extraplanar or [[Monsters of Vendrith|monstrous]] in nature. The Eidolons are believed to temporarily return to their 'home plane' whenever desummoned, though there isn't a known or confirmed home plane for all types of Eidolons.
