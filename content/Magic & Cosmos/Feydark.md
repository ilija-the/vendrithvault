One of the [[Planes of Vendrith]]. The Feydark is described as a fairytale-esque dimension in which many of the stories of old and myth appear as reality. Usually however, they take on a nightmarish or gruesome form, or at the very least an unnerving one. Some have speculated [[Gnomes]] originate from the Feydark.

Some have compared the plane of [[Deep Gray]] to the Feydark for its dreamlike feeling. In this comparison, the Feydark is usually described as a nightmare as opposed to a mere dream.
