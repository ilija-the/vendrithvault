[[Devil|Devils]] are famous for enjoying making contracts, often with normal denizens of [[Materia]]. These are known to grant great boons or power to the contract-taker, but at some cost or in return for some work or action. Many claim these contracts are magically binding, though it is unknown if there is some greater magical force that forces a [[Devil]] to be true to the law.
