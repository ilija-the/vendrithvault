A weather phenomenon in which blood rains, known to happen upon parts of [[Ferweth]]. These areas, usually safe from [[Vampires]] if behind a wall or in a home, have been viciously attacked by vampires in such a frenzy they no longer follow their usual rule of not entering uninvited.

It is unclear if this is related to the similar and geographically close [[Blood Storm|Blood Storms]].
