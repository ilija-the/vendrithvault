A primal entity that resides in [[Friedport]]. The myth goes she was created after a girl was tragically killed in an underground tunnel by an outsider, her body left in a series of tree roots that may have absorbed her soul. The ritualistic grieving done by her [[Landcrabs|landcrab]] tribe, the [[Quietgraves]], may have contributed to her reincarnation as the Reedmother. Those whom have visited the site since describe seeing a beating heart made of roots. Friedport may have experienced a growth spurt to its trees, grass and other plants since the Reedmother came to.

![[Reedmother.png | center | 800]]

In later times, the speculated killer [[Carlos Valenci]] faced much hatred, causing him to take a job somewhere he believed he'd take less attention - the [[Portaupearl Crypts]]. He worked here until his own mysterious murder.

A small now-broken open shack near [[Amberhole]] saw a large and old-seeming tree suddenly and overnight grow through it. It has since become a site of devotion to nature, and is frequently visited by [[Leshy]] spirits.
