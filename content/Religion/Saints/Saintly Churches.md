In certain parts of the world [[Saint|Saints]] are worshipped either as deific figures in themselves, or as conduits or icons of deities. Most known for this is the region of [[Ferweth]], which often has different patron saints for each settlement depending on its historical and local monstrous threats. As an example, the settlement of [[Twinberry]] has been known to plead to [[Saint Jonathan]] for protection against creatures weak to silver.
