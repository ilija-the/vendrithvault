Reverence is the gradual disappearance of a deity from both [[Materia]] and [[Haeven]], no longer coming back to either planes of existence and broadly considered to have "moved on." This can be done with and against a deities will. It is believed those who have undergone reverence have ascended to a higher plane of existence, if not returned back to the [[Great Flow]].
