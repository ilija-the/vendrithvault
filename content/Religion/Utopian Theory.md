Utopian theory is a [[Crusader Sects|Crusader Sect]] that believes the [[Titans]] can be reached by merely following the instructions left in the [[New Creed]] correctly. The namesake Utopia refers to the plane in which they will be found.

Attempts to decode and carry out the instructions in the New Creed have lead Utopian Theorists to study clockwork, [[Automatons|automatons]] and [[Leylines]]. One settlement studying Utopian Theory was [[Veridica]].
