Saltfield is a recently growing settlement on the eastern edge of [[Mogxa|Central Mogxa]], constructed in order to harvest the salt from a lake that evaporated. Some have called this a [[Disasters of the Silence|Disaster of the Silence]], whereas others only call it that sarcastically while raking in money from the [[Mercantilism|salt trade]].

Saltfield does lie on the crossroads between Central Mogxa, the Mogxan Lakes and Eastern Mogxa - however it remains off the main path and still lacks major roads connecting all of these.
