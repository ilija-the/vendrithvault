Gulmfort is a fortress settlement inland of [[Auchiton]] and the [[Celene]] coast of [[Canath]], sitting atop a central hilly area with great control over land passages north-west and south-east - including that of the Old Passage to [[Galae]]. It touches lake Gyila to its south, where some amount of fishing is done for the fort.
