One of many apocalyptic prophecies predicting the fall of [[Vilhun]] due to divine forces shattering the despotic Vili rule. Its gaining popularity with the ever increasing [[Dsi]]

A notable orc diaspora believes in the prophecy, said to be originating from [[Promised Revealer|the great seer]] himself. While not violent on, they share many beliefs with the [[Macabre Maelstrom]].
