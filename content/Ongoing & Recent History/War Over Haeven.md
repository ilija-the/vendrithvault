The War Over Heaven is easily the most defining event of the last century, and served as the starting point of the two broad factions which dominate the geopolitical situation - the [[Crusaders]] and the [[Sanitorium]]. While the war's origins lies in a relatively humble set of inter-deity skirmishing in [[Canath]], it was through a cascading series of events, opportunism and desires for revenge that the situation began to come out of control.
