A name given to a variety of natural disasters that have happened since the start of the [[The Great Silence]]. The [[Magus in the High Tower]] has famously attributed the frequency and scale of these directly to the exiling of the [[Titans]] - essentially blaming them on the [[Sanitorium]]. [[Crusaders]] often see them as divine retribution and use it to justify the need to return the Titans.

One such disaster is the [[Sinking of Sargathia]], in which the southeastern peninsula and traditional homeland of the [[Dragons]] that ran the [[Draconic Empire]] sank under the sea.

The [[Leyline Problem]] has also been called a Disaster of the Silence.
