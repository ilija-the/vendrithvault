Vann is or was a [[Goblins|Goblin]] [[Underworld|criminal]] of the [[Bemdali Fireworks]] in [[Philosophia]]. Old but wise, Vann has been a semi-paternal figure in the gang for many. He has also fathered a number of children in it, but as per the culture there biological fatherhood has not been kept track of.
