Different cultures and ancestries often treat the bodies of the dead differently. In some cultures, including certain [[Canath|Canathi]] and [[Crusaders|Crusader]] ones, it is customary to cremate bodies. In others, including those of the church of the [[Immortal Memory]], one should instead be buried in graveyards or crypts. Others yet again wish for bodies to be returned to nature as mulch for plantlife, or to be eaten by fauna such as on sky-burial towers.

[[Dwarves]] become as [[Dwarven Statues|statues]] when they die, and wish to be left as such. Ideally, the site of their statue becomes a [[Shrines|Shrine]], and if secluded, may contain their most cherished tools, weapons or creations.

In history some [[Halflings]] have had their [[Halfling Markings]] turned into books or scrolls, to document history and the experiences of their lives. More recently it has become normal to print these over onto paper usinor tablets nstead of using their actual skin.
