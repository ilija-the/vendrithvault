The islands of [[Vilhun]] are ruled over by an emperor backed by three dynasty heads, one for each of the [[Hun Lords]]. 

The emperor is elected by the noble families, but the dynasty heads have veto power on whether a given candidate is elected to the role of emperor. As such, the emperor is considered to have the divine decree to rule over Vilhun (as long as the dynasty heads do not .
