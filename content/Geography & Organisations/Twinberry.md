Twinberry is a town that straddles two sides of a ravine, usually connected by a rope bridge. The town finds itself on the lesser used mountain route through Ferweth, and serves as the definitive starting point of the region.

Twinberry has been known to observe superstition, such as showing hospitality to [[Shoony]] to avoid being cursed. The town has experienced various attacks from [[Vampires]], which got worse during [[Blood Rain]].
