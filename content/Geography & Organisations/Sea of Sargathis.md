Sargathis (meaning Sargathian Sea) is the famously dangerous and often shark-infested waters around the current island chain and peninsula of [[Sargathia]], the southernmost peninsula and island-chain of [[Mogxa]]. The waters of Sargathis have also been called the "Mouth of Mogxa" at times. 

Sargathis is now also home to the [[Sinking of Argathia|sunken]] parts of former [[Argathia]], sometimes called Argathis in its sunken form. Some brave [[Adventurers|adventurers]] and archaeologists have attempted to dive into Sargathis to find the Argathis ruins and its many lost artefacts and history.
