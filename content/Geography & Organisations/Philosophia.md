#places 
A large and ancient city at the centre of [[Mogxa]]. Said to have been co-founded by the [[Icen Maiden]]. In many ways built like a fortress that could never in a million years be taken by an outside attacker. The city has a unique form of self-governance under the [[Guilds of Philosophia]], and despite being the largest city and technical capital of the [[Sanitorium]], its autonomy persists.

Technically the capital of the [[Sanitorium]], but exists in a unique state of self-governance under the [[Guilds of Philosophia]] as part of the [[Pactlaw]].

Recently was home to the [[Jadefall Crisis]].

The [[Dragontears of Philosophia]] are speckled over the city.
