Sargathia used to be a peninsula on the southernmost tip of [[Mogxa]], and the traditional homelands and power base of many [[Dragons]] and Draconic Tribes. At times, the [[Draconic Empire]] held their courts in Sargathia. While the Draconic Empire was largely infamous across the world, Sargathia itself was a flourishing centre of culture and saw many visitors from abroad seeking to experience and learn from its traditions of Sargathian Theatre - often outshining the at the time decaying city of [[Philosophia]].

These lands contained much history as the historical homelands of the [[Dragons]] of the [[Draconic Empire]], still home to many of Mogxa's Draconic Tribes at the time. Many of the survivors vacated to other parts of Mogxa or beyond in fear, though the sinking did leave behind some landmass now forming the island chain of [[Argathiaaaaa]]. 

Much of Sargathian and Draconic Imperial history now lies at the sea floor of famously dangerous and sometimes shark-infected waters. One tradition which was essentially destroyed was Sargathian Theatre, as essentially all of its theatres and [[Sargathian Statues]] were taken by the sinking.
