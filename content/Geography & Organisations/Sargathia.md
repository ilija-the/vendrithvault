The modern name for the southern peninsula of [[Mogxa]]. Distinguished from the old name [[Sargathis]] since the [[Sinking of Sargathia]]. The underwater sections of formerly Sargathia is now called [[Argathia]].  Used to be the epicentre of the [[Draconic Empire]].

The seas around Sargathia are called [[Sargathis]]
