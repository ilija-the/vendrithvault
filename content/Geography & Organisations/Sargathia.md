Sargathia is the name for the surviving parts of [[Argathia]] since its [[Sinking of Argathia|sinking]], and consists of both the still southernmost peninsula of Mogxa as well as an island-chain. The now underwater sections of former Argathia are known as Argathis, and the seas around are called Sargathis.
