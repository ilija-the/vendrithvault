Knights assigned to each of the [[Philosophers of the Round]] that governs the [[Sanitorium]]. Each philosopher gets 2 knights each, creating a sort of [[Trinities|trinity]]. Whereas the philosophers create the theories, rules and goals, the knights put their wishes into action. Often a more respected position than that of the philosophers.

Wears a dress partially reminiscent of the [[Draconic Legions]], the former elite forces of the [[Kobold Throne]] known for their wide-brimmed metal hats with horns. Also similar to the [[Demonic Brigade]] in the same sense, as their uniforms are leftovers from the era of the late Draconic Legion.
