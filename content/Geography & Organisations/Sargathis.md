

Sargathis is the famously dangerous and often shark-infested sea around the current island chain and peninsula of [[Sargathia]], the southernmost peninsula and island-chain of [[Mogxa]]. Sargathis is now also home to the [[Sinking of Argathia|sunken]] parts of former [[Argathia]], sometimes called Argathis in its sunken form.
