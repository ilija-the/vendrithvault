A historical region that today is quite contested in its extent. It lies in between [[Ferweth]] to the northwest, [[Mogxa]] to the east and [[Encanath]] to the west and south. It also shares a small jungle border with [[Nagonkor]], which it once shared a civilization with (Galaekor).
