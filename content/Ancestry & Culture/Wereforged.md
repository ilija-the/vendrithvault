A type of [[Automatons|automaton]] created in [[Philosophia]] as part of the [[Prisoner Plot]]. It was revealed after [[Jadefall Crisis]] that they were given their spark of life through humanoid souls simply refitted into them, even though it seemingly erased all their memories.
