A title given to [[Valfrid Veldaks]] as a famous and beloved protector of [[Friedport]]. The title has also been used for the potential heroes of friedport [[Zcaldon]], [[Varvevan]] and [[Xvavos]]; as well as the winners of the [[Summer Games]] [[Ashur Holdok]], [[Gator Grippli]], [[Magnus Strike]] and [[Roslyn Chatkur]]. [[Cardinal Rackawn]] who didn't win but was the most popular participant also got called this for a while as he worked very visibly to save the city during the [[Operation Blackout|blackout]], though he has since left the city.
