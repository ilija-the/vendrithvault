One of the [[Ancestries of Vendrith]], also known as "half-elves." The aiuvarin are the result when [[Elves|elves]] procreate with humans or closely related ancestries. Unlike the elves, the aiuvarin consist of regular flesh and blood, though they still share much of the bodily size and form of elves. They are known to be skinnier and vary more in height than humans tend to do, and usually have black sclera.
