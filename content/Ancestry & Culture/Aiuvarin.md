One of the [[Ancestries of Vendrith]]. Also known as a half-elf, aiuvarin are the result when [[Elves]] procreate with humans or closely related ancestries. Unlike the elves, the aiuvarin consist of regular flesh and blood, though they still share much of the bodily size and form of elves.
