Kitsune are a shape-changing [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] believed to originate in [[Vilhun]] or [[Mewji]]. Their primary forms are either like humans, sometimes with fox traits, or as anthropomorphic foxes, often with fur colouration considered rare with actual foxes. Their secondary forms can either be that of an anthropomorphic fox as described previously, or as an actual fox, which appears similarly to a primary form.
