Shoony are an anthropomorphic dog-like [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]] with [[Undead]]-like features that are often viewed with great fear and mistrust. These features can be external bones or skulls, bones glowing through their flesh, ectoplasmic body parts, unusual eyes, eerily black or white fur, and most commonly, luminescent fur markings. They are said to originate in the [[World Fleece|fogs]] of [[Ferweth]], and superstition states that they must be appeased by being granted hospitality in order to avoid disaster.
