A legendary magus that largely acts as a recluse, but occasionally enters the public eye to give grand predictions or warnings.

He's lived for quite a while, appearing in records going back to before the [[War Over Haeven]].

He was the target of an attempted [[Magus Assassination Attempt|assassination]] one of the last public speeches he held, taking a poisoned knife to his chest in view of all before continuing his speech.

Has supported certain gifted students at the [[Academie Peripherie Philosophia]], such as [[Magnus Strike]].
