Officially known as the "Commonwealth of the Folksy [[Guilds of Philosophia|Guilds of]] [[Philosophia]]." Often in opposition to the [[Formal Guilds]] and the [[Faddish Guilds]]. The folksy guilds have deep ties to the [[Wheel Societies]] of Mogxa, which they have evolved with concurrently from a common source.
