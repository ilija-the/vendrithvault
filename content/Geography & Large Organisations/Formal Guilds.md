Officially known as the "Silver Coalition of the Formal [[Guilds of Philosophia|Guilds of]] [[Philosophia]]." Often in opposition to the [[Faddish Guilds]] and the [[Folksy Guilds]]. The formal guilds have deep ties to the [[Mirror Societies]] of Mogxa, which they have evolved with concurrently from a common source.
