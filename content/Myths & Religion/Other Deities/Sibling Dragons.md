### Description
**Summary** The three sibling dragons Velf, Tritas and Kiya are said to make the earth we walk on as the [[Mythos of Vendrith]]. They are said to linger with consciousness in the earth itself, and to watch on from the [[Moons of Vendrith]]. Collectively called the Nourishing Bulwark.
