### Description
**Summary** A [[Deity]] and mythical monkey-like figure said to have wandered the great expanse when he found the dead [[Sibling Dragons]], and painted all known life onto them like a canvas. Part of the [[Mythos of Vendrith]]. Artist monkey who wept at the dead dragons and painted a living world across it. 
**Base of Worship** Entire corners of the world revere the Creator Monkey as their creator in some way or other, to the point it is almost taken for granted. However, this reverence has usually taken second priority to the much more immediate, earth-near worship of Titans.
