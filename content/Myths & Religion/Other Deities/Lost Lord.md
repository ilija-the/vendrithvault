Identified as the +1 part of [[Hun Lords|Vilhi religion]] through the lens of the [[3+1 Theorem]].

Widely believed to have died heirless past [[War Over Haeven]], but there is a consistent diaspora of people that claim their heritage. Those claims widely discarded as the people making them are often charged with crimes.

Patron saint of Vilhi [[Gnomes]].

Considered to be the **sly** and **far-sighted** of the siblings. Ever so ironic, given their death.

Has been forgotten to time, and thus is not particularly thought of as anything but insignificant.
