Said to have helped lead now-venerated titans into the horizon, the Cosmic Guide serves as an inspiring light to many. From the lowly sailors and navigators entirely relying on his aid out on the cold sea, to starstruck explorers and mystic fortune tellers, the guide helps find the path both physically and temporally.
