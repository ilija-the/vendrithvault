The Astral Guide serves as a guiding light to those whom are lost, from sailors to guildmasters. He has been described to have the head of a bird, and to carry a starry cloak that allows him to hide. 
**Summary** The Astral Guide serves as a guiding light to those whom are lost.
**Base of Worship** He finds much of his support amongst sailors and explorers, but also sometimes fortune tellers, or people of power seeking guidance on how to lead. Anyone may turn to the Guide when lost.
**Appearance, Items and Powers.** He's been described as having the head of a bird and a cloak. When inverted, the cloak turns starry and allows him to become hidden.
