**Trinity** Duty and Wellbeing


The fiercely loyal lead soldier of the titans, tragically stuck fighting for deities that have since been vanquished. During the war over heaven, she turned swathes of Galae into a desert of blood carving out the plane of Crimson, from which she continues the fight from.
