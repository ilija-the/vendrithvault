#deities #religion 
Four lords appointed by the [[One & Only]] to rule [[Vilhun]] in her stead. Three of the lords became the ancestor of virtually all recognised houses of Vilhun, while the fourth is said to have died heirless.

Some outsiders have identified the [[Hun Lords]] as titans. Controversially, the Hun Lords are never meant to be considered equals to the One & Only, which causes problems when the titan label is applied to both her and the lords.

The Hun Lords are as follows:

- The [[Free Lord]]. Justice (VIII).
- The [[Horde Lord]]. Strength (XI).
- The [[Steel Lord]]. The Emperor (IV).
- The [[Lost Lord]]. The Hermit (IX).
