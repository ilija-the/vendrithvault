#deities #religion 
Four lords appointed by the [[One & Only]] to rule [[Vilhun]] in her stead. Three of the lords became the ancestor of virtually all recognised houses of Vilhun, while the fourth is said to have died heirless.

Some outsiders have identified the Hun Lords as titans. Locally, the Hun Lords are never meant to be considered equals to the One & Only, which causes problems when the titan label is applied to both her and the lords.

The Hun Lords are considered to be emissaries and viceroys of the [[One & Only]], but are also to be commune with the rest of the Titans.
