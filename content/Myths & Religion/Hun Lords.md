#deities #religion 
Four lords appointed by the [[One & Only]] to rule [[Vilhun]] in her stead. Three of the lords became the ancestor of virtually all recognised houses of Vilhun, while the fourth is said to have died heirless.

The Hun Lords are considered to be immortal emissaries and viceroys of the [[One & Only]], able to commune with her, and more broadly with the rest of the [[Titans]].[^1] Prayer to Hun Lords then, is often a means to communicate with the rest of the titans that are relevant to the subject of the prayer, with a belief that they are still in the material plane to this day, despite the unfolding of [[War Over Haeven]].

Some outsiders have identified the Hun Lords as titans. Locally, the Hun Lords are never meant to be considered equals to the One & Only, which causes problems when the titan label is applied to both her and the lords.
