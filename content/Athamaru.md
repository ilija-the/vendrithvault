Athamaru are a fish-like humanoid [[Ancestries|people]] whom can traverse sea as well as land for periods of time, though usually stick to the coastlines at most. Not much is known about their society or origins, as with most [[Aquatic]] history and civilisation - which has oft and long been feared. Many for example fear the Athamaru might serve the horrid [[Ocean King]], or that they're like cosmic agents of [[Abbadon]].
