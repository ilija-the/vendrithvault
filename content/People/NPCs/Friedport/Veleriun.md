Veleriun is a [[Goblins|Goblin]] member of the [[Quietgraves|Quietgrave]] [[Landcrabs|Landcrabs]] of [[Friedport]]. He is quite comfortable down in the caves and [[Portaupearl Crypts]], and has served as a guide and guard down there. Like the other quietgraves, he worships the [[Reedmother]], and is ready to use his slight abilities with alchemy and crossbows to defend it. He has an interest in tattoos, of which he has many prominently on his back. He makes sure to wear clothes revealing his tattoos,
