Odd is a [[Kobolds|Kobold]] almost inseparable from [[Chimney]], and the two's "owner," [[Vendy Vendetta Rapidly]]. Odd is aptly named for his blank stare and verbal disengagement from the world. They often perform odd jobs to set up infiltration opportunities and spy for Vendy.
