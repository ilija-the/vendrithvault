The world of Vendrith is inhabited by many people, creatures possessing intellect, a soul and freedom. 

>  In the folder structure of the vviki, the People folder contains Player Characters ( #people/pc), Non-Player Characters ( #people/npc), Companions ( #people/companion ) and Groups which denote a collection of people (though whole Ancestries should go in Society and Governments into Lands).
