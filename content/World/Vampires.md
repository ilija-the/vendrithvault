There are different kinds of vampires, all sharing being partially or fully undead and a propensity for blood-feeding and strange rites. A large part of them are also able to make more vampires.

[[Ferweth Vampyr]], an animalistic and horrifying creatures that lusts for blood and meat yet is normally incapable of entering places uninvited. Blind, these Vampyr often have to rely on [[Vampyr Eyes]] (also known as Vampyr Bats) to locate their prey, which have a sort of symbiotic relationship with the Vampyr.
