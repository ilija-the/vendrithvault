Materia is the material world, the world of Vendrith that surrounds one unless one travels to other [[Cosmology of Vendrith|Planes]]. In the [[Cosmology of Vendrith|cosmological]] [[Great Flow]] theory, the plane of [[Materia]] is believed to (roughly) lie at the centre of the flow, as it is characterised by a balance between matter and nothingness, pressure and free flow, light and darkness. It is in this balance that the conditions which allow most [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]], [[Fauna|animals]] and even [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]] to exist. Creatures that don't originate from Materia or otherwise are adapted to living in other planes are called [[Extraplanar Creatures]].
