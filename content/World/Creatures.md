Creature is a broad term that refers to all manner of living beings with a mind, ranging from sapient [[Ancestries of Vendrith|people]], to ferocious and dangerous [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]] that trouble them, and the various [[Animals|animals]] that skirt the line in-between. 

"Creatures" can also refer to more fundamental or powerful life-forms or forces of nature such as spirits and [[Deity|Deities]], if they take on a body of sorts.
