Haunts are stationary [[Spirits]] haunting an area, such as a battlefield, a mass execution site, a mass grave, or a desecrated temple. These often require assuaging the spirits or banishing them to truly get rid of them for good, reforming after a time period if physically dealt with instead. The nature of spirits is usually tied to [[Divine Magic]] and [[Occult Magic]].
