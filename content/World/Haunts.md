Haunts are stationary [[Spirits]] haunting an area, such as a battlefield, a mass execution site, a mass grave, or a desecrated temple. These often require assuaging the spirits or banishing them to truly get rid of them for good, reforming after a time period if physically dealt with instead. The nature of spirits and haunts is usually tied to, and can be handled by, [[Divine Magic]] and [[Occult Magic]].

Haunts sometimes co-inhabit [[Dungeons]] together with [[Traps]].
