Nature is the world surrounding is largely before being affected by man, which can be categorised into Flora, Fauna and Geology. [[Primal|Primal Magic]] is most certainly tied to nature, encompassed in how [[Druid|Druids]] are taught such magics in order to be its guardians. Fauna consists of various [[Creatures|creatures]], mostly those called [[Animals|animals]]. [[Monsters of Vendrith|Monsters]] aren't always easy to separate out, and as such may be considered part even if they're more commonly accepted as something in need of rooting out. [[Leshys|Leshys]] are known to serve as nature spirits tending to its running and well-being. 

Nature is also essential to [[Ancestries|humanoid]] life, producing its nutrition as part of [[Agriculture]] and foraging, providing the building materials used to create [[Society|civilisation]], and even acting as an important cornerstone of spiritual health.

### Flora
Flora refers to the variety of plants and fungus that grow across the world.

Some notable special types of flora-life include:
- [[Fairy Lanterns|Fairy Lanterns]]
- [[Pricklybursts|Pricklybursts]]
