Also known as Tjehen.

A type of [[Special Materials]] that is reminiscent of either ceramic when thin or sandstone brick when thick, and can comes in bright paintlike colours. It bears elements of sonic resonance. Often used to make religious symbols in [[Talvas]], as well as ritual cups for [[Orcs]] to consume [[Lotus Orcus]].
