Mantles are mysterious [[Divine]] aspects that deities, or at least Ascended Deities, take on, use, and possibly shape and/or are shaped by. Mantles are most commonly assumed to be a sort of congregation of domains, values and rules. Mantles are used to explain the continuity between various ascended [[Titans]].
