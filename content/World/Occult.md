The Occult is a field covering much knowledge that is either lost, taboo or forbidden - not included in the other [[Divine]], [[Arcane Magic|Arcane]] and [[Primal]] schools of thought. This includes the Occult type of [[Magic]], which is less understood and seemingly more arbitrary than that of other magic systems. The Occult famously makes up the +1 in the [[3+1 Theorem]], as an alien force outside of the sacred [[Trinities]] of the [[Cosmology|Cosmos]]. The Occult is often studied in cults and secret societies, away from the eyes of the [[Law]] which often forbids much of it, and the commoners who fear it so.
