The Occult is a field covering much knowledge that is either lost, taboo or forbidden - not included in the other [[Divine]], [[Arcane Magic|Arcane]] and [[Primal]] schools of thought. This includes the Occult type of [[Magic]], which is less understood and seemingly more arbitrary than that of other magic systems. The Occult famously makes up the +1 in the [[3+1 Theorem]], as an alien force outside of the sacred [[Trinities]] of the [[Cosmology|Cosmos]]. The Occult is often studied in cults and secret societies, away from the eyes of the [[Law]] which often forbids much of it, and the commoners who fear it so.

In the popular imagination, the Occult is merely a tool to cast [[Curses]] and control people. Those who understand the Occult more describe it as a magic that works through consciousness, and thus makes common use of mental elements such as stories and belief - in a sense a form of deep persuasion that can affect both [[Persons]] and the [[World]]. This is what makes [[Bard|Bardic Magic]], the most commonly accepted and beloved form of Occult Magic (though still sometimes feared), work like it does. [[Witch|Witches]] and [[Necromancer|Necromancers]] are especially feared practitioners of occult magic.

Occult magic can be considered related to Arcane magic and their arcane language in turn, though whereas arcane magic uses symbols with a specific (if unique) meaning, occult magic uses ghost characters whose complete meaning is (and perhaps must remain) unknown.
