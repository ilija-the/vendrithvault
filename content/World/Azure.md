One of the [[Cosmology]] speculated to be at the top of the [[Great Flow]] (a model of the [[Cosmology]]), opposite of [[Abbadon]]. It is described as light, bright, airy and of negative pressure. It is believed to be where all essence (life, energy, light, matter and sometimes even thought) is created.
