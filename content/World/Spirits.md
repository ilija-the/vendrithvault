Spirits are a general term for any —often incorporeal or metaphysical— being that is outside of the usual life cycle. 

There are many different kinds of spirits, plenty of them being related to different traditions of [[Magic]], such as [[Leshys]] for [[Primal Magic|Primal Magic]], [[Ghost|Ghosts]] for [[Occult Magic|Occult Magic]], and [[Angels]] for [[Divine Magic|Divine Magic]]
