# Overview
Awakened Animals are what were once [[Animals|animals]] whom have somehow permanently gained sapience and became as [[Ancestries|people]]. This is in contrast to [[Spirits]] who might take animal-like forms or temporarily inhabit animal bodies. Awakened Animals can be created by [[Druid|Druids]], [[Wizard|Wizards]] or other spellcasters, but remain rather rare. They are much more common as characters in [[Performance|stories]].
