A revered [[Divinity]] of [[Vilhun]] considered the founder of its culture, religion and state. She eventually assigned four [[Hun Lords]] to rule in her stead, of which three became the ancestors of essentially all recognised houses of Vilhun.

The creation myth of Vilhun says of her chiseling the main isles out of the tallest mountains that [[Materia]] had to offer, with the remaining smaller islands being the debris of her carvings.
