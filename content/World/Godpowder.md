> [!warning]
>  Not to be confused with the similar [[Redpowder]]. There is no real life equivalent of blackpowder or gunpowder in Vendrith.

A highly potent, [[Divine|divine]] golden powder enchanted with explosive properties when in the hands of a religious person. The rituals of its creation were taught in the [[New Creed]], alongside the instructions to grow the [[Firearms]] the powder is usually used for.
