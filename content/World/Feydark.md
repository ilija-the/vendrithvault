One of the [[Cosmology of Vendrith]]. The Feydark is described as a fairytale-esque dimension in which many of the stories of old and myth appear as reality. Usually however, they take on a nightmarish or gruesome form, or at the very least an unnerving one. Some have speculated [[Gnomes]] originate from the Feydark.

![A creature](https://cdn.bsky.app/img/feed_fullsize/plain/did:plc:5pah37pg5c2qeji74yg2omn6/bafkreiajwgw4zejdbk7d7oaor4fujvrluwqqwfelnrsy5c7ysrvs2wceii@jpeg)

Some have compared the plane of [[Deep Gray]] to the Feydark for its dreamlike feeling. In this comparison, the Feydark is usually described as a nightmare as opposed to a mere dream.
