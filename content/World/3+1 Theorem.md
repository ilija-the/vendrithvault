A theory relating to the [[Cosmology]]. Closely connected with the four traditions of magic. Simultaneously embraces but also challenges the tradition of the [[Trinities]].

Goes something like this: 
First world, a dot - something surrounded by nothing - there is only something - something can't exist without nothing - unstable, collapse.

Second world, an empty sphere - Nothing surrounded by something - the nothing is implied by the something around - can exist, stable.

Third world, sphere with a dot at its centre - The first world, previously unable to exist, reappears in the framework of the second world - perfect stability is disturbed, pieces of the something around nothing are shattered and sent loose around the sphere now orbiting around the something in the centre.

+1 world - creation of something may imply anything outside of it. The cracks created in the sphere allows anything outside of our framework to slip in.
