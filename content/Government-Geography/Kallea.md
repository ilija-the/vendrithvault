Kallea is a large and central city in [[Canath]], straddling the [[Celestaria|Celestarian]] coast while also connecting to the neighbouring [[Vön]] and [[Galae]]. It is often considered the capital of [[Canath]], and its home to the largest [[Crusader Armies|Crusader Host]] which is camped around its periphery. Kallea has historically lead the Kallean Coalition, an empire of city states and islands under its dominance.
