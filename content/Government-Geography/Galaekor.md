A region of [[Galae]] which was ruled from [[Nagonkor]]. Even today its people retain a kinship southwards to the Nagonkori. The region is nested away in a jungled valley which is hard to reach, only realistically accessible from the [[Regal Path]].

Galaekor became a target of the [[Crusaders|Crusaders]] during the [[March of the Corpse Flowers]], which resulted in a particularly long and brutal jungle siege.
