Adamsworth is a major settlement in [[Horiza|Horiza]], which has recently alongside [[Newshore]] bypassed the importance of [[Friedport]] in many ways. Adamsworth is governed by the [[Horizan Guild Association]] (like all settlements of note in Horiza are), and is home to much of their leadership in Horiza. The settlement is characterised by its series of walls surrounding an enormous statue of a man at its centre, left by [[Horizan Precursors]].

Adamsworth has somewhat of a rivalry with [[Newshore]], which outproduces it in food and culture.
