Eleina is a barren rock of an island in the [[Blissfeldt Coast|Blissfeldt]] sea, straddling the spheres of [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]], [[Mogxa]] and [[Vilhun]]. It has for most of its existence been part of the Galaen culture and political sphere, but it has also served as a Mogxan and Vilhi imperial province at separate points in time. The island has a history of Pearl-[[Diving]], and a folklore which makes oft mention of [[Merfolk]] and other [[Aquatic]] creatures. 

Today it has 'reverted' back to being Galaen, though in reality it most importantly serves as a base for northern [[Crusaders|Crusader]] [[Piracy|Pirates]]. Some suspect [[Home Base]] has at least at one point been been located on Eleina, guiding crusader pirate attacks across the north, supplying the [[Enui]] campaign, and staying in control of the [[Crusader Sects]] in [[Ferweth]].
