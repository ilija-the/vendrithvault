A shortcut through the mountains between [[Canath]] and [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]], normally only used by the [[Silver Couriers]] of the [[Town of Ire]]. Encompasses the [[Razor Route]], [[Thousand Tombs]], and [[Freitz Valley]].
