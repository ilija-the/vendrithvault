The Rubyfirth is an internal sea bordering all of [[Mogxa]], [[Galae]], [[Nagonkor]], [[Canath]], [[Eydwin]], [[Talvas]] and [[Veducia]]. A series of city states, multicultural communities and elite merchants exist in-between these worlds, mainly with their roots in Talvas, whom dominate seafaring and trade. They were once rivals of the [[Zardoban Crown|Zardoban Empire]], but have since its defeat triumphed as the world's utmost experts of shipbuilding
