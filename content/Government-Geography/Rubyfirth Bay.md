The Rubyfirth is an internal sea bordering all of [[Mogxa]], [[Galae]], [[Nagonkor]], [[Canath]], [[Eydwin]], [[Talvas]] and [[Veducia]]. 

Rubyfirth can also be used to refer to a cultural-social set of groups. A series of city states, multicultural communities and elite merchants exist in-between these worlds, mainly with their roots in Talvas, whom dominate seafaring and trade. They were once rivals of the [[Zardoban Crown|Zardoban Empire]], but have since its defeat triumphed as the world's utmost experts of shipbuilding and commerce. The Rubyfirth merchants were instrumental in founding the [[Horizan Guild Association]], which has its official headquarters on the island of [[Jedaia]].

The [[Rend Peoples]] are a series of [[Ancestries of Vendrith|peoples]] whom have traversed across and lived along the Rubyfirth bay and more. They can be considered precursors of the modern Rubyfirth culture, and despite their growing apart, still constitute a series of linked cultures across the shores of this sea.
