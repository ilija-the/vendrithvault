The Regal Path is a passage between [[Nagonkor]] and [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]] which once constituted the link between Nagonkor and its sister realm of "Galaekor." Since the decline of Galaekor the path saw little use, with its jungle and cliffside paths, as well as safe dwelling caves, abandoned. Interest in the path resurrected after the beginning of the [[Eternal Crusade]], with the [[Crusaders|Crusaders]] hoping to hold control of Nagonkor and reopen the path to use for its armies. These efforts have gone slowly, so far.
