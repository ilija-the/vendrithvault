The Path of Gold is the main road connecting central [[Canath|Encanath]] and [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]], and directly leads to the former [[Hightower Civilisation|Hightower]] city of [[Babael]]. As this Galaen part finds itself on a plateau, the Path of Gold is a rocky mountain road that requires many thousands of steps to ascend.

Alternatives to the Path of Gold are the northern route, the [[Bronze Canal]], the [[Silver Pass]], and the [[Regal Path]].
