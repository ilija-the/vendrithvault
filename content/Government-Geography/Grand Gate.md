The Grand Gate is a fortress-settlement on the by the [[Sanitorium]] in order to keep the [[Crusaders|Crusaders]] from being able to march into their lands, cutting off one of the two land routes between [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]] and [[Mogxa]].

The other possible land route between Mogxa and Galae is north of [[The Scar]]. However, it is especially unstable, and leads into the difficult terrain of [[Cragmoore|Cragrock]] and its Sanitorium ally [[Cragmoore|Cragmoor]]. Consequently, the Crusaders have not seriously attempted to pass it, and instead hold out hope to get through the Grand Gate one day.
