Sergetis is the name of a peninsula and its coastline and immediately adjacent islands, forming a Crescent-like shape at the southern tip of [[Mogxa]]. Sergetis is the traditional homelands of [[Dragons]], and was home to the [[Draconic Empire]] - alternatively called the Sergetine Empire. The peninsula is today home to most of the [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa|Draconic Tribes]] still remaining. 

The landmass used to be larger, but about 1/3rd of it sank into the sea during the [[Sinking of Sergetis]], sometimes hailed as the largest [[Disasters of the Silence|Disaster of the Silence]]. The sinking saw many casualties and even more people leaving the peninsula altogether. A number of migrants left for [[Lorgun]], which is already populated by a community with roots in Sergetis.

The Sergetine coastal waters, also sometimes called the "Mouth of Mogxa," are turbulent and shark-infested. It is home to various submerged ruins since the sinking, which some brave [[Adventurers|adventurers]] and archaeologists have attempted to dive.

While the Dragons were infamous abroad, the peninsula itself was a peaceful and flourishing centre of culture, seeing many visitors from abroad seeking to experience and learn from its traditions. One particularly proud piece of Sergetine culture was its theatre - often outshining the at the time decaying city of [[Philosophia]]'s.
