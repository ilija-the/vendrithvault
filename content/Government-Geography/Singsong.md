A coastal city in eastern [[Mogxa]], as ran by the [[Sanitorium]]. It has gained increasing importance as it has been built up as the ideal port hub for connecting to [[Horiza]], which the Sanitorium used as way to gain leverage over the [[Horizan Guild Association]]. It eventually became the origin of the [[Horiza#Third Wave and Politicisation|Third Wave]] of settlement there, which saw a mass of people emigrating or even sneaking themselves to Singsong in order to try to get on those ships. A number of people who were rejected entry to the ships have since stayed around in Singsong, making up a destitute homeless class often used for harbour labour.

Surrounding Singsong are a number of [[Singsong Manors|Manors]] owned by aristocratic families. Inside the city, the [[Singsong Medical Academy]] is a standout.

Singsong was once decimated by the [[Singsong Harbour Fire]].
