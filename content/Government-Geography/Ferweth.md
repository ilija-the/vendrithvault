Ferweth is a large but sparsely populated region to the north of [[Canath]] and [[Galae]], mostly consisting of various unfriendly terrains and climates like mountains, thick forests and barely traversable marshes. It is famously a gloomy region that has since time immemorial been haunted by various [[Monsters of Vendrith|monsters]] and myths.

## Culture
Ferwethi culture is enveloped in its status as the "region ruled by monsters and myth."

## Geography
The largest and safest settlements of Ferweth all find themselves on its northern edge. The capital, [[Longdawn]], sits safely in a corner far away from any potential rivals and as such as long served as the sovereign of the Ferwethi polities.

## History
Due to Ferweth's terrain, the location of its most valuable and capital area safely far away, and it's lacking interest or capability to create an empire stretching much further, Ferweth has largely stayed uninvolved with the other regions. While there have been conflicts with outsiders on a few occasions, these have been more akin to army marches than traditional warfare.
