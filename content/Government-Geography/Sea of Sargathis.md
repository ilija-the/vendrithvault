Sargathis (meaning Sargathian Sea) is the famously dangerous and often shark-infested waters around the current island chain and peninsula of [[Sargathia]], the southernmost peninsula and island-chain of [[Mogxa]]. The waters of Sargathis have also been called the "Mouth of Mogxa" at times. 

Sargathis is now also home to the [[Sinking of Sergetis|sunken]] parts of former [[Sea of Sargathis]], sometimes called Argathis in its sunken form. Some brave [[Adventurers|adventurers]] and archaeologists have attempted to dive into Sargathis to find the Argathis ruins and its many lost artefacts and history.

Argathia used to be a peninsula on the southernmost tip of [[Mogxa]], and the traditional homelands and power base of many [[Dragons]] and Draconic Tribes. At times, the [[Draconic Empire]] held their courts in Argathia. While the Draconic Empire was rather infamous, Argathia itself was a flourishing centre of culture and saw many visitors from abroad seeking to experience and learn from its traditions. One particularly proud piece of Argathian culture was its theatre - often outshining the at the time decaying city of [[Philosophia]]'s.

The Argathia of old is no longer, however, since the [[Sinking of Sergetis]].

These lands contained much history as the historical homelands of the [[Dragons]] of the [[Draconic Empire]], still home to many of Mogxa's Draconic Tribes at the time. Many of the survivors vacated to other parts of Mogxa or beyond in fear, though the sinking did leave behind some landmass now forming the island chain of [[Sargathia]]. 

Much of Argathian and Draconic Imperial history now lies at the sea floor of famously dangerous and sometimes shark-infected waters. One tradition which was essentially destroyed was Argathian Theatre, as essentially all of its theatres and [[Sergetine Statues]] were taken by the sinking.
