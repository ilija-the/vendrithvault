Vanderkact is a settlement usually found in the northern [[Crimson Desert|Red Desert]], between [[Ferweth]] and [[Galae]]. It is notable for consisting of small mobile homes on wheels that allows the settlement to move around, like a nomadic herd. These mobile homes were invented and maintained by a lineage of amurrun descended from [[Saint Clover]], the settlement's patron saint, ending with [[Father Emeri]].

Vanderkact, collecting and making use of Cacti, have trade ties to [[Cocatun]]. It has also helped move shipments of Cocoa from there to the world beyond - such as when the [[Zardoban Crown|Zardoban Emperor]] was addicted to the stuff.

The myth of [[The Blare]] was central to Vanderkact.

Citizens include:
- Father Emeri
- Mathilda, barkeep of the Vanderkact Bartlebees
