Eydwin is a geographical term with a vague meaning and that only exists as an exonym (nobody calls their own lands Eydwinese), but which roughly starts at the south of [[Canath]]

A name for the lands that begin south of the [[Iduria]], as split up by the [[Rubyfirth Bay]] and a vague land border south of [[Canath]].

Sometimes just used to describe the lands south of Encanath but west of Talvas. This direction is once where a great number of people of Canath came from, but the descendents of these peoples are said to have been pushed to Brincanath.
