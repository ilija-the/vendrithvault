Eydwin is a geographical term with a vague meaning and that only exists as an exonym (nobody calls their own lands Eydwinese), but which roughly starts at the south end of [[Canath]] and encompasses all the lands that follow. Depending on context, Eydwin is either included in the newer concept of [[Iduria]] - really just meaning the old world (which is to say not [[Horiza]]) - or not. Culturally, linguistically, religiously and historically, Eydwin has remained somewhat of an unknown factor to those to its north, despite the continued presence of trade and migration.

Rarely the term excludes [[Talvas]] and the [[Veducia|Veducian]] lands east of it, really just referring to the tribes and nomads living on the western end the lands south of Canath. These people do have a shared lineage, and 

Sometimes just used to describe the lands south of Encanath but west of Talvas. This direction is once where a great number of people of Canath came from, but the descendents of these peoples are said to have been pushed to Brincanath.
