Riverside is town in eastern [[Canath]] that was founded after the abandonment of the [[Vestitur Castle]] built some miles away, a place famous for being the home of the [[Vestitur Relics]]. Straddling the edge between central Encanath and the sparsely populated wintery area of [[Vön]], it serves as the capital of Vön and hosts its king.
