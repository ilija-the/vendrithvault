The Blissfeldt Coast is a highly calm coastline that encompasses the northern shores of [[VVIKI/Government-Geography/Galae]]. Striking a temperate climate, the coast has been described as having a serenity to it unmatched by any other place in Galae. Still, the lands to its south being unfarmable and it containing many shallow waters that make fishing difficult, the Blissfeldt Coast has never hosted any particularly large settlements.

The largest settlement on Blissfeldt is [[Littlehorne|Littlehorn]].
