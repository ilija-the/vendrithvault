#places 
A sparsely settled region in [[Eydwin]], east of [[Talvas]] and south of [[Mogxa]]. Said to have been the origin point of the peoples of [[Nagonkor]].

Like Nagonkor, the region is often considered wild, or even but a hunting ground. There are settlements in these lands still. Some are highly walled off secret cities that engage only in trade with outside regions, and others are villages made by creatures not considered fully civilized.
