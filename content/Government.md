### Overview
Most societies in the world organised under a form of government, though it varies in form and content greatly. Examples of government range from tribal structures and nomadic herds, to independent city states, feudal monarchies, autocratic monarchies, guild states and colonies, empires of conquest, and bureaucratic empires.

### Tribal Rule
The roots of man lie in tribal and nomadic rule, which can still be found in many areas and societies of the world.
