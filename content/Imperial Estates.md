The Imperial Estates are a large mostly wild land that was exclusively tied to the [[Imperial City]], whose citizens once enjoyed exclusive rights of access to and hunting in it. This has been loosening up however, much to their dismay. The [[Draconic Tribes of Mogxa|Draconic Tribes]] also have a certain claim to parts of it, especially the [[Red Gown Tribes]] towards the southern coast.

The Imperial Estates can also be accessed from the area of [[Kathayle]] as well as [[Wedu]].
