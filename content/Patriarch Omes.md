Omes is a [[Landcrabs|Landcrab Patriarch]] of [[Crabraven]], [[Friedport]]. A rather large and strong fellow, Omes is generally sociable and enjoys perusing and partaking in the variety of social activities and games usually hosted around the carnival streets. He was rather passive and neutral in the landcrab conflicts, but is broadly known as a kind-hearted person (he has been known allow newcomers to purchase property and even on occasion to forgive certain debts).
