An ongoing crisis in which the material of diamonds, used in many rituals (especially those which concern extensions of lifespan or resurrection) has become extremely scarce. Primarily centred on [[Mogxa]], which has historically used such rituals to a high degree.

[[Philosophia]]n politics have long been dominated by figures with abnormally long or extended lifespans, and have fought the hardest and paid the most to secure a steady influx of diamonds still - exacerbating the issue in the rest of Mogxa.
