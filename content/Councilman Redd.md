Redd is a councilman of the [[Horizan Guild Association]] and [[Crafting|crafter]] in [[Friedport]]. As a veteran of [[Mogxa|Mogxan]] wars, he has particular experience working with the explosive [[Redpowder]] (often used in fireworks or as a replacement for [[Godpowder]]), and with commanding sailing ships.
