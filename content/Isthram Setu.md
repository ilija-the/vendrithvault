Isthram Setu is a settlement on the western edge of the [[Celene]] of [[Canath]], which famously guards the [[Celtwix|Celtwix Strait]] which spans from it to the shore of [[Kiltox]]. It sits on a peninsula-island, whose land bridge is sometimes submerged by the sea and sometimes not.

The settlement is not too long of a sail from [[Amoas]].
