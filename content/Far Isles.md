Far Isles are mysterious islands which sailors and explorers occasionally claim to have discovered or been shipwrecked on, far off known coasts, and, often, quite close to the [[World Fleece]]. They are unique not only in terms of being rare to find and even more rare to be rediscovered twice, but also in expressing select traits of other planes - such as rains of water elementals, walls of lava, or spaces in which wind and gravity misbehave. 

Some Far Isles are undoubtedly more fiction that truth, forming a core part of the lore of the sea. The mythical Island of Neboule and its forests of glass is perhaps the most well known story of a Far Isle.

The island of [[Mewji]] has been identified by some scholars as a Far Isle. Like with many other things, the Vilhi generally view the classification of such central pillars of their lore and identity into broad terminology and systems as arrogant.
