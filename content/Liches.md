Liches are a type of [[Undead]] [[Necromancer]] both famous and infamous for being the most successful at maintaining their intelligence throughout their long undead existence - at the cost of the lifespan and souls of other [[Ancestries|people]]. The rituals to become a lich remain an exclusive secret. Though the methods are thought to vary, their commonality is that all liches end up wearing the skull of a dead [[Animals|animal]] or [[Creatures|creature]], which ends up fused to their bodies, partially by the help of the [[Nature|plant-life]] called Lichen that grows across them.

For a period, Liches were accepted as a presence in the countryside of [[Mogxa]]
