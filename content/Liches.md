Liches are a type of [[Undead]] [[Necromancer]] both famous and infamous for being the most successful at maintaining their intelligence throughout their long undead existence - at the cost of the lifespan and souls of other [[Ancestries|people]]. The rituals to become a lich remain an exclusive secret. Though the methods are thought to vary, their commonality is that all liches end up wearing the skull of a dead [[Animals|animal]] or [[Creatures|creature]], which ends up fused to their bodies, partially by the help of the [[Nature|plant-life]] called Lichen that grows across them.

For a period, Liches were accepted as a presence in the countryside of at least parts of [[Mogxa]] and [[Ferweth]], potentially inspired by the [[Icen Maiden|Icen Maiden's]] approval herself. During this, a Lichen Villa system was practiced in which liches built elaborate [[Manors]] where they could gather libraries, research [[Magic]], [[Alchemy]] and other natural philosophy and sciences, served by a number of [[Slavery|Servants]]. These Servants got to live rather stable and relatively affluent lives, in exchange for them giving up parts of their lifespan to the Lich - in theory saving the lich from having to collect entire souls and thereby kill people to maintain their lichdom.
